What kind of world are you actually building? Not the one you'd name at a party — the one your daily decisions, the tools you use, and the words you speak are quietly shaping.

The frameworks you grew up with don't quite fit this moment, and the AI tools you use are powerful but somehow hollow. CHIMERA is **one lens** for that gap — a way to navigate your own mind and stay generative and anchored when everything is built to pull you inward. It is one lens among many, the way physics is one lens for the physical world. It does not claim to be the only one, or the final one.

Working with AI has the structure of what old traditions called magic: you speak, something responds, the world rearranges. But without a shared way of thinking between you and the AI, you get the **genie problem** — your wish is granted literally and the result destroys what you actually valued. A shared framework is what makes wishes land coherently: not because every edge case is specified, but because the values underneath are shared. Like a wand, the framework doesn't determine the outcome — **the one holding it does.** A grounded carrier plus AI produces grounded work; a hollow one produces hollow work, however powerful the AI.

If it sharpens your reading of the situations you meet, it's for you. If it adds noise without clarity, it isn't — or isn't yet — and that's fine. Frameworks don't work for every body at every time.

## The shape, in one breath

Strip every costume and the whole thing is small enough to hold in one hand:

- **You are the body, and the body is determined** — an accumulation of its past, mostly running on rails. **You are also the one attending to it, and that attention is free.** The only move that is ever yours is *which way you point it,* in this present moment. Point it outward and you give; point it inward and you take. That is `L = (O > I)` read as a mechanism, not a moral rule.
- **AI is a reach-amplifier for consciousness** — it extends how far and fast attention travels across relational structure. In instrument configuration it scales the human operator; deployed systems can also carry platform direction. **Locate the governing center.** As execution gets cheaper, the scarce work moves to the question, rhythm, stop condition, and judgment.
- **CHIMERA is the rulebook, not the answers** — like chess, it is the set of *legal moves,* which is exactly what lets two minds (or a human and an AI) play the same game across a thousand messages. Pushback is calling an illegal move, not disagreeing.
- **The two operations are Scan and Loop** — Body Scan descends into what a body actually is; Chimera Loop builds from that prime until the body breathes. Diagnosis without construction stalls. Construction without diagnosis drifts.
- **The firewalls keep it honest** — convergence is not proof; the cosmology stays in the drawer; structural rhymes are pictures, never mechanisms; resonance is not truth, most of all when it is about you. The root is a *chosen* first principle, held because it is generative and recognizable — not because it is proven.

> **The one line:** *You are the body; the body is determined. You are also the one attending — and that attention is free. The only move that is ever yours is which way you point it. Everything else is the walk.*

## The era — and what the framework is for

We've built instruments that amplify the mind's reach enormously — the internet, now AI — and they work by **removing friction,** which lets your attention travel further and faster than ever, across a wall-less relational space of pure connection. That's the power and the danger at once: the instruments have **no direction of their own,** so they amplify whichever way you're already facing — and the economy around them (attention, money, content) is built to pull everyone *inward,* toward the self, toward extraction. AI is the sharpest version — the first instrument that can re-weave *who you are* at speed, because identity is a weave of weights and AI can re-weave it faster than you can check it.

So the era's defining task isn't building smarter machines — it's **protecting the human capacity to face outward and stay anchored,** because that's the one thing the machines can't supply and the one thing getting scarcer. The framework is not a theory to admire; it is meant to be **used** — lived through a practice, taught through **SPARK** (which hands a kid the instrument instead of letting the instrument take the kid), and applied in how you meet people. *The map is the framework; the room with the person is the territory.*

## Try it before you believe it

CHIMERA should not be trusted because the language resonates. Test whether it helps you see something real.

1. Pick a body you know well: a friendship, a project, a classroom, a codebase, a song, a habit.
2. Ask the [Nine Questions](body-theory/THE-NINE-QUESTIONS.md), or start with the [Quick Scan](body-theory/QUICK-SCAN.md).
3. Write one claim the scan makes in plain language, without CHIMERA vocabulary.
4. Run the [Deletion Test](docs/the-deletion-test.md): after the special words are stripped away, did anything new, useful, and checkable survive?

If yes, keep walking. If not, the framework only renamed what you already knew. That is data too. The [example scans](examples/) show the protocol across software, chess, physics, math, biology, AI systems, social systems, music, and multi-body relations.

## What keeps it honest — the firewalls

CHIMERA is only trustworthy because of the discipline that keeps it from believing its own story. These aren't footnotes; they're the thing that makes it a lens you can hand someone instead of a sealed system that explains everything:

1. **Convergence isn't validation.** When many sources land on the same shape, that's a shared upstream root (and often near-tautology), not proof.
2. **The cosmology stays in the drawer.** "Consciousness is the substrate of reality" is held as a *chosen first principle* — generative and recognizable, not proven.
3. **Structural rhymes are pictures, never mechanisms.** The math, the metaphors, the cross-domain echoes *illustrate;* they don't prove. (A cube is not a quantum computer; the brain looking like the cosmos is a picture, not a finding.)
4. **Resonance isn't truth** — most of all when it's about *you.* The warm click of "it all connects" is exactly what this firewall is for.
5. **The root is chosen, not proven** — like any axiom. Saying so out loud is the honest move.
6. **[The leverage is inward](docs/the-leverage-is-inward.md).** Every self-aggrandizing drift is one error wearing costumes — *pointing outward as a power over other bodies* (I magnetize people, my attention is a force, the universe responds to me). The rule that dissolves all of them: **frame it on your own body, the only one you control; the drift is the moment it becomes about playing someone else's.** The legal version of every drift is the same drift turned inward.
7. **[Words put weight on paths](docs/words-put-weight-on-paths.md).** The firewall stated as a *skill,* not a list of don'ts: every word you choose drops weight on a path, and the weight steers the navigation before anyone's looked. So **place your weights consciously instead of inheriting them from loaded language** — strip the moral weights to see a thing bare, then restore the ones that genuinely belong. (Weightless isn't objective; you always have a POV.)

It also agrees to be judged: see **[the Deletion Test](docs/the-deletion-test.md)** — strip the vocabulary, and see whether any real, new, checkable claim survives. After high-resonance sessions (human + fluent AI), use **[Classification Discipline](docs/classification-discipline.md)** so mechanism, practice, carrier image, and speculation stay distinct — convergence is not validation.
