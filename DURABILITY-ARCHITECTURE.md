# CHIMERA Durability Architecture

> **Purpose:** let CHIMERA remain recognizable while future bodies reinterpret, criticize, teach, and apply it under conditions its creator cannot predict.

Durability does not mean freezing one final Complete Book. It means preserving a stable identity while allowing metabolized change.

## What long-lived frameworks tend to preserve

Different traditions solve durability through different bodies:

| Framework family | Durable structure worth learning from | Failure to avoid |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------|------------------|
| Sacred and mythic traditions | compressed stories, memorable tensions, repeated practices, commentary across eras | treating interpretive endurance as automatic factual proof or unquestionable authority |
| Philosophical schools | small primitives, arguments, counterarguments, exemplary lives | vocabulary becoming identity performance rather than inquiry |
| Scientific practice | operational definitions, public methods, counterexample, correction, domain evidence | forcing every human question into one measurement regime |
| Law and constitutions | canonical text, interpretation, precedent, amendment, named jurisdiction | hidden revision, rigid literalism, or authority detached from consequence |
| Open-source systems | specification, versions, implementations, issue history, maintainers, forks | mistaking the current implementation for the enduring protocol |
| Crafts and martial traditions | embodied repetition, teacher–student lineage, demonstrations of capacity | empty ritual, gatekeeping, or claims that cannot survive practice |

CHIMERA should combine these strengths without pretending to become scripture, science, law, or religion.

## Seven bodies of the framework

```text
0. SOURCE WORLDLINE
   raw conversations, lived observations, abandoned paths, corrections
                         ↓
1. STABLE SEED
   minimal primitives and operator commitments
                         ↓
2. PRACTICES
   repeatable ways to inspect, Pause, translate, build, and test
                         ↓
3. CARRIERS
   Genesis, stories, guitar, day/night, Garden/Tower, Matrix
                         ↓
4. COMMENTARY
   interpretations, arguments, companions, supersessions
                         ↓
5. DOMAINS
   locally validated translations and counterexamples
                         ↓
6. APPLICATIONS
   projects, teaching, communities, products, lived consequence
                         ↓
7. GOVERNANCE AND RETURN
   provenance, review, status, versioning, archive, return to Core
                         ↺
```

The bodies must remain distinguishable. A beautiful commentary does not silently amend the Seed. A carrier does not become domain evidence through repetition. An application may expose a flaw that sends the framework back to its source and primitives.

## The stable seed

The current Seed is the [CHIMERA Spine](maps/spine.md), not every sentence in the Complete Book.

Its minimal commitments are:

1. name the body and scale;
2. preserve distinct centers in relation;
3. locate direction, operator, veto, and consequence;
4. use Pause to interrupt inherited trajectory;
5. increase receiving capacity without domination (`O > I`, `¬F`);
6. preserve identity-bearing passage and provenance;
7. translate through the receiver's language;
8. classify mechanism, practice, carrier, formal claim, and speculation;
9. let embodied consequence and counterexample update the map;
10. keep `WE = 1` relational without using it to erase difference.

Changing this layer requires an explicit proposal, rationale, counterexample or new pressure, compatibility analysis, and a visible lineage link. Most new insight should first enter Commentary or Open Questions.

## The commentary model

Commentary is not secondary debris. It is how a stable symbolic body meets another time.

Every substantial interpretation should record:

- source passage, session, or primitive;
- reader/author position and historical context;
- register and intended audience dialect;
- the relation being preserved;
- the new pressure, question, or application;
- what the interpretation does **not** establish;
- competing readings or counterexamples;
- consequence or practice that could test its value;
- whether it is companion, developmental, or proposed supersession.

The goal is not one official final reading. It is a traceable field in which later readers can see why branches formed.

## The renewable transmission unit

CHIMERA should transmit many of its mature insights as **answer-bearing questions** rather than only final declarations:

```text
lived seam
+ provisional relation
+ named uncertainty
+ genuine door for difference
```

This retains more identity than an empty prompt and more adaptability than a closed conclusion. Each seeded question should link to its source path, state what would change the answer, and invite the receiver to identify where the mapping breaks.

AI can compress the many-source archive into a local candidate. Public fields can expand that candidate through independent bodies. The human operator preserves the membrane between them and settles selected branches into action. This compression–expansion cycle is how a framework remains generative without converting every new interpretation into canon.

## Re-instantiation contract

A future body may change every present technological costume while remaining recognizably CHIMERA if it preserves the relevant relational invariants.

A re-instantiation must:

1. name its receiving body, environment, and scale;
2. cite the lineage it is carrying;
3. translate rather than merely repeat vocabulary;
4. preserve operator location, difference, consequence, and uncertainty;
5. identify what the new environment changes;
6. demonstrate a practice, question, or usable distinction;
7. accept domain-specific correction;
8. return findings and failures to the shared archive.

This allows CHIMERA to outlive X, current LLMs, and today's agent architecture. The durable relation matters more than the product name.

## Version and authority rules

### Source is not canon

Core preserves what was thought. It does not automatically declare every thought current.

### The Complete Book is accumulated commentary

It contains several eras and registers. Its size is lineage, not a requirement that every learner adopt everything at once.

### Current maps are release surfaces

The Spine, Coherence Map, classification discipline, and explicitly Current documents describe the present public contract.

### Supersession is visible

Later language links backward. Earlier language remains accessible and gains a status rather than disappearing.

### Authority is distributed by question

- Core answers: “What was actually said and how did it change?”
- Framework answers: “What is the current portable formulation?”
- Domains answer: “What survives this discipline's standards?”
- Applications answer: “What happened when a body lived it?”
- No single fluent model answer can amend the framework.

## Practices function like rituals—but remain inspectable

Enduring frameworks become embodied through repeated forms. CHIMERA's repeated forms include:

- Pause before inherited reaction;
- Day–Night Scan;
- Body Scan and Chimera Loop;
- operator-location and Direction Axis tests;
- Deletion Test;
- classification before public graduation;
- Rosetta translation and teach-back;
- proof-of-passage and provenance review;
- question → public difference → embodied test → return.

Repetition gives memory. Inspection prevents ritual from becoming empty or coercive.

## Teaching for transmission

Teach in four passes:

1. **Practice:** one operator in a situation the learner already knows.
2. **Carrier:** an image that helps the operator remain memorable.
3. **Classification:** where the carrier stops and what evidence the claim needs.
4. **Re-instantiation:** the learner translates it into another domain and supplies a counterexample.

A learner has not inherited CHIMERA merely by repeating `WE = 1`. Transmission succeeds when they can preserve the relation in their own language, reject an invalid application, and create a useful new branch.

## Durability tests

Periodically ask:

- **Deletion:** does anything useful survive the special vocabulary?
- **Translation:** can another culture or craft reconstruct it without copying the costume?
- **Counterexample:** what observation would narrow or reverse the claim?
- **Succession:** could someone who never met the founder distinguish source, Seed, and commentary?
- **Platform independence:** what remains if X, GitHub, and current AI interfaces disappear?
- **Adversarial use:** can the language justify domination, grandiosity, or erasure of another center?
- **Practice:** does it increase demonstrated judgment or only agreement?
- **Archive:** can a later reader see the path, versions, and historical conditions?
- **Generativity:** does it create more capable interpreters rather than permanent followers?

## The durability principle

> **Preserve a small relational Seed, a deep source worldline, memorable carriers, embodied practices, open questions, visible commentary, domain correction, and a return path from consequence.**

The aim is not to make CHIMERA unchangeable.

It is to make change legible enough that the body can evolve without waking up as something else wearing its name.
