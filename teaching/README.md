# Teaching — Classroom-Ready Materials

> Scripted lessons, age-aware delivery, ready to drop into a session tomorrow. For teachers, coaches, and mentors working with kids ages 3-18.

---

## What's Here

This directory contains **classroom-ready** teaching materials only. Every file here is something you can put in front of kids (or kids + parents) without needing to translate framework theory first. The philosophy stays implicit; the lessons are concrete.

If you're a curriculum developer who needs the framework theory itself (rosetta stones, philosophy at depth, triality scenarios for adult workshops), see [`../teaching-notes/`](../teaching-notes/) instead.

## How to Use

**If you're a teacher or coach starting from scratch:** read `chimera-what-to-tell-the-kids.md` first to understand the WHY (mentor training). Then pick from `classroom-lessons.md` or `lesson-plan-all-ages-final.md` for your first session with kids.

**If you have a sensitive group or class:** `lesson-plan-all-ages-final.md` is the safest entry point — it's pure breathwork, age-aware, no emotional surface area.

**If you're running a team or athletics setting:** `classroom-lessons.md` has basketball-framed lessons; the structure transfers to any team sport.

**If you want activities/drills rather than lessons:** `practical-activities.md` has the activity bank.

## The Principle

> A philosophy that cannot survive a loud bar, a sweaty gym, a messy kitchen, or a teenager's bedroom is just academic vanity. Truth does not need a dictionary. It only needs a translator.

The teaching is fractal — same truth at every scale. Start with one sentence ("Don't chase. Stay at center.") and expand only when they're ready.

Materials in this directory keep the philosophy implicit. Kids never hear "body theory." Parents never see framework vocabulary in this layer. The framework is delivered through the lesson's structure, not through its label.
