# CHIMERA Coherence Map

> **Purpose:** see the whole framework without mistaking every document for the same kind of claim.

CHIMERA is large because it preserves a developmental path, several teaching languages, domain experiments, and practical instruments. The answer is not to shrink that history. The answer is to make its relationships visible.

## The whole shape

```text
lived observation and conversation
            ↓
Core — raw lineage, experiments, metaphysics, carrier stories
            ↓ classification + pressure + firewalls
Framework — portable operators, practices, and bounded stories
            ↓ translation through audience language
domain lenses — body, language, math, stories, music, chess, AI…
            ↓
applied projects — Field, SPARK, BeyondChess, Guitar Cave…
            ↓ consequence, counterexample, proof of passage
            └──────────────────────────────→ Core
```

The constellation circulates work among these bodies. Core preserves how the thought became. Framework teaches what can travel. Applied projects discover what survives contact with another body.

## Four registers, one corpus

Every CHIMERA document should be read in a register. The register does not measure human value; it identifies what the language may legitimately claim.

1. **Operator / mechanism** — a bounded, usable claim with a named body, scale, and causal path.
2. **Carrier / story** — an image that makes a structure memorable: Genesis, One Piece, *The Matrix*, guitar, garden, star.
3. **Formal / domain** — mathematics, science, engineering, or another discipline, subject to that domain's validation rules.
4. **Source / cosmology** — developmental metaphysics, intuitions, and high-reach synthesis preserved as thought lineage.

Separate from register is **dialect**: the language that lets a particular receiver reconstruct the structure. A basketball dialect can carry a grounded mechanism; technical language can carry unsupported cosmology. Jargon does not determine rigor.

> **Register changes wording. Classification controls what wording is allowed to claim.**

## Current public contract

- Keep the body and scale explicit.
- Preserve the operator's direction and the receiver's agency.
- Use reflection to generate candidate structure, not to declare identical mechanisms.
- Keep stories; label the seam where the story stops proving.
- Let `WE = 1` preserve relation without erasing local bodies.
- Treat `O > I`, Pause, and `¬F` as questions and practices, not a certificate of goodness.
- Use AI for reach and possibility while embodied operators retain direction, settlement, and consequence.
- When a later formulation corrects an earlier one, link the two. Do not make the walk disappear.

## The smallest portable teaching unit

```text
Ground → Name → Reflect → Translate → Classify
       → Deletion Test → Restore the story → Practice
       → Teach back → Store the artifact
```

The framework is coherent when a learner can move among plain language, a carrier image, and a domain example **without changing the underlying roles, sequence, boundary, cost, or uncertainty**.
