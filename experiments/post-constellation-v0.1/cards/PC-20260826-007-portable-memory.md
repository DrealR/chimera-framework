## One-line seam

Platforms can accumulate useful memory about a person while leaving that person unable to carry a usable version of the relationship elsewhere.

## Candidate post

> Every platform gets to build a memory of you.
>
> Why do you have to start over every time you leave one?

## Why this entrance carries weight

- It begins from a common platform experience rather than AI-system vocabulary.
- It contains an answer-bearing tension: memory is being accumulated, but continuity remains provider-bound.
- It opens distinct Returns about privacy, export, lock-in, correction, forgetting, convenience, and identity.
- It can enter AI assistants, social profiles, music recommendations, health portals, work systems, and learning platforms without claiming those systems are equivalent.

## Candidate mechanisms

### 1. Memory asymmetry

```text
operator supplies behavior and correction
→ platform accumulates a useful model
→ operator leaves
→ model stays or becomes unusable
→ next relationship begins cold
```

The question is not whether every internal model should be exposed. It is what operator-owned state would be sufficient to continue responsibly.

### 2. Data reciprocity

A useful export must be inspectable, correctable, selective, portable, and able to reconstruct the working relation. A transcript dump may preserve words while losing decisions, authority, current state, unresolved gates, and consequences.

### 3. Continuity without total capture

Portable memory should preserve the minimum state needed for direction. It should also support expiry, revocation, chosen forgetting, and private/public membranes. More memory is not automatically more continuity.

## Desired Return

- experiences of useful or unusable data export;
- reasons someone would prefer a fresh start;
- examples where interoperability genuinely preserved capacity;
- privacy, safety, or security costs of portable state;
- the smallest artifact that let a person resume a real project elsewhere.

## Counterpaths

- Starting fresh can protect privacy, growth, safety, and the right not to be governed by an old profile.
- A platform may be unable to reveal internal models without exposing security, other users, or proprietary systems.
- Portability can reproduce false, stale, or harmful records across providers.
- Different systems have different capabilities; a portable state cannot guarantee equivalent behavior.
- An export standard can become compulsory identity infrastructure if refusal and deletion are weak.

## Firewall

- Do not equate portable continuity with consciousness or identity transfer.
- Do not imply that providers should expose credentials, hidden security signals, other people's data, or proprietary model weights.
- Do not make permanent memory the default.
- Do not treat familiar tone as proof of synchronization.
- Do not attribute a candidate drafted by this Body to another model or harness.
