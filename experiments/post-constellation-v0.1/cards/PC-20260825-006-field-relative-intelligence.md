## One-line seam

A rule can be locally intelligent in the field that shaped it and look irrational when another body imposes a foreign environment.

## Posted text

> The right rule inside the wrong environment can look like stupidity.
>
> A flying insect's orientation system works in nature. Put it around artificial light and that same rule can trap it.
>
> How often do we judge a body's intelligence by placing it inside a world it was never built to read?

## Why this entrance carries weight

- It begins with one concrete carrier rather than Framework vocabulary.
- It contains an answer-bearing proposition: apparent failure can be a field mismatch.
- The final question opens a real membrane for counterexamples and transfer.
- It can return through animal behavior, disability, education, software, institutions, migration, workplace design, and AI evaluation without claiming those domains are equivalent.

## Candidate mechanisms

### 1. Field-relative competence

```text
body + rule + native cues
→ locally competent behavior

same body + altered cues + foreign architecture
→ failure or apparent confusion
```

### 2. Environment as part of the test

The observer does not test a body from nowhere. A room, benchmark, classroom, interface, law, or workplace selects which signals are available and which actions count as success.

### 3. Rule–field mismatch

A stable rule can remain internally consistent while the surrounding field changes. The repair may require changing the rule, changing the environment, adding a translation layer, or refusing the test.

## Desired Return

- native-domain examples where apparently poor behavior becomes intelligible;
- counterexamples where the rule itself, not the environment, is the primary failure;
- distinctions between adaptation, accommodation, translation, and excuse;
- design practices that test both the body and the environment.

## Counterpaths

- Field mismatch does not make every failure intelligent.
- An adapted rule can remain harmful or obsolete even in its native field.
- Artificial light is one documented mechanism for some flying-insect behavior; it does not explain every insect indoors.
- Changing the environment can protect a body, but accommodation can also export costs to other bodies.
- A story transferred into human institutions requires evidence at the destination; the insect is a carrier, not proof of every analogy.

## Firewall

- Do not diagnose a person, group, animal, or model as secretly intelligent from one failure.
- Do not use ecological fit to erase accountability or deny learnable change.
- Do not claim one general intelligence ranking across species or systems.
- Do not imply that any foreign environment is inherently wrong or any native environment inherently healthy.
