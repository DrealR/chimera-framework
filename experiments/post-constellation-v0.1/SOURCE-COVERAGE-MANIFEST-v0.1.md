2. Food appears heavily through media/appetite; agriculture, access, disability, labor, ecology, and culture need deeper independent treatment.
3. Sexualization source material is gender-skewed and must not become moral panic or one-sided scrutiny.
4. Gambling, sports betting, gacha, loot boxes, investing, prediction markets, and fraud require separate evidence.
5. Historical “things were different” claims need prevalence, visibility, law, commerce, and acceptance separated.
6. Global South, non-English, rural, working-class, disabled, and culturally distinct contexts are underrepresented.
7. Physics and cosmology carriers are often rich but frequently outrun native-domain validation.
8. Positive countercases—healthy digital communities, accessible convenience, ethical adult work, beneficial equity, AI companionship, and non-extractive creator economies—need deliberate collection.
9. The Cube has abundant mappings but fewer preregistered predictions, falsifiers, and world-return tests.
10. A public question field can itself become an engagement harvester; views and agreement cannot promote Framework claims.
