# Cross-Platform Transduction Protocol v0.1

> **Classification:** PUBLIC_SAFE RESEARCH AND POST-CONSTELLATION PRACTICE
> **Authority:** analysis and candidate drafting may be delegated; publication remains operator-only
> **Purpose:** move a relation across platforms without stripping its context, laundering its certainty, or treating people as content inventory.

## The smallest complete loop

```text
Sense → Anchor → Branch → Lift → Re-anchor → Release → Return → Settle/Correct
```

1. **Sense:** notice a live seam, surprise, contradiction, or question.
2. **Anchor:** identify the high-context body that makes the seam legible.
3. **Branch:** read multiple situated responses, including resistance and minority paths.
4. **Lift:** extract a relation or question—not a sentence to disguise as original work.
5. **Re-anchor:** build a self-contained, answer-bearing portal for the destination platform.
6. **Release:** the operator chooses whether and where to publish.
7. **Return:** preserve attributed replies, silence, corrections, counterexamples, and later rediscovery.
8. **Settle/Correct:** update the repository, a practice, an experiment, or a decision.

Skipping settlement turns the loop into content mining. Skipping lineage turns it into context laundering.

## Platform-organ map

Platforms are not permanently assigned one role. Name the organ actually being used in the current path.

| Organ | What it contributes | Typical failure |
|---|---|---|
| Sensor | fast detection of a live seam | novelty mistaken for importance |
| Context anchor | shared compression and enough background to coordinate response | one narrator's tunnel treated as the whole field |
| Branch field | situated examples, jokes, objections, and lived variation | self-selection treated as a representative sample |
| Weighting field | visible routing, response, and social pressure | popularity treated as truth |
| Translation chamber | changes the shape for a new medium or audience | certainty inflated during compression |
| Emission layer | gives a relation a portable public address | posting confused with completion |
| Settlement layer | test, artifact, changed practice, or embodied consequence | explanation theater |
| Archive | versions, provenance, corrections, and later Return | latest polish erases the walked path |

If the candidate depends on a person's distinctive wording or lived report, link and attribute it. If context cannot be carried safely, preserve it privately or use `PRESERVE_ONLY`.

## Question-shaping pass

A portable portal should contain:

1. **recognizable mirror** — a concrete experience another person can enter;
2. **live seam** — a tension that is genuinely unresolved;
3. **bounded claim** — enough provisional structure to be worth answering;
4. **open membrane** — more than one honest answer can survive;
5. **return value** — an answer could change the map, not merely signal agreement;
6. **lineage** — the system can recover where the question came from.

Avoid:

- a lecture with a question mark;
- a stolen comment with cosmetic rewriting;
- a premise that diagnoses whole groups;
- trend mimicry without an operator observation;
- “engagement” as the only intended consequence.

## Sampling pass

Where the interface permits it, compare more than one view:

- Top / relevant;
- Newest / chronological;
- top-level comments;
- reply branches;
- agreement, resistance, humor, lived example, and correction.

Do not claim completeness. Infinite scroll, deleted material, moderation, personalization, regional differences, ranking experiments, and authentication state make the visible surface conditional.

## Privacy and safety

- public visibility does not erase human dignity or contextual integrity;
- do not collect cookies, credentials, hidden identifiers, private account data, or unrelated page state;
- minimize author identity by default;
- do not automate posting, replying, liking, subscribing, or persuasion;
- do not evade authentication, rate limits, access controls, or anti-bot measures;
- stop when the page or platform indicates no more visible material, a user-set limit is reached, or interaction becomes unstable;
- do not use comments involving children, abuse, health, finance, legal risk, or other sensitive experience as casual content inventory.

## Promotion test

A candidate may become a Post Constellation node only when:

- its destination and source anchor are named;
- the lifted relation survives deletion of distinctive source wording;
- a real counterpath is visible;
- factual load is independently checked or removed;
- the question remains answerable by someone outside the source audience;
- publication authority is explicit;
- silence is accepted as a valid Return.

## Carry line

> **Let one platform hold the context, another give the relation a portable address, and the repository preserve what happened between them.**
