# Question Portal Grammar v0.2

> **Classification:** PUBLIC_SAFE_EXPERIMENTAL_CANDIDATE. This is a human-selected writing and research instrument, not an engagement-maximization prompt or authorization to publish.

## The design target

The operator does not need more statements dressed as questions. The useful shape is a small public body that someone can enter from their own life and alter with a real answer.

The controlling grammar recovered from the Question Seed lineage is:

```text
widely felt observation
+ second observation that appears separate
+ remembered or expected prior state
+ open relational question
= a Pocket people can complete from different lived paths
```

The topic, people, and wording should change while the relational function survives. Changing a few words while retaining the same topic and actors is surface paraphrase. Costume transfer changes the domain or actors and then tests whether the relation still works.

## Portal anatomy

### 1. Recognition surface

Open with something observable enough that a reader can locate it without accepting a theory.

Weak: “Society has lost discipline.”

Stronger: “Food videos now often center the bite, dip, crunch, and reaction more than the meal.”

### 2. Relational turn

Bring in a second surface or changed relation.

> “At the same time, shopping, gambling, and reaction videos increasingly let us watch other people consume.”

### 3. Contrast or seam

Name the before/after, expectation/reality, inside/outside, or freedom/capture gap.

> “The object used to be the product. Now the act of consuming it can be the product too.”

### 4. Open causal invitation

Ask a question that can admit more than one path and could genuinely revise the author's map.

> “Are we becoming more excessive—or have platforms simply learned that appetite itself is watchable?”

### 5. Private return target

Before posting, name what would count as useful return: lived example, counterexample, mechanism, historical correction, expert source, design alternative, or new question.

## Nine portal archetypes

### Recognition Portal

Names a widespread but under-articulated change.

> When did personal profiles stop feeling like scrapbooks and start feeling like portfolios?

### Contrast Portal

Places an earlier and current interface side by side without assuming decline.

> Why did unlimited access make some things easier to consume but harder to remember?

### Seam Portal

Locates where two plausible readings stop fitting.

> When does convenience stop serving an appetite and start training it?

### Mirror Portal

Lets one domain reveal another while keeping a deletion test.

> What do gambling streams and mukbangs share, if anything, besides letting us watch someone else consume?

### Threshold Portal

Asks for the condition under which a mechanism changes category.

> At what point does a helpful role become a costume you can no longer take off?

### Operator Portal

Returns direction, veto, and exit to the reader.

> Which convenience in your life still serves you only because you deliberately keep a stopping point?

### Lived-Proof Portal

Invites an example with consequences rather than a slogan.

> What became more meaningful after you stopped having unlimited access to it?

### Counterpath Portal

Opens the strongest alternative explanation in the post itself.

> Are we less disciplined now, or are more private appetites simply visible and monetized than before?

### Carrier Portal

Uses a story, movie, character, object, or current event as an entrance—not as proof.

> What does Peter Pan reveal about the difference between a role that helps you navigate and one you cannot exit?

## The Question Constellation around one destination

Do not rewrite one sentence ten times. Rotate the aperture:

1. **Open:** What changed?
2. **Mechanism:** What could be producing it?
3. **Counterexample:** Where does the relation fail?
4. **Affected body:** Who receives the benefit and who receives the cost?
5. **Historical:** Is this new, newly visible, newly legal, or newly commercial?
6. **Design:** What boundary or interface would preserve the benefit without the capture?
7. **Failure:** What happens if this logic continues?

Seven apertures can explore one destination without pretending to be seven new destinations.

## Shape checks before a portal becomes a post node

### Recognition

Can a reader understand the observation in one pass?

### Branch capacity

Can reasonable people answer through different causes, histories, or lived experiences?

### Answer skeleton

Does the post carry a provisional relation rather than an empty “thoughts?”

### Reversibility

Could a good reply change the author's interpretation?

### Harm and dignity

Does the wording examine a mechanism rather than recruit contempt for a group?

### Evidence ceiling

Does it imply prevalence, causation, historical novelty, or consensus that has not been established?

### Portability

Will the question still mean something after the trend or celebrity carrier disappears?

### Settlement

Can an answer change a practice, design, source trail, relationship, experiment, or neighboring question?

If the only imagined return is “people agree and follow,” the portal is not ready.

## Public wording discipline

Prefer:

- one vivid observation over a catalogue of decline;
- “when,” “what changed,” “where is the line,” and “what do these share” over “why is everyone”;
- a mechanism question over a verdict about a type of person;
- ordinary words in the post and deeper CHIMERA language in the lineage card;
- one carrier per post;
- uncertainty that is real, not staged;
- short enough to invite participation, dense enough to survive without replies.

Avoid:

- “we all know”;
- “everyone does this now”;
- conflating legality, prevalence, visibility, commercialization, and approval;
- nostalgia that treats the past as safe for everyone;
- labeling people as slop, gluttons, degenerates, addicts, gold diggers, or NPCs;
- forcing private disclosure;
- using children as emotional proof without evidence;
- causal claims from a single clip, poll, or comment section;
- stacking five destinations into one post;
- automatic persona speech in the operator's name.

## The profile as sediment

A profile should not be one endless argument. Build a finite, mixed deck:

| Layer | Function | Typical portal |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | low-cost entry | familiar change or contrast |
| Mechanism | depth | seam, threshold, mirror |
| Carrier | memory and cultural access | story, movie, event, object |
| Lived proof | independent paths | example or counterexample request |
| Synthesis return | show that listening changed the map | “After reading the replies…” |

The profile becomes a field of coordinates. A new reader can enter through the doorway that matches their own life.

## Plant–Release–Return

### Plant

- choose the destination that is alive now;
- select one aperture;
- verify factual load;
- preserve the lineage card;
- post only with operator approval.

### Release

- leave the post intact unless correction or harm requires action;
- do not refresh metrics as a substitute for living;
- do not defend the question into a closed answer;
- let silence and delayed discovery remain possible.

### Return

- revisit at a natural checkpoint rather than continuously;
- preserve the strongest differing paths, not only agreement;
- distinguish testimony, explanation, source, joke, insult, and bot residue;
- update the destination or create a typed branch;
- publish a synthesis only if the return actually changed something.

Suggested light rhythm, adjustable by the operator:

```text
post
→ leave the platform
→ first qualitative review after the immediate loop has cooled
→ one later review for resurfacing or durable replies
→ archive the return
→ move on
```

## Evaluation

Primary field measures:

- qualified replies;
- causal-branch diversity;
- counterexamples and corrections;
- lived proof of passage;
- reply-to-reply conversation;
- independent questions generated;
- later return or rediscovery;
- change to the map or practice.

Secondary distribution measures:

- impressions;
- view-to-reply conversion;
- bookmarks;
- shares and quotes;
- profile visits and follows;
- temporal decay and resurfacing.

Distribution reports reach. It does not grade truth, worth, or the operator.

This is a **pre-node capture**, not a sufficient Post Constellation card. Bold labels in the candidate deck are editorial navigation only. Canonical metadata is assigned when a portal is promoted.

Portal archetypes map into the protocol's question-body types as follows:

| Portal archetype | Default Post question-body type |

| Contrast | Seam or Temporal |
| Seam | Seam |
| Mirror | Mirror |
| Threshold | Seam or Operator |
| Operator | Operator |
| Lived-Proof | Operator or composite |
| Counterpath | Seam or composite |

`Earth` is assigned when the question specifically tests whether activity becomes reciprocal, living, consequential relation. `composite` must name its component types. Promotion must then add every field required by the Post Constellation protocol: stable node ID, state, title, one-line seam, source Signals, lineage, mechanisms, counterpaths, firewall, why now, relations, version history, operator verdict, and later receipts/returns.
