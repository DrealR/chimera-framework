## Appetite and attention

### `DA-001` — Appetite Without Interval

1. **Strong shape candidate — Contrast**

   When did convenience stop meaning “less effort” and start meaning “no stopping point”?

   Food delivery, autoplay, one-click shopping, sexual media, and social validation can now answer an appetite without requiring us to leave the room.

   What happens to desire when it never has enough silence to reset?

2. **Lived proof**

   What is something you enjoy more because you *cannot* have it every day?

   Is the limit depriving you—or is the interval part of what gives the pleasure its shape?

3. **Operator**

   Which convenience in your life still serves you only because you deliberately keep a stopping point?

### `DA-002` — Consuming Consumption

4. **Strong shape candidate — Recognition**

   Food used to be the product.

   Now the bite, the dip, the crunch, the reaction—and even watching someone else eat—can be the product too.

   Why has the act of consuming become so watchable?

5. **Mirror**

   What do mukbangs, shopping hauls, unboxings, and gambling streams actually share?

   Are we watching the object—or borrowing the feeling of someone else consuming it?

6. **Counterpath**

   When someone watches another person eat, is it gluttony, companionship, sensory entertainment, curiosity—or something else entirely?

   What does the answer depend on?

### `DA-003` — Synthetic Stakes and Fast Money

7. **Strong shape candidate — Seam**

   Gambling, meme coins, and viral fame offer a visible chance to skip the middle.

   Is fast money mainly greed—or does it become most attractive when ordinary progress no longer feels believable?

8. **Contrast**

   Which lesson do we actually expect them to learn?

9. **Collectibles seam**

## Selection note

The strongest immediate field from the present Signal is not “people have become gluttonous.” It is:

```text
more appetites became continuously accessible
→ more acts of consumption became public spectacle
→ more platforms learned to monetize the spectacle and its measurement
→ stopping, context, and downstream cost moved away from the interface
```

That relation can be tested through food, gambling, shopping, sexuality, feeds, or AI production. Use one carrier at a time. Let the constellation carry the rest.

The newest portals add a second operating field:

```text
operator-owned state
→ context-bearing transfer
→ source-preserving platform translation
→ answer-bearing public question
→ attributed Return
→ visible correction
```

Do not publish the sequence as a campaign. Choose the question that is alive, preserve its source path, plant it, and walk away until a real Return arrives.
