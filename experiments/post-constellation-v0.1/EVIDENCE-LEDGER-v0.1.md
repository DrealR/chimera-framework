# Question-Destination Evidence Ledger v0.1

> **As of:** 2026-08-25
> **Classification:** PUBLIC_SAFE EVIDENCE SUPPORT / NOT A COMPLETE LITERATURE REVIEW
> **Purpose:** bind factual load for selected portals. A source can support one bounded claim without proving a destination mechanism.

## Evidence states

- **PRIMARY_OFFICIAL:** regulator, filing, law, or official statistics.
- **PRIMARY_RESEARCH:** original peer-reviewed study or meta-analysis.
- **RESEARCH_REVIEW:** peer-reviewed synthesis of multiple studies; useful for field boundaries but not an independent experiment.
- **SOURCE_ONLY:** supplied post, poll, video, or comment not independently verified.
- **CANDIDATE_MECHANISM:** interpretation to be tested.
- **UNKNOWN:** evidence not yet retrieved or insufficient.

## Ledger

### Cashless payment and spending

- **State:** PRIMARY_RESEARCH.
- **Design/qualifier:** meta-analysis.
- **Source:** Schomburgk, Belli, and Hoffmann, “Less cash, more splash? A meta-analysis on the cashless effect,” *Journal of Retailing* 100(3), 2024. [DOI landing page](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jretai.2024.05.003)
- **Observed finding:** meta-analysis of 71 papers reported an overall cashless-spending effect with meaningful variation across contexts; the effect was stronger for conspicuous consumption and weaker for prosocial spending.
- **Supports:** asking whether payment abstraction changes the salience or felt friction of spending.
- **Does not support:** “digital money makes everyone overconsume,” one causal pathway for every payment method, or a moral conclusion about cashless systems.
- **Routes:** DA-001, DA-003, DA-012.

### Mukbang/cookbang and dietary behavior

- **State:** PRIMARY_RESEARCH.
- **Design/qualifier:** observational and primarily cross-sectional studies; the cited studies do not establish causation.
- **Sources:**
  - Sung et al., “Mukbang and Cookbang watching and dietary behavior in Korean adolescents,” 2024. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39109196/)
  - “Food-Related Online Media (Mukbang and Cookbang) Exposure and Dietary Risk Behaviors in Korean Adolescents,” 2024. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38992471/)
  - “Associations between mukbang viewing and disordered eating behaviors,” 2023. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36807921/)
  - “The Mukbang (Eating Broadcast) Paradox,” 2026. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42124079/)
- **Observed finding:** several cross-sectional studies found associations between frequency or problematic viewing and selected dietary-risk or disordered-eating measures. The 2026 adult study reported a mixed pattern: better balance scores alongside poorer moderation measures and more reported delivery/instant-food and impulsive/binge-eating changes.
- **Supports:** treating mukbang as a non-binary field worth investigating through appetite, companionship, culture, moderation, and media exposure.
- **Does not support:** causal claims, universal effects, diagnosis of viewers, or “mukbang equals gluttony.”
- **Routes:** DA-002, DA-008, DA-020.

### Young people's exposure to gambling promotion

- **State:** PRIMARY_OFFICIAL.
- **Design/qualifier:** official surveyed recall in one jurisdiction and study year.
- **Source:** UK Gambling Commission, *Young People and Gambling 2025*. [Official statistics](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/statistics-and-research/publication/young-people-and-gambling-2025-official-statistics)
- **Observed finding:** 79% of surveyed young people recalled gambling promotion through at least one online or offline source. Among those exposed through specific channels, social media, apps, and live/video-streaming platforms were the most frequently cited weekly exposure channels. The report separately measured following gambling content and influencer advertising.
- **Supports:** the claim that gambling promotion is a meaningful part of the surveyed youth media environment and that streaming/social channels belong in the child-boundary question.
- **Does not support:** universal exposure, causation of gambling harm, equivalence between all gambling-like systems, or a claim about every country.
- **Routes:** DA-003, DA-007, DA-008.

### Social/video surveillance and child/teen protections

- **State:** PRIMARY_OFFICIAL.
- **Design/qualifier:** regulator staff report based on company responses to compulsory orders.
- **Source:** U.S. Federal Trade Commission, 2024 staff report and release. [FTC summary](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-staff-report-finds-large-social-media-video-streaming-companies-have-engaged-vast-surveillance)
- **Observed finding:** the FTC reported extensive collection and monetization of user data among major social/video services and described inadequate safeguards for children and teens, based on company responses to FTC orders.
- **Supports:** examining how adult advertising and data incentives reach younger users and how platform design shares responsibility with families.
- **Does not support:** one mental-health effect for every child, identical practices across all platforms, or a claim that users have no agency.
- **Routes:** DA-004, DA-008, DA-036, DA-037.

### Nonconsensual intimate imagery and AI forgeries

- **State:** PRIMARY_OFFICIAL.
- **Design/qualifier:** enacted law, agency guidance, and enforcement notices; not prevalence research.
- **Sources:**
  - FTC, “Take It Down Act enforcement starts now,” 2026. [Business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2026/05/take-it-down-act-enforcement-starts-now-what-know-about-ftc-tida)
  - FTC, warning letters concerning “nudify” services, 2026. [Enforcement release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/05/ftc-sends-warning-letters-companies-about-compliance-take-it-down-act)
- **Observed fact:** the Act's covered material includes real and digitally created or altered intimate imagery, including AI-generated forgeries; covered platforms must provide a notice/removal process and remove validly reported content and known identical copies within the statutory window.
- **Supports:** separating nonconsensual synthetic abuse from consensual adult expression and treating it as a concrete governance problem.
- **Does not support:** claims about prevalence, effectiveness of enforcement, or equivalence among all sexual content.
- **Routes:** DA-005, DA-008, DA-019, DA-036.

### Nvidia employee-equity example

- **States:** [PRIMARY_OFFICIAL, SOURCE_ONLY].
- **Design/qualifier:** official filing for equity-program structure; unverified open-poll claim for employee net worth.
- **Official source:** NVIDIA fiscal 2025 Form 10-K. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000023/nvda-20250126.htm)
- **Observed official fact:** NVIDIA describes restricted and performance stock awards and an employee stock purchase plan; employees may purchase shares under stated plan rules and discounts.
- **Source-only claim:** supplied social posts asserted that roughly 78% or 80% of employees were millionaires based on a several-thousand-response poll. The supplied thread itself disputes the sampling and says it was an open TeamBlind poll. No representative company-wide net-worth statistic was found in the official filing.
- **Allowed wording:** “If the employee-wealth claim were true…” or “NVIDIA's equity programs and stock appreciation raise a useful worker-ownership question.”
- **Blocked wording:** “80% of NVIDIA employees are millionaires” as an established fact.
- **Routes:** DA-017, DA-018.

### Honeybee learning and flexible decision-making

- **State:** RESEARCH_REVIEW.
- **Design/qualifier:** reviews of controlled behavioral research, primarily with foraging honeybees; not a general insect-intelligence score.
- **Sources:**
  - Zhang, Si, and Pahl, “Visually Guided Decision Making in Foraging Honeybees,” 2012. [Open-access review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3376410/)
  - Giurfa, “Learning of sameness/difference relationships by honey bees,” 2021. [Open-access review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8772047/)
- **Observed synthesis:** honeybees can learn cues and rules, generalize to novel tasks, switch decisions across contexts, and learn selected relational concepts such as sameness and difference under experimental conditions.
- **Supports:** rejecting the claim that social insects are merely rigid command-following automata; asking how individual flexibility coexists with colony-level role coordination.
- **Does not support:** human-like free will, consciousness, one ranking of insect versus mammal intelligence, or equivalence across all insect species.
- **Routes:** DA-024, DA-030, DA-033, DA-040.

### Artificial light and insect field mismatch

- **State:** PRIMARY_RESEARCH.
- **Design/qualifier:** field stereo-videography around artificial point lights, covering multiple flying-insect orders; one mechanism for one class of human-built disturbance.
- **Source:** Fabian et al., “Why flying insects gather at artificial light,” *Nature Communications*, 2024. [Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-44785-3)
- **Observed finding:** artificial point light induced recurring abnormal flight motifs, and the authors' evidence supports disruption of a normally useful dorsal-light orientation response rather than simple long-range attraction.
- **Supports:** the field-relative competence question: a rule adaptive in one environment can produce apparently irrational behavior in an altered sensory field.
- **Does not support:** a general explanation for insects inside houses, a claim that built environments make insects unintelligent, or a consciousness comparison.
- **Routes:** DA-024, DA-030, DA-040.

### Domestication, experience, and domain-specific cognition

- **State:** PRIMARY_RESEARCH.
- **Design/qualifier:** controlled comparative dog/wolf studies with small or bounded samples and task-specific measures.
- **Sources:**
  - Lampe et al., “The effects of domestication and ontogeny on cognition in dogs and wolves,” *Scientific Reports*, 2017. [Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-12055-6)
  - Range et al., “Wolves lead and dogs follow, but they both cooperate with humans,” *Scientific Reports*, 2019. [Study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-40468-y)
- **Observed finding:** results differed by task and interaction style. In one study wolves outperformed dogs on causal cues while groups performed similarly on tested communicative/behavioral cues; another found both could cooperate with familiar humans while wolves more often initiated and dogs more often followed.
- **Supports:** domain-specific, environment-sensitive comparison and the possibility that domestication changes the distribution and legibility of capacities.
- **Does not support:** “pets are smarter,” “wild animals are simpler,” or one general domestication effect across species and cognitive domains.
- **Routes:** DA-020, DA-024, DA-030, DA-040.

## Evidence gaps deliberately left open

- Whether appetite-focused video formats causally increase overconsumption across populations.
- Whether sexual, gambling, or scam behavior is more prevalent now versus more visible, commercialized, or discussable.
- Comparative historical exposure to bragging, infidelity, and transactional dating norms.
- Longitudinal effects of algorithmic exposure on stable preferences.
- Direct child and teen accounts across geography, class, disability, and culture.
- Whether “slop” can be operationalized as a production/digestion rate mismatch rather than a taste label.
- Population-level distributional effects behind exceptional employee-equity outcomes.
- Whether reduced survival/maintenance pressure generally creates more behavioral exploration or merely changes which behavior observers can see.
- Cross-species comparisons of colony coordination, individual flexibility, play, and intelligence using commensurable measures.

## Use rule

Every factual post node should declare:

1. the exact claim the source supports;
2. design, sample, jurisdiction, and date;
3. whether the finding is causal, correlational, self-reported, or administrative;
4. the strongest plausible counterpath;
5. language that remains blocked.

> **Evidence can raise the floor of a question without closing its ceiling.**
