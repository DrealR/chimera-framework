# Attention Metric Signatures

> **Origin:** July 2026 — dimensional physics of the X attention field; views as baseline light; likes/reposts/comments as derived dimensions; chords of engagement; Living Magnet sensor evolution.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

Public attention metrics are not a single number to maximize. They form a **small dimensional space** whose **ratios and configurations** reveal the quality of relationship between a signal and the field.

> **Views** = baseline presence (light). Without visibility, nothing public exists.  
> **Likes, reposts, comments** = derived dimensions that only become meaningful *relative to views and to each other*.  
> A **signature** = a recognizable chord of those ratios — a timbre of attention, not a scoreboard.

Reading signatures is **field literacy**. Chasing raw views alone is often a death spiral of local optimization.

---

## Dimensions

| Metric | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Views (V)** | Entry into the public field — presence / light |
| **Likes (L)** | Low-cost affirmation / soft agreement |
| **Reposts (R)** | Amplification / “this should travel” |
| **Comments (C)** | Processing / dialogue / tension / labor |

Derived ratios (examples; normalize carefully by audience size and time-since-post):

```text
like_rate     ≈ L / V
repost_rate   ≈ R / V
comment_rate  ≈ C / V
discuss_ratio ≈ C / max(L, 1)
amplify_ratio ≈ R / max(L, 1)
```

Absolute numbers without ratios lie. Ratios without time-since-post also lie. Always note **age of post** and **account scale**.

## Lightweight signature taxonomy (v0)

Heuristics for navigation — **not** universal laws. Re-validate on your domains.

| Signature ID | Rough configuration | Often means (hypothesis) |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **hollow_reach** | High V · very low L/R/C | Reach without relation; bots, farming, or pure cold exposure |
| **soft_consume** | High V · L moderate · R/C low | Passive consumption; mild like, little processing |
| **amplify_wave** | High R relative to C · L mid | Emotional/meme/narrative spread; amplify more than discuss |
| **discussion** | High C relative to L · R mid/low | People laboring in replies; debate, teaching, controversy |
| **healthy_balance** | V enough to register · L/R/C not collapsed · C and R both alive | Genuine multi-mode human attention (target-ish for fertile ground) |
| **early_momentum** | Low–mod V · rates already match healthy/amplify patterns while young | Possible early compounder — Magnet gold zone if topic resonates |
| **pile_on** | Explosive C · hostile texture · low constructive reply depth | Discussion-shaped but extractive or swarm (firewall: not “healthy” just because C is high) |
| **prestige_quiet** | Mod V · high quality replies from known centers · low mass R | Niche high-signal; not viral, often valuable |

**Music metaphor (carrier):** views = volume of the room; ratios = which chord is being played.

## Operator read method

**Primary cut is always extreme recency** (last 30–60 minutes). Signatures are a **secondary** layer on top of that window — never a replacement for it.

Within the recency window:

1. Optional **momentum boost** — light tilt toward accounts/posts that already move attention.  
2. **Observe metric configurations** (V + L + R + C + age) — especially early rates.  
3. **Extract reference signatures** from posts that later proved high-signal for *you*.  
4. **Prefer early_momentum + discussion/healthy_balance** in resonant topics over hollow_reach.  
5. **Pause** before narrative lock: “this signature always means X” is collapse (¬F).

**Daily default:**  
> Show live activity (last 30–60 min). Optionally boost natural momentum. Optionally rank by healthy early ratios + Magnet resonance.

## Personal calibration practice

1. Take 10–20 of *your* posts with varied V.  
2. Record V, L, R, C, age, topic.  
3. Label felt quality: alive / flat / distorted.  
4. Note which ratio chords matched “alive.”  
5. Those become **your** reference signatures — not the internet’s.

---

## Firewalls

- Engagement farming can **forge** signatures; texture and account history still matter.  
- Controversy inflates C; classify pile-on vs discussion.  
- Small accounts and large accounts need different normalizations.  
- Taxonomy is a **map**, not a scoring religion.  
- No auto-optimize-for-signature bots as core CHIMERA practice — that becomes Tower extraction.
