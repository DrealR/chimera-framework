# Algorithmic Worldlines and Relational Distance

> **Classification:** attention-environment mechanism and diagnostic. “Worldline” is a carrier for recursively conditioned exposure across time, not a claim that recommendation systems obey relativistic spacetime physics.

## The recursive worldline

A recommendation environment does not merely display an existing self. It participates in rehearsing a next one:

```text
person
→ reaction
→ ranking update
→ new exposure
→ changed memory and salience
→ changed person
```

Repeated exposure thickens some paths and thins others. The resulting **algorithmic worldline** is the path a body walks through a recursively updated encounter field.

Two people in the same room can therefore live under different rendered skies: different witnesses, vocabularies, crises, norms, collaborators, and expected futures.

## Relational distance is a vector

“Near” and “far” are incomplete until the dimension is named.

```text
d(A,B) = [space, time, sense, action, memory,
          semantic, social, cultural distance]
```

- **spatial:** physical separation;
- **temporal:** delay or phase difference;
- **sensory:** what each body can receive;
- **action:** ability to affect one another;
- **memory:** shared or mutually accessible history;
- **semantic:** conceptual and vocabulary distance;
- **social:** graph and relationship distance;
- **cultural:** protocol, norm, and interpretation distance.

A faraway collaborator can be semantically and socially near. A family member in the same house can be emotionally or informationally far.

> **Before comparing bodies, ask: distant according to which dimension?**

## Accumulated divergence

Small differences in rate compound:

```text
Gap(T) = ∫₀ᵀ Δr(t) dt
```

This structure appears in clocks, habits, feeds, learning, markets, and institutions. A small persistent difference in what enters the body can produce a large later separation.

Every cumulative system therefore needs release: revision, forgetting, sleep, decay, repair, retirement, succession, or deliberate recoupling. Without release, residue becomes trajectory.

## Narrative age and embodied age

Digital exposure can produce high **informational mileage**: many stories, crises, identities, and simulated paths pass through attention quickly. This is not the same as embodied mileage.

```text
exposure supplies traces
practice supplies capacity
consequence supplies calibration
rest supplies consolidation
```

A body can sound narratively old while remaining physically unpracticed. AI can deliver the map quickly; the body still compiles it through attempts, error, recovery, and proof of passage.

## Synchronization events

Shared physical bodies—meals, classrooms, rehearsals, games, work, weather, care, and local crisis—can recouple divergent informational worldlines. They do not erase difference. They provide a common event whose consequences are harder to personalize away.

## Worldline scan

1. Map the feeds, people, and models supplying the current encounter field.
2. Identify which distance dimensions are widening or narrowing.
3. Distinguish informational mileage from walked capacity.
4. Add an independent source or a real resistance current.
5. Create a shared reality event that can return consequence.
6. Recheck the accumulated gap after time, not after one exposure.

## Firewalls

- Algorithmic shaping does not eliminate agency; it changes the available field and friction.
- A personalized field is not unreal. It is partial and recursively constructed.
- Shared physical presence does not guarantee mutual understanding.
- The integral is a structural model, not a claim that every human variable is precisely measurable.
