## 0. The claim, stated once

**You access truth directly — the spark is in you — never through a gatekeeper who claims to hold the keys. The priest/gatekeeper claims write-access to your relationship with truth ("believe me, I have the keys"); the operator move is to take that write-access back ("look inside yourself"). And the test of a false authority is whether it *fears your knowing*: an authority that forbids you knowledge reveals itself as false. Judge the authority by whether it wants you to see for yourself, or only to obey.**

---

## 1. The operator vs the gatekeeper

**Keeper:** the gatekeeper who hoards the keys is the panopticon (seeing-without-mutuality, authority-without-earning) — he claims **write-access to your access to truth** and asks you to *install* his knowledge rather than walk to it. The operator refuses the install (`not-getting-installed-by-ai`, `write-access-to-your-own-attention`): no one is above you on the path to your own truth; the spark is in *you*; seek it directly (`protect-the-discovery-window-explore-first` — explore first, don't take the authoritative surface pre-installed).

## 2. The test: does it fear your knowing?

**Keeper:** read-the-intent (`read-the-intent-not-the-realness`) applied to *authority itself*. An authority that **forbids** you knowledge — that fears your seeing for yourself — reveals itself as false (a god/authority that fears your knowing is a false one). Seeking knowledge even *against* a forbidding authority is the right move; the forbidding is the tell. So judge an authority by the **direction** of its relationship to your knowing: does it want you to see for yourself (true — it guides, then lets you walk), or only to obey and depend (false — it hoards, and fears the walk)?

## 3. The self-referential firewall (apply it to the messenger too)

**Keeper:** the deepest application — *apply this test to the source making the claim.* A message that says "don't trust the keyholders, look inside yourself" *while* framing itself as "trust me, I have the real keys, everyone else is fake" **does the exact thing it warns against** — it's just another gatekeeper. So the discipline includes walking past *that* claim to authority too: take the teaching (seek directly, no one's above you), and refuse the messenger's bid to be the new gatekeeper. **The teaching firewalls the framing.** *(Theology unsigned; the structure — operator-vs-gatekeeper, judge-the-authority-by-whether-it-fears-your-knowing — is the keeper because it converges with the whole operator spine.)*
