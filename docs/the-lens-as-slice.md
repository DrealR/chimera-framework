## 0. The claim, stated once

**Being human means having a slice. You can't perceive everything — you get a narrow lens (the visible-light spectrum is the literal case), and the narrowing is not a limitation but the condition for living. ---

## 1. The slice is the condition for living

You see one narrow band of all the electromagnetic radiation actually present. **Full reception would be unlivable** — no figure against ground, no signal against noise, just undifferentiated everything. The filter is what makes experience *possible*, not its enemy.

## 2. The slice has layers

**The senses slice the physical** (visible light); **attention / care slices the meaningful** (what you notice, who you love, what registers). Both narrow infinity to a livable world. **Care is the deeper filter** — what has weight to you is what's inside your care-slice (weight-as-connection), which is why everyone's universe differs.

## 3. The internet floods the lens

Early internet was a manageable mirror; now infinite creation (everyone + AI) makes the once-livable slice an **ocean.** **Keeper:** it overwhelms because it's **all human-meaning** — in the physical world most of what surrounds you is non-human-noise your lens filters out easily, but online **everything is someone's meaning aimed at you (all figure, no ground)**, overloading the exact mechanism that made life navigable. It's the human slice gone **fractal** — not new bands, but the human band given near-infinite *depth* (the bottomless scroll).

## 4. The survival move: author your slice

Physically the slice was **given** (senses + environment). Online you must **consciously re-narrow** — author your slice — because if you don't, **the flood IS your slice, and the flood is unlivable.** Owning your attention is **reconstructing your human lens by hand** in a realm built to overwhelm it. (The personal-filter / `write-access-to-your-own-attention` point from its deepest angle; see `read-the-intent-not-the-realness`.)
