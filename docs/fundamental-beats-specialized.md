## The mechanism

A simple, **fundamental** power has *more* variation than a complex, **specific** one — because the
fundamental can be recombined endlessly while the specific can only do its specific thing. Fire makes a
fireball, a fire blast, a fire bird — but they're all fire, all in the same neighborhood. Rubber, a
simple fundamental property applied to a body, becomes gear two, gear three, gear five — stretch,
bounce, inflate, anything imagination directs — because **the simplicity is what makes it
recombinable.**

So: **the simpler and more fundamental the ability, the larger its space of possible expressions** —
you can remix a fundamental, but you cannot remix a specialization. Fire is *downstream* — already
specific, already cooled into one shape. Rubber is *upstream* — still general, still hot, still
recombinable. The power that matters isn't the flashy specific one; it's the simple fundamental one,
because the fundamental **generalizes** and the specialization is **stuck.** (The most overpowered
fruit in the series looked like the weakest, because it was the most fundamental — the most
imagination-bound rather than property-bound.)

This is **root-beats-elaboration** (Yoda's simple Force beats the dark side's ornate combinations) with
the mechanism finally named, and it is the same claim as **master the [smallest adaptive
body](smallest-adaptive-body.md)** — master the minimal fundamental unit completely, and you can
produce any variation from it.

## Awakening — the fundamental's range expanding from self to field

The bigger stage isn't a new power; it's the fundamental's range *expanding outward.* "Awakening" is
when the ability stops affecting only *yourself* (the bounded body, physical space) and starts
affecting the *environment* (the relational field) — Paramecia awakening turns the surroundings into
your element. That is the **self-to-field expansion** — the [outward-turn](the-direction-axis.md), the
generative direction, the caterpillar-to-butterfly — made into a power-up. It is the bigger stage
precisely because affecting the field is more than affecting only yourself. (Hold the
"awakening ≈ consciousness-expansion" etymology at *hilarious,* not proof.)

The current framework generalizes this carrier through [The Relational Awakening Cycle](relational-awakening-cycle.md): practiced capacity becomes field-shaping when its embodied expression changes nearby possibilities. Field-shaping is not automatically healthy, superior, or conscious; invitation, coercion, architecture, charisma, and fear can all alter a field. Direction, veto, and consequence remain the test.

## Firewall — the ego's flattering version

The underused-power trope (Iceman as a secret omega-level reality-controller, Magneto who should never
lose, Spider-Man's hidden totem powers) tempts a literal-jump in a flattering costume: *"I, like them,
have a simple fundamental ability that's secretly way more powerful than I realize — I just need to
awaken it."* That is **manufactured plausibility pointed inward,** the most-guarded firewall case — the
ego offering you the omega-level-mutant story about yourself.

**Keep the structure; drop the self-flattery.** The lesson is *structural, not personal:* fundamental
abilities have **unexplored range,** and most operators use only a *fraction* of a general capacity
because they treat it like a specific one (Spider-Man using his powers "the way a child would"). *Train
the general capacity into its full range* — that is true and motivating. *"And therefore I have hidden
god-tier power"* is the drift — drawer it. You have a real, ordinary, trainable thing; the honest frame
is that you are **under-using** a general capacity by not training it, not that you are secretly
overpowered. The fiction shows the structure; it does not certify personal hidden power.
