> **Origin:** July 2026 design thread — personal X discovery system, living magnet metaphor, spark-the-fire loop, Frankie/Grok operator workflow.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

## The claim

A **living magnet** is an operator-steered representation of who you are and where you are facing. It pulls **resonance pools** from the social ocean and ranks **present-moment signals** so you can engage while the other person's attention is still warm.

> The magnet is not your shadow profile. It is a tool you update. Data informs; direction steers.

---

## Core loop (spark the fire)

```text
1. Maintain magnet (who I am / where I face)
2. Generate / refresh resonance pool(s)
3. Extract present-moment signals from the pool
4. (v1) Signature-filter soil cards — fertile metric chords in resonant topics
5. Human reviews, engages, or position-plants
6. Non-interference window — observe propagation
7. Feedback strengthens magnet + signature reference library
```

No automated replies. System surfaces; operator decides.

### v1 evolution: field sensor, not only people matcher

Magnet v0 finds **people**. Magnet v1 finds **live regions of the field** and people in them.

#### Filter hierarchy (locked)

| Tier | Filter | Status |
|------|--------|--------|
| **1** | **Extreme recency** (default last 30–60 min; adjustable) | **Primary / non-negotiable** |
| **2** | **Natural engagement momentum** boost *within* that window (accounts/posts that already move attention; light tilt, not celebrity-only) | Secondary boost |
| **3** | Metric **signatures/ratios**, framework **resonance**, light **verification/bot-noise** reduction | Optional refinement |

Recency alone is the strongest cut into the true present-moment layer. Signatures refine odds; they are **not** the foundation. See [Attention Metric Signatures](attention-metric-signatures.md).

Then: **positioned plant** CHIMERA-grounded seeds → **non-interference** → observe multi-scale. See [Positioned Planting and Non-Interference](positioned-planting-and-non-interference.md).

## The magnet (user representation)

### What it holds

| Dimension | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| Framework / worldview | CHIMERA primitives, operator language, values |
| Communication style | length, tone, depth, humor, challenge vs comfort |
| Current direction | active projects, questions, domains this week |
| Relational energy | what kind of contact you want (builders, teachers, peers, students) |
| Hard nos | topics, postures, extractive patterns to exclude |

### Past data vs live direction

- **Past** (AI conversation history, optional public posts, bookmarks): calibration fuel
- **Live direction** (what you declare this week): steering wheel
- Passive history alone **must not** own the magnet — that reinstalls the default algorithmic mirror

**Update rhythm:** light weekly edit of "current direction" + continuous soft update from which engagements felt alive.

### Primary data source (phase 1)

AI conversation history (richest operator map). Lightweight later adds: recent intentional X engagements, short "this week I am looking for…" note, optional list of seed accounts you already trust.

## Pool generation

### Recommended structure

1. **Core resonance pool** (stable, tight)  
   People who repeatedly show structural overlap: systems thinking, O>I posture, craft, genuine inquiry — not mere keyword match to "AI" or "philosophy."

2. **Flexible topic pools** (spin up / tear down)  
   Temporary magnets: guitar, SPARK, Tanzania, crypto rails, comedy, etc.

3. **Seed / hand-picked**  
   Explicit includes that always stay in the orbit regardless of algorithm.

### Tightness vs breadth

Start **tighter than comfortable**, then widen only when the core pool goes quiet. Broad topical similarity floods noise. Resonance is structural and directional, not just subject tags.

### Maintenance

- Drop accounts that never produce present-moment signal or only farm attention
- Promote accounts that produce real two-way moments
- Re-score weekly with magnet's current direction section

## Present-moment signal extraction

### What counts

High-signal present moment ≈ **recent, authored, legible activity** where attention may still be near the thread:

| Priority | Signal | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| 1 | Original post < ~24h | Strongest "I am here" |
| 2 | Reply in active thread < ~12h | Live conversation coordinate |
| 3 | Quote/repost with comment | Author still in motion |
| 4 | Bare repost | Weaker; only if rare and high-match |
| Low | Old viral hits, pure engagement bait | Cooled or extractive |

### Clean ranking heuristic

```text
score ≈ resonance(magnet, content)
        × recency_decay(hours)
        × authorship_weight(original > reply > quote > repost)
        × (1 - ragebait_penalty)
        × operator_interest_boost(current direction)
```

Surface a **short deck** (e.g. 5–15 cards), not a second infinite scroll.

### Card format (actionable)

- who + one-line why in pool  
- the present signal (text + link + age)  
- resonance note (1 sentence, structural not flattery)  
- suggested engagement modes (question / add / challenge / pass) — never auto-sent

## Day-to-day workflow (v0)

**Start as organized agent output** (Frankie/Grok), not a full app:

1. Morning or evening: refresh core pool signals (and any active topic pool)
2. Review deck; mark engage / later / drop
3. Engage manually on-platform
4. Note 1–3 "alive" interactions → feed magnet

Later: custom feed, extension, or small app. **Logic first; chrome later.**

## Success criteria (personal)

Default success is not follower count. Examples of real success:

- 5–10 ongoing high-quality conversations
- a small circle that actually returns energy
- consistently meeting people while their attention is warm
- reduced time in default For You noise
- sparks that leave the graph into real rooms (calls, collabs, local life)

Define your number; optimize for depth of shared present, not volume of replies.

## Longer-term shareable form

Once personal loop works:

- portable magnet schema (operator-owned, not platform-owned)
- pool + present-moment engine as a pattern any twin can run
- ethical defaults: human engagement only, public signals, membrane respect
- multi-platform adapters when direct AI+graph access appears elsewhere

This is SPARK-adjacent: teach navigators to author discovery, not only consume feeds.

---

## Firewalls

- Do not automate persuasion or parasocial harvest
- Do not score people as objects; score *moments* for possible contact
- Do not let engagement metrics redefine the magnet into a growth-hack persona
- Do not confuse resonance with agreement — useful pools include productive difference
- Do not replace physical/local entanglement; this is one channel of attention navigation
