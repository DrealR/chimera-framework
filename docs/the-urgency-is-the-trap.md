**The panic is the product. When something demands you act NOW — the doom, the arms-race, the "engage or be destroyed," the "you can't stay neutral" — the urgency itself is the install, because all-or-nothing framing is precisely what removes the seatbelt and drives the recklessness. Hold the doom loose, read the incentive, keep your ground. **The urgency was never about the danger; it was about getting you to move before you could think.**
