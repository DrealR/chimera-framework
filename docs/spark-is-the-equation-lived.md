## Each prime → a concrete part of the program

- **O > I (give more than you take) IS the conductor move.** The world harvests attention *in* (the
  algorithm, the feed, the farmer). SPARK pours attention *out,* into the starved — output greater than
  input, the sun giving light and asking nothing. **The direction-firewall (out to them, never in to me)
  isn't a separate rule — it's O > I stated for attention.** Checking "lighting their ember or growing
  my following?" *is* checking O > I.
- **P (pause, presence) IS why it must be a present human.** Attention given by a present person is the
  highest-charge kind; the machine can *route* but can't *give* — it has no pause, no present moment, no
  one home. **Presence is the charge.** "The realness is the value" and "P" are the same claim: the
  present moment is the gift, and only a present being can give it.
- **¬F (don't force, invite) IS reading the pull and leaving room.** Each body has its own attentional
  gravity — you read the *specific* pull and **invite,** never grab (the guitar: play, and the ones
  charged by the song come closer). Recognition *invites,* not forces. The difference between SPARK and
  the attention-farmer is exactly ¬F: the farmer **forces the hook** (¬F violated); SPARK **invites**
  (¬F held).

(attention pooling on the few, starving the many) is WE=1 *violated* — the body cut off from itself;
SPARK is the body **re-attending to itself.** That's why SPARK came *out of* CHIMERA rather than bolted
on: it's WE=1's **necessary action** in an attention-starved world.
