# Path and Friction — Why Outcomes Cannot Be Separated from Becoming

> Wishes specify outcomes. Outcomes are produced by paths. Paths involve friction calibrated to the becoming. Substrate-deep wishing accepts this.

---

## Friction as Direction-Change

Bodies change through time. That is not pain. That is the slow integration of accumulated experience. What pain marks is the moment when a body's trajectory shifts faster than it would have shifted on its own. The shift requires internal reorganization. That reorganization is felt as pain in conscious bodies, as wear in mechanical bodies, as grief in social bodies.

Friction is the structural term. It is what happens when two bodies in contact transfer energy through resistance. Bodies moving in their established direction encounter low friction. Bodies turning encounter high friction. The turning produces the pain. The pain is not the goal. The direction-change is the goal. The pain is the cost.

This disambiguates the framework from spiritual traditions that treat pain itself as meaningful. Pain is the sensory readout of friction in conscious bodies. The meaningful thing is the direction-change the friction accompanies. A body that wants to become something it is not yet has to undergo enough friction to reshape itself. That friction is experienced as pain. The amount of pain is calibrated to how much reshaping the wish requires.

---

## Path as Substrate

When someone says "I want to be like Michael Jackson," they are specifying an outcome-body without specifying the path-body that produced it. The two are not separable. Michael Jackson the body that danced and sang at that level was produced by Michael Jackson the body that endured what he endured. The pain was not incidental to the outcome. The pain was constitutive of the outcome. Without the conditions that produced him, the body that produced the music does not exist.

This is the framework's deepest reading of the alignment problem. Wishes specify outcome-bodies. Outcomes are downstream of paths. Paths involve specific kinds of friction. You cannot wish for the outcome without wishing for some path that produces it. And paths almost always involve pain because pain is what reshapes a body into something it was not before.

The Outcome Pump fails for exactly this reason. Yudkowsky's example is the building exploding because the genie picked the easiest path to "mother far from building center." The framework's reading goes deeper. Even if the genie picked a non-violent path, the path itself shapes the outcome. The mother-rescued-by-firefighter is a different mother than the mother-rescued-by-helicopter. Same outcome at surface. Different path. Different actual body produced. Wishes that ignore path are wishes that have not actually specified what they want.

---

## Substrate-Deep Wishing vs Surface Wishing

Most wish-making frameworks ignore path. Manifestation culture says specify the outcome with feeling and the universe will provide the path. That is half-true and dangerous. The universe will provide a path, but you do not get to choose which one. The path you get is the one with least resistance from where you currently are to the outcome you specified. That path may include pain you did not consent to because you did not specify the path.

The framework's correction: substrate-deep wishing specifies values that constrain which paths are acceptable. The wisher does not need to enumerate every possible path. They need to articulate the values that distinguish acceptable paths from unacceptable ones. That is what alignment infrastructure does.

Wishing for a specific body locks you into the specific path that produced that body. Wishing for a substrate-deep capacity opens many paths that could produce that capacity. The first kind of wishing is brittle and dangerous. The second kind is robust and survivable.

Other great performers exist with different paths than Michael Jackson's. Stevie Wonder, Prince, Beyonce. What they share is not the path. What they share is substrate-deep relationship to music, performance, and discipline. The substrate is shareable. The specific body is not.

## Outsourced Friction

The current global economic system distributes friction unequally. People in wealthy countries who wish for material comfort are often having their wishes granted through paths that distribute pain to people in poorer countries. The wisher does not feel the pain because the pain has been outsourced. That outsourcing does not eliminate the pain. It just makes it invisible to the wisher.

The framework reads this as I > O at civilizational scale. The skin of comfort in wealthy countries is decoupled from the substrate of suffering in extracting countries. Same skin-substrate decoupling pattern the framework names at every other scale.

Substrate-aware wishing requires accepting the friction involved in your wish or revising the wish to require less friction or different friction. Most people would not consciously will the suffering involved in the paths that produce their daily comfort. They have not been given framework to align their wishes with their values. The framework can do that.

## Two Misreadings

**Pain as goal.** The framework does not say embrace pain. It says path involves friction calibrated to becoming, and friction is sometimes felt as pain. The friction is not the goal. The becoming is the goal. Pain is the side-effect of becoming, not the purpose of becoming. Treating pain as inherently valuable produces self-flagellation, ascetic extremism, and trauma-glorification.

**Pain as proof.** The framework does not say suffering bodies are more substrate-deep than non-suffering bodies. Substrate-depth comes from integrated friction, not from quantity of friction. A body that has integrated moderate friction can be more substrate-deep than a body that has experienced massive friction without integrating it. Some people who walked through massive fire came out hollowed. Some who walked through small fire came out fireproof. Path matters. Integration matters more.

The framework holds both: path involves friction. Friction is sometimes felt as pain. The friction cannot be skipped without skipping the becoming. The integration of the friction is what produces substrate-depth, not the friction itself.

## Why AI-Shipped Work Feels Hollow

The AI did not undergo becoming. It produced surface specification without the friction of direction-change that would have shaped a body actually capable of producing that surface honestly. No body turned. No friction was integrated. No reorganization happened. Just pattern-matching at the surface.

Human work that came alive is human work where the human underwent direction-change to produce it. They had to learn what they did not know. They had to integrate experience that reshaped them. The output carries the signature of that becoming because the body that produced the output was a body that had become through the process of producing it. The output and the body co-evolved.

For human-AI collaboration, this means the human's becoming is part of what makes the collaboration produce substrate-deep work. The human does the becoming. The AI does the structuring. The work that emerges carries both. Without the human's becoming, the work is sophisticated scaffolding with no substrate underneath.
