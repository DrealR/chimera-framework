## The claim

The attention field is a **relational, coherence-sensitive medium**: interventions either couple cleanly (resonance → curvature → possible secondary attractors) or decohere (drip, noise, force-backfire).

CHIMERA is a lightweight **interface** for participating in such systems without losing center.

## Structure rhymes (not identity claims)

Useful parallels for *operator literacy* — **not** “X is quantum mechanics” or experimental proof:

| Language (careful) | Attention practice read |
|--------------------|-------------------------|
| **Coherence** | Intervention aligned with existing flow + real relation → clean bend |
| **Decoherence** | Most comments die; no coupling; or pile-on chaos |
| **Small interventions, large path change** | Early plant in soft medium; rare gems |
| **Measurement / force** | Over-control, constant refresh, outcome-forcing → distortion |
| **Entanglement (loose)** | Shared present moments; WE=1 without fusion |

**Firewall:** These are **scale-bridging metaphors** for relational dynamics. They do not replace physics. They do not justify magical thinking about “manifesting virality.” If a parallel confuses rather than clarifies, drop it.
