## 1. Can't be manufactured (it was never the place)

**Keeper:** engineering a third place (optimizing it for engagement/money) kills it — same as manufactured authenticity. The sharp line: **people aren't NPCs until you treat them like NPCs — they don't become NPCs until you don't talk to them.** The eye-lock (`the-eye-lock-mutual-recognition`) made daily: the driver, the neighbor, the regular become real-as-someones the moment the gaze goes both ways. **A third place is the *practice* of mutual recognition in the rooms you're already in** — you can't make one, you can only stop killing the mutual gaze and make room for it. (Why the automation-of-everything — apps, no-contact delivery — is the threat: it eliminates the rooms where the gaze could go both ways.)

## 2. The WE=1 firewall: connection, not merger

**Keeper:** the capacity to feel-*together* is real and beautiful — the eye-lock at species scale, the seed of WE = 1. **Firewall:** it can tip toward the **hive-mind / shared-consciousness inflation.** The grounded version is *we feel WITH each other — connection between separate someones, WE = 1 as connection NOT merger.* The hive-mind erases the *other*, so there's no gaze to return; **merger destroys the mutual recognition it mimics.** The eye-lock needs *two poles* — staying separate and reaching across is *more* beautiful than merging. (People aren't evil — they get lost on paths and become their actions, but the pull is toward connection; `judge-actions-not-essences`.)

## 3. The full answer: two moves

**Keeper:** a third place takes (1) **make the room** — the mutual gaze, turn NPCs into someones — AND (2) **keep it un-performed** — be yourself so they can be themselves (`being-yourself-is-the-gift`). It's not just where you *see* people; it's where you can be *unperformed* with them. **Home is wherever you're both seen and unhidden** — mutual recognition + mutual un-performance = the real room.
