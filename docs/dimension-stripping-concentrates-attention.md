# Dimension-Stripping: Constrained Input Concentrates Attention

> **Origin:** Walked through blindfolded guitar, no-UI Call of Duty, eyes-off dribbling, and the
> Daredevil video (June 2026). A clean training principle — remove a channel to flood the rest — and
> it's how skill acquisition actually works, not mysticism. Firewalls applied in writing.
>
> **Date:** June 2026

---

## The principle

**Remove a dimension — an input channel — to free attention for another.** Constrained input
**concentrates attention,** and concentrated attention is what either *trains a sense* or *drops a
skill into the cooled rail* (flow). It is how skill acquisition works:

- **Eyes off the ball (dribbling):** the visual channel that was spending effort tracking the ball is
  freed to read the court; the dribbling drops into the rail where it runs without conscious attention.
- **Blindfold the guitar:** kill the visual channel and attention floods into touch — the *relational
  feel* of where the notes are, and the sound itself.
- **Strip the game's UI and audio:** the body handles the game on autopilot while attention runs your
  actual thinking underneath.

The constraint doesn't subtract — it *redirects.* Whatever channel you close, the attention it was
spending floods the channels that remain, and that flood is what trains them.

---

## The gravity chamber

Train in **harder physics** — fewer channels — and normal physics feels easy. The constraint *is* the
load: closing a channel forces the remaining ones to carry more, which is exactly what develops them.
This is [Move 2 under load](write-access-to-your-own-attention.md) with the load manufactured by the
constraint — you walk the new thread while a deliberately removed channel keeps the difficulty high.

---

## Daredevil — the cleanest costume

He lost sight, **let everything in,** found equilibrium, and *then* could hear which heartbeat
mattered. Losing the dominant channel didn't grant a power — it forced attention to learn what to
**select.** That is the same selection that runs through the strum, sparse-attention architectures, and
[the direction axis](the-direction-axis.md): finite attention learning to pick the load-bearing signal
out of the flood. *Let everything in, find equilibrium, then hear which heartbeat actually matters.*
That is the training — ordinary, real, no totem required.

---

## Dimensional-access clarification

The July dimensional-ascension pass widened the word "dimension." A dimension can be a spatial axis, but it can also be a perceptual/relational channel. Dimension-stripping is therefore the training inverse of dimensional expansion: close one channel, force attention to develop another, then return to the full field with more usable access.

**Keeper:** higher access is not more input by default. Sometimes the next dimension opens because one noisy dimension gets quiet.
