## The biological carrier band

Living systems appear to favor certain tempos for integration. The conversation centered on a broad 0.5-4 Hz band, with special attention around 2 Hz: roughly one event every half-second.

That rhythm appears near:

- walking cadence;
- speech/prosody timing;
- 120 BPM music;
- neural integration and reset windows;
- call-and-response timing in social bodies.

Framework reading: **the body may have a carrier band where signal, Pause, and integration travel with less friction.**

---

## Rhythm as low-level operator support

The operator is not only abstract thought. It rides the body.

Breath, gait, speech pacing, music, and shared rhythm can make Pause easier or harder. A stable rhythm gives the nervous system a predictable frame in which attention can settle, relation can synchronize, and the next choice can be made without panic.

This gives CHIMERA a practical lever:

> To help the operator, tune the body's rhythm before demanding higher-order insight.

---

## Resonance is not validation

A rhythm can make a message easier to receive without making it true.

This is why the Firewall belongs inside the resonance doc. Propaganda, cults, advertising, and extraction systems can all use rhythm. Resonance is a transmission condition, not a truth test.

---

## LLM caveat

LLMs do not share this biological carrier band. Their rhythms are context windows, token streams, compute budgets, and sampling loops. They can simulate human pacing, but they do not participate in the same embodied reset window.

That distinction matters when asking AI to support human attention: the machine can help structure rhythm, but the human body still has to integrate it.
