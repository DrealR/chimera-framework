## Synthetic dimension

A synthetic dimension is an engineered degree of freedom. The system is not necessarily discovering a new physical place; it is wired so behavior propagates as if another direction exists.

That distinction is the whole lesson.

Framework translation:

> A body can gain new effective directions when its connectivity changes.

The operator does not need a new universe to create a new path. It can rewire attention, relation, tools, spaces, and habits so that a formerly unavailable move becomes reachable.

---

## Reach is authored by structure

When the structure changes, the legal moves change.

A person with a guitar tuned differently has different reachable shapes. A community with a shared protocol has different coordination moves. A child with a safe teacher has a different learning dimension. An AI agent with tools has more reach than a chat box.

The new dimension is not fantasy. It is a real effect of changed connectivity.

## Topological protection as firewall shape

Topological protection means local defects do not easily destroy the path because the global structure holds.

CHIMERA translation: a well-built firewall is not a brittle rule. It is a structure whose direction survives local noise.

A value that only works when conditions are perfect is not protected. A value with topological protection can bend around local defects and remain coherent.

## Firewall

- **Do not claim the experiment proves extra cosmic dimensions.** Synthetic dimensions are engineered effective directions.
- **Do not make metaphor do physics.** The physics is an example; the framework mechanism is connectivity-authoring-reach.
- **Do not confuse reach with direction.** More dimensions give more possible moves; they do not decide whether the operator faces outward.
