# Attention Capture Types

> **Origin:** July 2026 — live examples (X vs Threads observation, rage-bait mega-V, niche tech drama, Sam Altman secondary attractor); raw views ≠ strong field effect; positioned vs random virality; big-body activation.
>
> **Date:** July 2026
>
> **Status:** graduated taxonomy for field literacy + plant soil choice

---

## The claim

High visibility comes in **different species**. Raw views do not equal meaningful curvature, healthy branches, or good soil for planting.

> Some posts explode as **spread without relation**. Others create **real secondary attractors**. Strategy is not “get V” — it is **read the capture type** before you enter the local present.

---

## Type table

| Type | Shape | Branch quality | Plant soil? |
|------|-------|----------------|-------------|
| **Rage / moral signaling** | High V, fast emotional charge; often amplify-heavy; performative replies | Weak or toxic | Usually **pass** or light observe only |
| **Platform / field observation** | Reports on attention itself (e.g. X vs Threads metrics) | Moderate; meta-discussion | Good for *mapping*; plant only if you add real insight |
| **Niche technical / cultural drama** | Strong in a semantic territory; lower absolute V | Can be higher quality | **Better** for positioned planting if you have resonance |
| **Big-body activated branch** | Comment gains gravity when a high-mass node replies | High secondary attractor odds | High leverage *if* early + real tension (not bait) |
| **Healthy discussion / healthy_balance** | V enough + C and R alive without pile-on | Strong | Prefer for genuine plants |
| **Hollow mega-reach** | Huge V, collapsed L/R/C | Dead soil | Pass |

Cross-read with metric chords: [Attention Metric Signatures](attention-metric-signatures.md), rage mechanics: [Rage Bait and Attention Harvest](rage-bait-and-attention-harvest.md).

## Case study pattern language (2026-07 examples)

| Example class | Capture type | Lesson |
|---------------|--------------|--------|
| X vs Threads comparison post | Platform observation | Traction from existing platform war current; reports field, doesn’t create new curvature alone |
| Small account + legal-age outrage mega-V | Rage / moral signaling | 20M+ V can be **low-quality spread**; weak branches; algorithm loves emotional velocity |
| Niche Elon/Sam drama post | Niche territory | Smaller V, denser local current |
| Comment that gets Sam Altman reply | **Big-body activation** | Branch becomes secondary attractor; tension + high-mass engagement; feedback loop into trunk |

### Big-body activation (special case)

Fastest path for a comment to become a secondary attractor: a **high-gravity account** publicly engages the branch.

| Factor | Role |
|--------|------|
| Sharp claim / real tension | Creates curvature (Holy Man / clean bend) |
| Big-body reply | Instant legitimacy + distribution |
| Feedback loop | Eyes enter via branch *and* trunk |

**Honesty:** Most such branches only look “special” *after* activation. Early plant on high-stakes drama trunks is a bet that activation *might* happen — not a guarantee. Without the big body, many tension-comments stay drips.

**Do not** only chase celebrity threads. Prefer: resonance + soft medium + optional big-body density. Pure whale-hunting is another forced game.

## Positioned planting vs random virality

| Mode | Controllability | Typical outcome |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Positioned plant** | High (choose soil, timing, resonance) | Consistent smaller traction; literacy compounds |
| **Original post hoping for explosion** | Low | Occasional lottery (rage/luck); hard to reverse-engineer |
| **Rage manufacturing** | False control | Hollow V, toxic branches, O>I failure |

Accept algorithmic randomness as weather. Influence what you can: **where** you enter, **what** you add, **when** you leave.

## Quick study method (any viral example)

| Step | Question |
|------|----------|
| 1 | Capture type? (table above) |
| 2 | Signature chord? (amplify_wave, discussion, hollow, pile_on…) |
| 3 | Branch vitality? Top-heavy drip vs secondary nodes |
| 4 | Big-body activation present? |
| 5 | Would I plant here? Why / why not (resonance, not V) |
| 6 | Felt: clean bend possible or only noise? |
