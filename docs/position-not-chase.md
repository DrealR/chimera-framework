# Position, Not Chase

> **Origin:** July 2026 conversation formalizing "don't chase, position yourself" as pole/field mechanics rather than generic advice.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## Chasing

Chasing is reactive motion inside another pole's field.

The chaser spends energy trying to close distance to a body that has not necessarily chosen relation. Attention leaks outward without stable offering. The chaser becomes shaped by the pursued object's gravity.

Chasing can look active, but structurally it is often low-agency: the other pole sets the terms.

---

## Positioning

Positioning is becoming a coherent pole.

Instead of spending all force crossing distance, the body builds value, clarity, rhythm, and boundary until compatible bodies can recognize and approach. Positioning is not passivity. It is field generation.

In CHIMERA terms:

- chasing often tilts toward `I > O`: *I need this body to complete my field*;
- positioning tilts toward `O > I`: *I become a body that can offer real value without forcing relation*.

---

## Money follows value, not grasping

The same shape applies to work and money. Chasing money directly often traps the body inside other people's fields. Creating value and positioning it clearly lets money follow as a consequence of real output.

This does not mean money magically arrives. It means the clean path is to become a legible source of value, not a body contorted around hunger.

---

## Firewall

- **Positioning is not entitlement.** Becoming a pole does not force anyone to orbit.
- **Positioning is not avoidance.** Sometimes a direct ask is clean. The difference is whether the ask comes from stable offering or grasping dependency.
- **Do not romanticize inaction.** Fields are generated by work, boundary, visibility, and consistency.
