# Boundary-Override Capitalism

*A framework study. Graduated from the 2026-06-30 Pump Fun Go / bounty-economy capture in core. Names the extraction pattern where platforms and market positions pay bodies to lower, perforate, or sell the membrane that normally protects self-governance. Internal register.*

## 0. The claim, stated once

**A healthy body governs what crosses its boundary. Boundary-override capitalism prices that boundary and calls the sale agency. It is the market form that pays bodies to do what their own membrane would normally refuse: humiliation, harassment, bodily risk, coerced kindness, reputation damage, or public debasement.**

---

## 1. The mechanism

The attention economy first harvested attention. Surveillance capitalism harvested data. Platform labor harvested task-time. The bounty economy moves one layer deeper: it bids on the body's refusal point.

```
membrane says no
    ↓
payout applies pressure
    ↓
membrane lowers
    ↓
body performs boundary-crossing event
    ↓
platform converts the crossing into attention
```

The critical object is not the task. The critical object is the override. The market is not merely buying action; it is buying the visible proof that a boundary can be crossed for a price.

## 2. Why "agency" is not enough

Consent matters, but it does not exhaust the scan. A person can choose under pressure. A system can preserve legal consent while still organizing the conditions that make refusal expensive.

CHIMERA reads the whole body:

- Who sets the rhythm?
- Who names the price?
- Who receives the compounding benefit?
- Who absorbs the dignity debt?
- Who becomes more free after the exchange?
- Who becomes more programmable?

If the sponsor gains leverage while the performer loses membrane integrity, the body is sick even when the transaction is voluntary at the surface.

## 3. Conduct vs harvest

Conduct strengthens membranes. It gives a body more capacity to govern itself after the exchange. Harvest weakens membranes. It teaches the body that its own no is merely a price discovery problem.

That is the difference between paying someone for skilled work and paying someone to degrade themselves. Both involve money. Only one builds substrate.

## 4. The system-level danger

Boundary-override markets train culture to see every membrane as liquidity:

- shame threshold as ad inventory;
- public reputation as yield;
- vulnerability as proof-of-work;
- kindness as a brand surface;
- protest as campaign asset;
- belief as market signal;
- bodies as remote-controlled limbs.

A society that normalizes this does not only harm participants. It teaches observers that personhood itself is negotiable under the right payout.

## 5. Firewall

Do not solve this by removing agency from poor or desperate people. The performer is not an object and not a child by default. The correction is structural: reduce desperation, stigmatize dignity extraction, forbid third-party harm, require consent from visible subjects, separate care from speculative promotion, and build boring proof systems that do not need humiliation to verify value.
