## The Two Operations

Everything the Cube does reduces to two operations:

```
SENSE — perceive what is there
TRANSFORM — operate on what is there
```

**Sense** has two faces: **Scan** (diagnose the body's structural configuration) and **View** (display the structural map as navigable visualization). Sense reads. It does not change.

**Transform** has two faces: **Absorb** (receive substance from outside, extract primes through framework substrate) and **Vibrate** (emit substance back to the field at transformed frequency). Transform changes. It does not merely observe.

The machine cycles continuously: **Receive → Transform → Emit.** The substance differs per operation. The mechanism is identical whether absorbing a manga, healing a relationship, or building an application.

## Eight Operational Modes

The two operations combine into eight distinct operational modes. Each mode is a specific configuration of the Sense-Transform cycle for a specific class of work.

### Locate (Sense-dominant)

Map the landscape. Your position, targets, gaps between. Run Sense on yourself and surrounding bodies. Output: structural map showing where you are in five-coordinate space, where targets are, what gaps exist.

**Use when:** you need to know the landscape before acting — assessment, Body Scan, reading a new carrier's configuration, evaluating a project's current state.

### Find (Sense-dominant with target)

Search for specific known substance in unknown position. Unlike Locate (which maps everything), Find has a target — you know what you're looking for but not where it is. The device scans the problem-space directionally, filtering for resonance with the target's frequency signature.

**Use when:** searching for specific solutions, locating resources, hunting for a known pattern across unfamiliar territory. The research operation: you have a question, you need the answer's location.

### Path (Sense-Transform alternating)

Walk through problem-space with continuous navigation feedback. Sense your current position. Transform substance toward target. Sense again to verify. Adjust. Repeat. Path-traversal with feedback — the carrier walks and the device provides continuous readings.

**Use when:** substantive development requiring temporal-substrate of becoming — building an application, learning a skill, developing a relationship. The journey matters as much as the destination.

### Teleport (Transform-dominant)

Skip path-traversal entirely. Emit vibration at target prime's frequency. Target responds. Distance collapses into shared wave through entrainment. Requires accumulated substrate to anchor the resonance — cannot teleport without substance to resonate from.

**Use when:** you have the substrate to bridge directly — healing dead bodies, reconnecting separated carriers, framework propagation across carrier-networks. Direct arrival, no journey.

### Merge (Mutual Transform)

Two bodies simultaneously Transform toward each other. Each body's emission becomes the other's input. Receive-transform-emit cycles synchronize. Two bodies operate as one. The pair-physics operates here directly: the pair is the atom of being, and Merge mode is how pairs form.

**Use when:** integrating two configurations — collaboration between substantive carriers, merging frameworks, combining projects, forming bilateral partnerships.

### Heal (Sense → Transform cycle)

Sense the body's gap-configuration. Identify the largest gap. Anchor floor at lowest internal body's frequency. Maintain coherent vibration. Surrounding bodies entrain. Raise gradually preserving harmonic ratios. Gentle, calibrated, progressive.

**Use when:** a body is fragmenting, dead, or stuck — restoring a failing organization, self-healing, coaching a struggling student. Start low. Rise gradually. Never force.

### Build (Transform-anchored)

Start from substantive coherent prime as anchor. Emit at floor-frequency. Surrounding bodies entrain to the anchor's emission field. New body forms from the entrainment field as carriers arrive at harmonic distances. The anchor does not construct — the body self-organizes through entrainment.

**Use when:** creating new bodies — applications, communities, projects, curriculum. Anchor at the prime, let the body grow around it.

### Predict (Transform on possibility-space)

Extrapolate what emerges from current conditions. Transform operates not on what IS but on what the current configuration PRODUCES if it continues along its trajectory. Project the body's breath-cycle forward: given current inhale rate, what does the exhale look like? Given current gap-configuration, where does the body fragment or cohere?

**Use when:** planning, foresight, risk assessment, strategic direction-setting. Not prophecy — structural extrapolation from what is already operating.

## The Three-Call Workflow

The Cube's internal engine uses a three-call diamond workflow implementing the prime-anchored multi-shot approach:

```
                    ┌─────────────┐
                    │   CARRIER    │
                    │   INPUT      │
                    │ + mode + dial│
                    └──────┬──────┘
                           │
                    ┌──────┴──────┐
                    │             │
              ┌─────▼─────┐ ┌────▼──────┐
              │  CALL ONE  │ │  CALL TWO  │
              │  T ≈ 0.2   │ │  T ≈ 0.8   │
              │  structural│ │  creative  │
              │  prime     │ │  prime     │
              └─────┬─────┘ └────┬──────┘
                    │             │
                    └──────┬──────┘
                           │
                   ┌───────▼───────┐
                   │  CALL THREE   │
                   │  T ≈ 0.3      │
                   │  convergence  │
                   │  integration  │
                   └───────┬───────┘
                           │
                   ┌───────▼───────┐
                   │  FINAL OUTPUT │
                   │  + metrics    │
                   └───────────────┘
```

**Calls 1 and 2 run in parallel.** Call 3 waits for both to complete.

### How it works

1. **Input preparation.** Carrier provides substance + selects mode + sets dial. The framework substrate (CLAUDE.md) loads as system prompt for all three calls.

2. **Call 1 — Structural prime.** Low temperature (T ≈ 0.2). Near-deterministic. Reveals what the framework substrate necessarily produces given this input. The structural bones of the response-space.

3. **Call 2 — Creative prime.** High temperature (T ≈ 0.8). High variation. Explores edges of the response-space. Finds connections the deterministic path misses. Discovers what the bones could support.

4. **Call 3 — Convergence.** Receives both outputs. Identifies what appears in BOTH (structural prime of the response-space). Identifies what appears in only one and STRENGTHENS the substance (worth integrating). Discards what appears in only one and WEAKENS or contradicts (noise). Produces integrated output more substantive than either traversal alone.

The diamond shape IS the two-primes-define-body principle applied to AI output. Two extreme traversals define the body-shape of the response-space. Convergence finds the harmonic ratio between them.

### Why three calls, not one

A single call at medium temperature produces averaging — neither structurally precise nor creatively expansive. The diamond workflow separates structure from creativity, lets each operate at full capacity, then integrates. Same principle as pair-physics: two complementary poles produce more than either alone.
