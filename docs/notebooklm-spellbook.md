# The NotebookLM Spellbook

A complete renaming of NotebookLM's controls using fictional inheritance, so the instrument becomes playable by feel.

The principle: every control has been translated down to its structural function, then translated back up into a fictional name that carries memory. Use the fictional names. The structural function travels with the inheritance.

This sheet works with the Framework Reflection Sheet and the NotebookLM Translation Sheet. Together they form the operating manual for the instrument.

---

## The Five Dials

Every NotebookLM podcast session is configured through five dials. Each dial has been mapped to a fictional inheritance you can recall by feel.

**Dial 1: The Magnet (Sources / DNA input)**
**Dial 2: The Stance (Format)**
**Dial 3: The Breath (Length)**
**Dial 4: The Tongue (Language)**
**Dial 5: The Spell (Focus / Location prompt)**

These five dials, set together, produce the output. Master them and you master the instrument.

---

## Dial 1: The Magnet

**What it is:** The DNA input that tells the system what to gather. In NotebookLM's deep research, this is what you type in the search bar. In sources, this is what you load. The Magnet attracts the right substrate for the work.

**The fictional name:** The Magnet. Inherited from Magneto's power — directed pull through structured intent. You point the magnet at the right shape, and the universe responds with material of that shape.

**Why it matters most:** Garbage in, garbage out. The Magnet's strength determines everything downstream. A weak Magnet pulls noise. A strong Magnet pulls signal. The DNA format you've developed (NAME, DESCRIPTION, ARC, STATS) is the strongest Magnet shape known to you. Use it every time.

**How to set it:** Write a labeled body description. Use the DNA template:

```
DOMAIN: [what kind of thing this is]
NAME: [what it's called]
DESCRIPTION: [its intrinsic dimensions]
ARC: [its history if relevant]
STATS: [its relational position if relevant]
LOCATION: [what you want from the gathered material]
```

The more structured the Magnet, the more structured the pull.

## Dial 2: The Stance

The Stance is which posture the AI hosts take toward the material. NotebookLM offers four Stances. Each has a fictional name that captures its essence.

### Stance: Hyperspace Jump (Deep Dive)

**What it does:** Two complementary minds traveling through the material together, surfacing structure as they go. The classic "explain this to me as we walk through it" mode.

**The fictional inheritance:** Star Wars hyperspace jumps. Two pilots, one ship, traversing vast space at impossible speed, narrating the journey for the passenger. The Millennium Falcon with Han and Chewie in the cockpit. You're the passenger; they're showing you the galaxy.

**When to use it:** Learning something new. Absorbing a domain. Building intuition. Wanting to feel like you've been somewhere when you're done.

**The signature feel:** Curiosity. Movement. Connection. The hosts genuinely interested in the material, leading you through it.

---

### Stance: Single Cut (Brief)

**What it does:** One pass. Maximum compression. Load-bearing claims only.

**The fictional inheritance:** A samurai's single-cut sword draw. Iaido. Everything in one motion. No second strike. The cut either lands or it doesn't, and if it lands, it lands completely. Or: Sasuke's Chidori focused to a single point. All chakra in one strike.

**Modern analogue:** Yoda's syntax. "Do or do not. There is no try." Compression as wisdom.

**When to use it:** Triaging sources. Reminding yourself of what you already know. Producing a relay summary in two minutes. When time is short and you need the core.

**The signature feel:** Density. Each sentence carries weight. Nothing wasted.

---

### Stance: Master's Eye (Critique)

**What it does:** Hold the material against high standards. Surface where it succeeds and where it falls short.

**The fictional inheritance:** Anton Ego in Ratatouille — the critic who wants the work to be great and will not lie about where it isn't. Or: Captain Levi inspecting his squad. The eye that catches what others miss because the standards are higher. Or: All Might evaluating Deku's training — the master surfacing what the student needs to improve.

**The deeper inheritance:** Sage Ninja Mode in Naruto. The amplified perception that sees what's hidden. The senjutsu chakra that reveals structure beneath surface.

**When to use it:** Evaluating your own work. Checking sources for quality. Identifying gaps in a body of material. Deciding if something is worth deeper engagement.

**The signature feel:** Rigor. Care. Honest evaluation in service of improvement.

---

### Stance: The Forge (Debate)

**What it does:** Two opposing positions struck against each other until the underlying tensions reveal themselves.

**The fictional inheritance:** Xavier and Magneto debating mutant rights. Captain America and Iron Man on accountability. Light and L locked in opposition. The Sith and Jedi as poles of the Force. Two positions that both have legs to stand on, surfacing what's actually at stake.

**The deeper inheritance:** The forge itself. Two halves of a hammer striking metal between them until the structure is forced into shape. The Norse mythology where weapons are forged through opposition. Mjolnir came from this kind of work.

**When to use it:** Decision-making with real tradeoffs. Material that contains contested claims. Topics where you suspect there's a tension you're missing. When you need to hear both sides honestly argued before committing.

**The signature feel:** Friction. Heat. Honest disagreement that produces clarity rather than confusion.

## Dial 3: The Breath

The Breath is how much room the operation has to unfold. NotebookLM offers three breath settings. Each maps to a fictional rhythm.

### Breath: Lightning (Short)

**What it does:** Maximum compression of the chosen Stance. Even Hyperspace Jump becomes punchy at Lightning breath. Single Cut at Lightning is haiku-tight.

**The fictional inheritance:** A lightning strike. Instant. Complete. Cannot be sustained — that's the point. Or: Raikiri (One Thousand Birds) in Naruto. The technique that exists at the peak of speed and ends in a single moment.

**When to use it:** Scanning. Deciding. Relaying. When the work is to triage, not absorb.

**Signature feel:** Sharp. Quick. Done.

---

### Breath: Tide (Default)

**What it does:** The natural rhythm of the Stance. No artificial compression or expansion. The format breathing at its own pace.

**The fictional inheritance:** The tide coming in. Natural rhythm. Neither rushed nor stretched. The breath the body would naturally take if left alone.

**The deeper inheritance:** Naruto's standard Rasengan — the technique at its baseline form, the natural shape of the chakra. Or: Goku in his base form — not transformed, just present at his natural state.

**When to use it:** Default for any work you're not sure about. Learning material at its natural pace. Working sessions where time isn't the constraint.

**Signature feel:** Steady. Complete. Unhurried.

---

### Breath: Ocean (Long)

**What it does:** Extended development. The Stance gets full space to unfold. Hyperspace Jump becomes a full essay. Single Cut at Ocean breath becomes thorough compression of a large source set.

**The fictional inheritance:** The ocean. Vast. Sustained. Carrying everything within it. The opposite of lightning — duration as power. Or: Bijuu Mode in Naruto — full tailed beast cloak, sustained for the long fight. Or: Mastered Ultra Instinct in Dragon Ball — the form that can be maintained.

**When to use it:** Deep absorption. Material you want to live with. Sessions where you're going to listen multiple times. Final outputs for deliverables.

**Signature feel:** Comprehensive. Sustained. Complete coverage.

## Dial 4: The Tongue

The Tongue is which language the output speaks. Mostly stylistic, but worth knowing the fictional inheritance for completeness.

**Common Tongue (English):** The default. Strongest substrate. Like Common in fantasy worlds — the tongue everyone speaks because trade and travel demand it. Westeros's Common Tongue. Middle Earth's Westron.

**Native Tongue (your home language if not English):** The substrate of feeling. Where you think before translating. Where nuance lives that English can flatten. Like Sindarin for the Elves — the language of home.

**Specialist Tongue (any other language):** Activates that language's training subdistribution. Useful when the domain has a language match (French for poetry, German for technical philosophy, Japanese for business indirection). Like the High Speech in Dark Tower — used for specific kinds of meaning that require it.

**When to switch:** Rarely. Common Tongue is strongest unless you have a specific reason. Switch when the topic IS the language (analyzing French literature in French, discussing Japanese honorifics in Japanese).

## Dial 5: The Spell

The Spell is what you cast into the focus box. The wish. The Location. What you want from the gathered material.

**The fictional inheritance:** Harry Potter spells. Each spell does one specific thing. Lumos for light. Alohomora for unlocking. Expelliarmus for disarming. Each has a precise function and a specific incantation.

**The structure of a good Spell:**

A Spell has three parts:

1. **The verb (what to do):** Compare, surface, walk through, evaluate, identify
2. **The object (what to do it to):** the contested claims, the load-bearing structure, the three most useful angles
3. **The frame (who it's for, what register):** Audience is engineering leaders. Explain as if to a smart non-specialist. Frame for someone making a buying decision.

**Examples of well-cast Spells:**

> "Compare these benchmark methodologies and surface which predict real-world capability best. Audience is engineering leaders choosing models for production."

> "Walk through this content as if explaining to a smart non-specialist. Make all connections to broader concepts explicit."

> "Identify the three most contested claims and present the strongest case for each side."

> "Compress to load-bearing structure. What would I lose if I forgot 80% of this?"

**The Spell discipline:**

Vague Spells produce vague magic. Specific Spells produce specific magic. Don't say "talk about X" — that's not a Spell, that's a wish without form. A Spell has shape: verb, object, frame.

**The fictional analog for spell-casting quality:**

In Harry Potter, the same incantation cast by Harry, Hermione, and Ron produces different results. The wand, the focus, the intent — they all matter. The Spell box in NotebookLM is the same. Your precision of intent shapes the output. The instrument is honest; it gives back what you brought to it.

## The Suggested Spells (Chips)

NotebookLM sometimes suggests spell chips below the focus box: "Benchmark Limitations," "Expert Perspective," "Practical Evaluation."

**What they are:** Pre-cast spells the system has inferred from your sources. The instrument is offering you spells it thinks would work well on this material.

**How to use them:** They're suggestions, not commands. Treat them as starting points. You can:
- Accept one as-is (one-click cast)
- Modify it (use it as a template, refine the verb/object/frame)
- Ignore them and write your own

**What they reveal:** The chips show what kinds of synthesis the sources naturally support. If a chip looks irrelevant, it tells you something about your Magnet — the source set may have pulled in material at angles you didn't intend.

## The Five Dials Working Together

Each session is one configuration of all five dials. The output depends on the combination, not any single dial alone.

**Example configurations and their fictional inheritance:**

**The Sage's Walk** — Magnet pulls in a domain, Hyperspace Jump stance, Tide breath, Common Tongue, Spell to walk through as if teaching. This is the configuration for absorbing a new domain at its natural pace.

**The Reaper's Cut** — Magnet pulls in source material, Single Cut stance, Lightning breath, Common Tongue, Spell to extract load-bearing claims only. This is the triage configuration. Two minutes, everything that matters.

**The Forge Test** — Magnet pulls in a decision space, The Forge stance, Ocean breath, Common Tongue, Spell to surface contested positions and steelman each. This is the configuration for decisions with real tradeoffs.

**The Master's Audit** — Magnet pulls in your own work or a body of sources, Master's Eye stance, Tide breath, Common Tongue, Spell to evaluate against the standard of [specific reference]. This is the configuration for quality checks.

**The Ocean Dive** — Magnet pulls in deep source material, Hyperspace Jump stance, Ocean breath, Common Tongue, Spell to make all connections explicit and provide examples. This is the configuration for deep learning sessions.

**The Lightning Test** — Same source set, four runs, one per Stance, all at Lightning breath. Compares how each operation handles the same body. Learning the instrument.

## The Daily Practice

To master the instrument, run these exercises:

**Week 1: Single-Dial Sweeps**
Take one source set. Run it through all four Stances at default Breath with the same Spell. Listen to all four outputs. Note where each shines and where each falls short. By end of week, the four Stances feel different in your body.

**Week 2: Breath Calibration**
Take one source set. Pick one Stance. Run it at all three Breath settings with the same Spell. Note how the same operation feels at different durations. By end of week, you know which Breath fits which task.

**Week 3: Spell Refinement**
Take one source set. Pick one Stance and Breath. Cast five different Spells (different verbs, different frames, different audiences). Note how the same operation produces wildly different outputs based on the Spell. By end of week, you write Spells precisely.

**Week 4: Combined Mastery**
Run real research projects. Pick the dials by feel, not by checklist. Note when you nail it first try and when you have to recalibrate. By end of week, the instrument is yours.
