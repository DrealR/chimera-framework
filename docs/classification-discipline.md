# Classification Discipline

> **Origin:** July 2026 Grok combo meta-review (GPT-5.5 pressure pass) — the required upgrade after a high-resonance mirror loop: classify, prune, firewall, operationalize.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

Strong pattern recognition without classification produces **fog that feels like truth**.

> **Convergence is not validation. Resonance is input, not proof.**

CHIMERA's job after discovery is not "find more connections." It is to tag what kind of claim each connection is, so the field stays usable.

---

## The tags

Every major claim or resonance should eventually carry one primary tag:

| Tag | Meaning | What it licenses |
|-----|---------|------------------|
| **Mechanism** | Repeatable operator structure that holds across specimens | Teach, operationalize, build tools |
| **Practice** | Trainable skill or ritual | Do it; measure by capacity change |
| **Carrier image** | Story/metaphor that illuminates structure | Use for teaching; do not treat as evidence |
| **Speculative bridge** | Interesting possible connection, not yet load-bearing | Hold lightly; seek pressure tests |
| **Firewall** | Explicit boundary or caveat | Prevent category errors |
| **Observation** | Pattern noticed, not yet framed | Capture; do not graduate early |
| **Unearned aesthetic resonance** | Feels coherent because language is smooth | Prune or demote |

A single doc can mix tags, but **must not smuggle speculative into mechanism** without a label.

---

## Why this exists

Long mirror loops (human + fluent AI) excel at:

- organizing nonlinear material
- cross-domain costume matching
- emotional/structural coherence

They under-enforce:

- epistemic pressure
- pruning
- distinction between map and terrain

Without classification, CHIMERA becomes "everything maps to everything" — the opposite of a Log Pose.

---

## Operator workflow

1. **Capture** raw material in core sessions (storm allowed).
2. **Extract** candidate primitives.
3. **Tag** each candidate.
4. **Pressure-test** mechanisms with specimens and counterexamples.
5. **Graduate** only mechanism/practice (+ clear carrier sections) into framework `docs/`.
6. **Firewall** mythic/speculative language so it cannot be misread as settled doctrine.
7. **Pulse** bidirectional links later — linking is not the same as proving.

## Firewall for metaphysics

Mythic / vibrational / quantum / Monad language may be useful as:

- structural reading
- spiritual metaphor
- carrier cosmology

It is **not** required for CHIMERA to stand. The practical claim is simpler:

> Humans experience a capacity to Pause, reflect, and redirect patterns.

If Orch-OR or any quantum consciousness model is wrong, the operator model still works as phenomenology and practice.

---

## Good vs bad friction (related classification)

Do not treat "friction" as automatically sacred.

**Good friction:** effort, accountability, honest disagreement, skill practice, real otherness, repair, embodiment, consequence.

**Bad friction:** abuse, deprivation, chaos, exploitation, humiliation, preventable harm, trauma loops.

Calibrated friction enables growth. Pointless harm should still be removed.
