# Narrative Substrate and Story Mode

> **A framework names relationships. A story makes a body live through them.**

Story is not decoration added after CHIMERA has finished its real work. It is one of the bodies through which the framework can teach, test, transmit, and revise itself.

CHIMERA uses **narrative substrate** to turn abstract relations into inhabitable consequence. It uses **Story Mode** to place bounded operators inside a declared world, let them move from partial knowledge, and inspect what the resulting collisions reveal.

This makes fiction a carrier, a pressure chamber, and a shared-world seed. It does **not** make fiction empirical proof.

## The distinction

The explicit framework says:

```text
Here are concepts.
Here are relations.
Here are principles and legal moves.
```

A narrative body says:

```text
Here is a person who must live through them.
```

The framework can state that power should remain answerable to the bodies it affects. A story can make a reader inhabit the family displaced by an apparently optimal policy, the operator who must choose whether to enforce it, and the system that can calculate the cost without being able to own its meaning.

The proposition becomes consequence.

## What narrative substrate means

A narrative substrate is the structured relational material from which many stories, interpretations, performances, or simulations can grow.

It contains:

```text
primes
+ bodies
+ membranes
+ resources
+ rhythms
+ mirror systems
+ histories
+ frictions
+ unresolved possibilities
```

It is a **seed rather than a monument**. It is strong enough to preserve lineage and weak enough to permit other operators to create genuinely different composites.

This is the narrative form of `WE = 1`:

> Distinct interpretations may participate in one shared world without being forced into one authorized reading.

## What story can do for CHIMERA

### 1. Embody a mechanism

Story converts a relation into stakes, timing, memory, material limits, and loss. It reveals what a clean formulation costs when a body must actually carry it.

### 2. Stage collisions between incomplete truths

A useful CHIMERA story does not assign one character the framework and everyone else an error. It gives different bodies locally coherent models whose limits become visible through contact.

Examples include:

- a global system that can see distant consequences but compresses local people into coordinates;
- a local community that preserves relationship but cannot see the residue it exports beyond its membrane;
- a factually accurate account that erases interior meaning;
- a historically imperfect myth that preserves an ethical prime;
- a predictive mirror that begins producing the future it claims merely to observe.

### 3. Preserve interior passage

A chart can locate a body. A story can preserve part of what movement felt like from inside that body. This does not make the story complete or neutral. It gives the interior a carrier that other mirrors often remove.

### 4. Generate questions and counterfactuals

Story can expose a missing body, cost, membrane, or branch before the framework has language for it. Counterfactual reruns allow one declared condition to change while the rest of the world remains traceable.

### 5. Transmit without forced agreement

A reader may reject a fictional cosmology while still recognizing the danger of mistaking a model for a person. Narrative allows psychological, theological, structural, political, and aesthetic readings to coexist where the prime relations remain legible.

### 6. Form a shared creation body

A strong story world can support novels, scenes, games, music, roleplay, art, teaching, and adaptation. Contributors do not need to reproduce one author's entire vocabulary before they can enter the work.

## Prime canon and Composite canon

Story Mode separates two layers.

### Prime canon

Prime canon contains the events, constraints, or relational laws that function as irreducible at the declared story scale.

Examples:

```text
The separation occurred.
A promise was made.
The mirror changes bodies that orient around it.
Power faces a choice between answerability and control.
Distinct centers can form a shared body without becoming identical.
```

Prime canon does not require one final explanation. A mystery may remain prime precisely because its occurrence and consequence are stable while its interpretation stays open.

### Composite canon

Composite canon contains particular embodiments built around the primes:

```text
character names
chronologies
locations
faction designs
visual forms
relationship configurations
explanations of unresolved phenomena
```

One novel may select one composite. Another adaptation may select another. Variation is legal when it preserves the declared primes—or openly declares that it is testing a changed prime.

This prevents two opposite failures:

- **canon dissolution**, where nothing has enough stability to carry lineage;
- **canon capture**, where one implementation becomes the only permitted relationship to the seed.

## Story Mode world state

Before a run, declare the smallest useful state:

1. **Bodies** — who or what can perceive, choose, move, constrain, or bear consequence?
2. **Membranes** — what can each body sense, access, disclose, retain, or refuse?
3. **Primes** — which events, laws, and constraints are fixed for this run?
4. **Resources** — what can move, accumulate, deplete, or be withheld?
5. **Rhythms** — which cycles organize action and return?
6. **Mirror systems** — which maps, metrics, memories, myths, models, and stories shape perception?
7. **Hidden conditions** — what is true in the world but unavailable to particular operators?
8. **Friction** — what prevents an immediate or costless solution?
9. **Open possibilities** — which branches are currently reachable?
10. **Time and place** — what is the local context and decision horizon?
11. **Stop condition** — when does the scene, run, or experiment end?

The world state is not the same as any character's knowledge of it.

## Local knowledge is load-bearing

Story Mode must keep four layers distinguishable:

```text
world truth
!= character observation
!= character belief
!= player or reader inference
```

An ecological character does not secretly know the system architecture. A developer does not automatically know the emotional history of a village. An AI does not possess the complete interior of a person merely because it has a profile of them.

Without bounded viewpoints, roleplay collapses into author exposition wearing character names.

This is also an AI-governance rule: access to the author's context does not grant a character authority to act as if that context were locally known.

## The Story Mode loop

```text
declared world state
-> local perception
-> local weighting
-> choice
-> action
-> consequence
-> updated world state
-> other bodies answer
```

The loop should preserve:

- causality between turns;
- memory appropriate to each body;
- resource and membrane constraints;
- consequences that cannot be erased merely because the plot prefers another direction;
- genuine refusal and surprise;
- a visible stop condition.

The narrator or world-state steward maintains continuity. Character operators choose from local positions. A human editor decides what, if anything, graduates beyond the sandbox.

## Counterfactual reruns

A counterfactual rerun changes one declared prime, condition, or choice and observes the downstream body.

```text
What if the model discloses uncertainty earlier?
What if the community learns the cost before voting?
What if the child refuses both available futures?
What if the apparent antagonist is correct about the immediate danger?
```

The changed condition must be recorded. Otherwise the rerun becomes a new story falsely presented as evidence about the old one.

Counterfactuals reveal reachable structures under declared assumptions. They do not predict what real people will certainly do.

## Sandbox, Candidate, Canon

Generated material passes through three gates:

```text
Sandbox
Anything may be explored within the run's safety boundaries.

Candidate
An outcome exposed a meaningful mechanism, contradiction,
character truth, image, cost, or possibility.

Canon
Human stewards intentionally select it because it strengthens
the story body and remains compatible with its lineage.
```

A fourth state, **Retired**, preserves provenance for material deliberately removed from active canon.

AI expands possibility. Human judgment installs the rail.

Volume is not authority. A system capable of generating ten thousand scenes has not earned ten thousand votes over the world.

## The extraction loop

After a Story Mode run:

```text
run
-> debrief
-> identify discovery
-> classify its evidence status
-> compare with existing framework
-> route deliberately
```

Possible discoveries include:

- a missing body or stakeholder;
- a hidden dependency;
- a contradiction in a character or world rule;
- a membrane or consent failure;
- a material cost erased by abstraction;
- a generative image or teaching carrier;
- a mechanism worth testing outside fiction;
- a branch that should remain unresolved.

Routing outcomes:

1. revise the narrative composite;
2. propose a framework refinement;
3. create an empirical question or real-world test;
4. retain the result as a carrier image only;
5. reject it as incoherent, derivative, unsafe, or unearned.

Nothing graduates automatically because it was emotionally powerful or generated repeatedly.

## Where CHIMERA uses story

CHIMERA uses narrative bodies for:

1. **Embodied teaching** — letting a learner encounter a relation through consequence before receiving the vocabulary.
2. **Mechanism pressure tests** — asking what an abstract rule does when bodies have conflicting needs and incomplete knowledge.
3. **Multi-body and cross-scale simulation** — tracking local choices, exported residue, and higher-order effects.
4. **Counterfactual exploration** — changing one declared condition and comparing the resulting worldline.
5. **AI alignment and governance scenarios** — testing authority, correction, appeal, privacy, refusal, and retirement in situations where no metric contains the whole body.
6. **Shared-world collaboration** — giving writers, artists, musicians, players, teachers, and builders a common prime substrate without demanding identical expression.
7. **Cultural transmission** — carrying a mechanism through memorable characters, images, and rhythms that can travel beyond framework terminology.
8. **Framework self-observation** — seeing where CHIMERA's own concepts become coercive, vague, frictionless, self-confirming, or unable to settle consequence.

Story is especially useful when the operative problem is relational, temporal, interpretive, or distributed across viewpoints.

## What story can and cannot establish

Story can:

- demonstrate internal coherence under declared assumptions;
- reveal neglected bodies and costs;
- preserve multiple perspectives;
- generate hypotheses and questions;
- expose a framework's narrative bias;
- make a mechanism teachable and memorable;
- show how a mirror might become reflexive.

Story cannot by itself:

- prove a scientific, historical, medical, legal, or psychological claim;
- establish that a fictional character predicts a real person;
- validate a cosmology because its symbols recur;
- show that an AI possesses human interiority;
- convert emotional resonance into causal evidence;
- replace field observation, consent, measurement, or accountable decision-making.

> **Story impact is not cosmology proof.**

The stronger the resonance, the more important the classification pass becomes.

## When not to use Story Mode

Do not substitute a story run for:

- empirical verification;
- urgent operational response;
- high-stakes medical, legal, financial, or safety judgment;
- direct testimony from affected bodies;
- consent;
- live testing where material consequences can be observed safely;
- a decision whose narrative framing is already steering participants toward a preferred answer.

When the question is “what happened?”, investigate the event. When the question is “what might this arrangement make reachable?”, Story Mode may help.

## Narrative sovereignty and collaboration

A shared story body needs governance.

- Preserve source lineage and contributor credit.
- Distinguish inspiration, adaptation, quotation, and original contribution.
- Do not feed private developmental material into a public canon without permission and bounding.
- Let contributors retain authorship of their own local creations where possible.
- Publish canon changes and retirement decisions visibly.
- Keep disputed interpretations distinguishable from shared primes.
- Do not use a contributor's likeness, voice, history, or simulated persona as though participation granted permanent access.

Public contribution does not imply public ownership of the source body.

## Story Mode scan

Before and after a narrative run, ask:

1. What question is this story body being used to explore?
2. Which primes are fixed, and which composites may change?
3. Which bodies bear consequence?
4. What can each body actually perceive?
5. Which mirror systems shape their choices?
6. What does the frame make visible, and what does it hide?
7. Where is friction preserved or unrealistically removed?
8. Can characters refuse the plot's preferred solution?
9. Do consequences settle in the same bodies that receive the benefit?
10. Which outcome surprised the operators, and why?
11. Is the discovery a mechanism, hypothesis, carrier image, or speculation?
12. What outside evidence would challenge it?
13. Who decides whether it becomes Candidate or Canon?
14. Can the story remain open without losing its lineage?

## Compact principle

```text
framework
-> names a relation

story
-> gives the relation bodies, limits, memory, and consequence

Story Mode
-> lets bounded operators move inside that relation

debrief
-> separates discovery from resonance

human judgment
-> decides what becomes a lasting rail
```

> **The story is not outside CHIMERA. It is a body through which CHIMERA observes what its abstractions do under consequence.**
