## 0. The claim, stated once

**Sight pulls your attention and your behavior automatically, before you choose — it's the highest-bandwidth, most involuntary channel into you, and being seen takes write-access to your behavior (the panopticon at body-scale). So limiting sight — by hand (cover the eyes) or by mind (master attention) — is the most direct way to reclaim the write-access. It's a tool, not withdrawal: own the pull, don't wall off the world.**

---

## 1. Vision is the fastest involuntary pull — and it's doubly costly

You don't *decide* to look; you notice you're already looking. The visual input reweights you before any decision. And being-seen pulls you into **performance** — you act differently observed than alone. **Keeper:** vision is doubly costly to the operator — it pulls attention *out* (toward what you see) and being-seen pulls behavior *into* performance; both are write-access taken through the eyes, automatically.

## 2. The discipline, in two versions

- **External** — cover/limit the eyes (blindfold, lowered gaze, veil, closing your eyes when safe). Faster, easier, total — the training wheels.
- **Internal** — master attention by will, focus where you choose. Slower to build, but **portable** (no cloth needed).

The external is the training wheels for the internal. **Gojo's blindfold, Loki's covered eyes:** the one who sees *too much* must regulate the input to function — `the-lens-as-slice` as power (full reception is unlivable; the master deliberately re-narrows). The **veiling traditions** implement the same structural logic socially (limit the visual input that pulls attention and triggers performance) — *read as structure only; the specific practices, their ethics, and who bears the covering are signed cultural content held in their own drawer.*

## 3. Oedipus — the blinding read structurally

He was the great seer/solver, but his sight failed him: he saw the surface and missed the structure (looked at his parents and didn't recognize them). The self-blinding says *outward sight showed everything and revealed nothing true.* That's **read-the-structure-not-the-surface / `strip-to-the-skeleton` in tragic form** — and the paradox: the *blind* prophet sees the truth while the *sighted* king is blind to it (outward-sight and inner-insight are inverse).

## 4. Firewall: tool, not withdrawal

**Keeper / Firewall:** limiting sight is a *tool* — own your attention (choose what pulls you), not *wall off* the world (refuse to see). **Oedipus is the cautionary extreme, not a model** (he goes too far, into blindness and self-punishment). See *structurally* and choose what steers you; the operator authors the slice and stays in the world — they don't destroy the eyes. This is the firewall as a sensory practice: owning attention in a room (where you look, whether you perform) is the same skill as owning it online — the eye is its fastest, most physical instance.
