# Doom Broadcast and Self-Fulfilling Attention

> **Origin:** July 2026 — *Tomorrowland* “machine that broadcasts doom”; amplification of one future until acceptance; self-fulfilling loops; attention as feedback; not denial-optimism but frequency change.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

Some systems don’t merely **predict** collapse — they **amplify and broadcast** one future so hard that people stop acting, and the broadcast becomes self-fulfilling.

> “It’s not just showing people the end of the world. It’s giving them the idea over and over until they just accept it.”  
> — *Tomorrowland* (paraphrase of the film’s thesis)

On the attention field: high-velocity media and platforms can install **doom / outrage / resignation loops** through repetition + emotional activation + ranking. The signal is not neutral information; it is a **shaping force**.

## Self-fulfilling loop

```text
Amplify doom/outrage
  → emotional activation + social proof
  → more attention + ranking
  → more resignation / reactivity
  → less constructive action
  → field looks more like the broadcast
```

This is belief + light + repetition (see [Light, Belief, and Observation](light-belief-observation.md)). Same mechanism as Matrix loop-install ([Local Reality and the Global Attention Field](local-reality-and-global-attention-field.md)).

**AI note (careful):** predictive + amplify systems can strengthen dominant patterns in data. Not “AI is demiurge-evil” — **structure** of prediction + scale feedback. Counter with Pause, ¬F, and selective non-amplify.

---

## Practice

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| Notice when a feed is *broadcasting* not informing | Treat every doom post as neutral data |
| Pause before RT/rage-reply (feeding the machine) | Live inside collapse territories for “awareness” |
| Plant generative frequency where real | Blind hopium cosplay |
| Protect local reality while sensing global | Fuse identity to the broadcast |

Sometimes the strongest move is **withdrawing attention** from the loudest broadcast and placing it where coherence can grow (Solar Punk design logic: path of least resistance for healthy loops).
