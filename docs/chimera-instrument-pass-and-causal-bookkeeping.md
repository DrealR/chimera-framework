# The CHIMERA Instrument Pass and Causal Bookkeeping

> **Classification:** cross-domain synthesis and thought-experiment pressure practice. It operationalizes Reflect-and-Connect and Classification Discipline at the moment a resonance tries to become load-bearing.

## The seven movements

### 1. Hear the literal note

State the observation without mythic expansion. What happened? What is measured? What is inferred?

### 2. Locate the body

Name its parts, membrane, medium, clock, history, and settlement layer.

### 3. Find the local sun

What currently organizes the body's attention and resources? Who or what has write-access? This is the governing center, not necessarily a person.

### 4. Identify the force law

Name the mechanism in plain causal language: feedback, selection, incentive, transduction, synchronization, accumulation, constraint, learning, or something else.

### 5. Harmonize

Reflect the mechanism into other domains. Transfer only the relation that survives the costume change.

### 6. Counter-tune

Find cases where the proposed pattern should fail. Identify alternative explanations, missing variables, and the dimension on which the bodies are actually different.

### 7. Play it back

Return four lines:

```text
Signal:    what was observed
Resonance: what structure transferred
Firewall:  where the transfer stops
New note:  what can now be tested or built
```

## Thought experiments as controlled construction

A thought experiment may fictionalize what lies outside the causal path of its conclusion. It may not fictionalize the mechanism that produces the conclusion and then cite the imagined result as evidence.

Every serious thought experiment should make five things legible:

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Variables | What changes? |
| Constants | What is held fixed? |
| Idealizations | What is simplified or removed? |
| Mechanism | What actually produces the result? |
| Reality test | What observation, simulation, experiment, or counterexample can grade it? |

Fiction can make a consequence inhabitable. It cannot replace causal bookkeeping.

## Connection discipline

Tag the strongest relation actually supported:

- common origin;
- direct causal relation;
- indirect causal relation;
- structural similarity;
- informational relation;
- social relation;
- metaphorical carrier.

> **`WE = 1` is permission to search for a path, not a certificate that every path has equal force. Connection is the field. Discrimination is the work.**

## Graduation rule

Only mechanism and practice graduate as load-bearing framework structure. Carrier images may graduate when clearly labeled. Speculative bridges remain open. Unearned aesthetic resonance is pruned.

## Firewalls

- More cross-domain examples do not independently validate one another when they inherit the same assumption.
- A beautiful carrier can remain useful after its literal claim is rejected.
- The widest explanation is not automatically the deepest.
- “Everything is connected” begins the scan; it does not complete it.
