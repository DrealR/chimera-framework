# LARPing as Identity Reweighting

> **Origin:** July 2026 conversation: "everybody is LARPing" until the role gathers enough evidence to become installed identity.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The clean mechanism

A person often has to believe into a shape before the body has enough evidence to feel natural there.

That is not automatically fake. It is how identity changes.

**LARPing is identity reweighting before installation.** You choose a role, act from it, gather evidence, and repeat until the body stops treating it as a costume.

---

## The evidence-seeking body

The brain is an evidence-seeking system. If the field keeps producing evidence for the old identity, the old identity stays installed. If the operator wants a new identity, the field must be curated so the new role can survive long enough to gather evidence.

Healthy identity change therefore needs both:

- **internal environment:** language, attention, memory, self-story, practice;
- **external environment:** people, spaces, defaults, incentives, rhythms.

Trying to become a new body inside the exact field that built the old body is usually asking the seed to grow in soil that keeps poisoning it.

---

## Sustainable LARPing vs extraction LARPing

**Sustainable LARPing** faces outward. It chooses a role because the role is worth becoming, then remains updateable when reality pushes back.

**Extraction LARPing** faces inward. It performs a role for status, money, dominance, or image while refusing correction.

The difference is not performance vs authenticity. All becoming has a performed phase. The difference is direction and updateability.

---

## Firewall

- **Do not use "everyone is LARPing" as nihilism.** The point is not that nothing is real. The point is that real identity is often installed through repeated performed action.
- **Do not confuse aspiration with fraud.** Fraud refuses reality-testing. Aspiration uses reality-testing to become sturdier.
- **Do not keep people trapped in old evidence.** If the field never lets a body gather new evidence, it becomes a prison.
