## Present moment as social coordinate

Someone's **present** on a platform is where their attention is living *now* — latest post, reply, repost, active thread — not their bio, not a post from three days ago they have already left.

Finding where attention is = finding their temporary coordinate in the shared attention field. Meeting them there raises the odds of reciprocity. Leaving comments on cooled content is often shouting into an empty room.

This is the same law as [The Shared Present Is Made](the-shared-present-is-made.md), applied to the graph:

> Presence is attentional. Go to where they are alive right now.

## AI + social graph stack

Two layers:

1. **Live people** depositing attention into a platform (posts, replies, media)
2. **AI** that can query, filter, compress, and re-lens that flood using an operator's magnet

Without AI: flood and default mirrors.
With AI: custom pools, present-moment ranking, framework-aware resonance, on-the-fly filters.

**Direct access** (near-real-time pipeline) is highest juice. Indirect/scraped access can work but often cools the present-moment signal. Pattern is platform-agnostic: X, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, etc. — whichever gives the cleanest AI+live-data path wins for this use case. In 2026, **X + Grok** is one of the strongest foundations for text-first spark.

---

## Why text-first platforms spark easier

Lightweight engagement (words, replies, quote-posts) can start fusion without the production cost of video. On video-first platforms, comments often don't move the body; the video *is* the body. For intentional entanglement at low cost, text-native graphs are structurally friendlier.

## Firewalls

- **Not surveillance.** The goal is mutual, organic contact — not tracking, harvesting, or automated persuasion.
- **Not automated intimacy.** System surfaces; human engages. No auto-replies as core design.
- **Not "AI finds your soulmate."** Resonance is a lead, not a verdict. Operator judgment stays final.
- **Not platform worship.** X is a specimen pipeline; the primitive is AI+live graph, not brand loyalty.
- **Not panopticon fantasy.** You do not see "everyone." You see a steerable slice. Humility about coverage is required.
- **Privacy ethics:** Prefer public signals; respect membranes; do not treat people as data prey.
