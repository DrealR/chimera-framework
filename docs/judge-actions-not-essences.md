## 0. The claim, stated once

**Knowledge widens your spectrum (the knowledge of good and evil; the honeymoon ends when you get more information). The danger isn't the knowledge — it's turning it into a fixed judgment. An observation stays open: a person did an action (specific, revisable). A judgment closes: a person is bad (total, fixed, foreclosing). Judge the action, not the essence — and love is choosing observation-and-acceptance over judgment-and-foreclosure when the widened spectrum reveals someone's flaws.**

---

## 1. Observation is open; judgment forecloses

- **Observation:** *a person did an action* — specific, bounded, revisable.
- **Judgment:** *a person IS bad* — total, fixed, closed.

**Keeper:** judgment freezes the widened spectrum into a verdict that **forecloses the person** — the same foreclosure as borrowed-weight (the park you fear but never visit) and the spoiler (the film you can't watch fresh). Judging the essence shuts the door that judging the action leaves open. (See `the-visibility-spectrum`.)

## 2. Love is observation applied to a whole person over time (WE = 1)

To share a body with someone (WE = 1, love) you **accept them past the honeymoon-phase information** — accept the flaws the widened spectrum revealed — instead of freezing them into a verdict. The honeymoon ends when you get more information; **love is choosing observation-and-acceptance over judgment-and-foreclosure when that information arrives.** That is *love-as-time / unconditional-love-as-consistency* made precise: love refuses to freeze the person into a verdict even as it learns their flaws.

## 3. Keep the distinction real — don't dissolve into vibes

The opposite failure is the relativist one ("truth is just resonance, the best story wins, everyone's right from their own frequency"), which dissolves real distinction into *whoever-frames-strongest* (the harvester) and dissolves the operator (no one home making a real judgment). **Keeper:** keep the real distinction (actions are observable and judgeable; truth isn't just vibes) *while* keeping persons open. Keep truth **and** the open person — don't collapse either.

## 4. You own the judgment (the operator's seat)

You're not *forced* to judge; choosing to keep people open — observe actions, don't freeze essences — is an **act of will you own.** The operator authoring their own moral life, against the determinism of "everyone's just on their frequency."
