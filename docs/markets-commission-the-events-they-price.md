*A framework study. Graduated from the 2026-06-30 Pump Fun Go / bounty-economy capture in core. Names the observer-actor inversion where prediction, attention, and bounty markets do not merely read reality; they can fund the events that move the reality they claim to measure. Internal register.*

## 0. The claim, stated once

**Prediction markets can function like collective body scans: a price reading distributed belief, information, and expectation. The new inversion is that markets can also commission the events they price. The scan becomes a spell when a position funds performances, posts, stunts, or campaigns that move the discourse/probability/price the market claims to measure.**

---

## 1. Market as scan

A market price is a compressed reading. It integrates many local views into one number. In that sense, a prediction market can resemble a body scan: not truth, but a live diagnostic of where belief and incentive currently sit.

Held properly, that is useful. The number is not oracle; it is an instrument reading. Read it as one signal among others.

## 2. Market as spell

The inversion appears when the market stops only observing and starts writing.

```
read probability
    ↓
place position
    ↓
fund performance / influence / stunt
    ↓
move discourse
    ↓
alter probability / price
```

At that point the market has become actor and observer at once. It writes into the body it is measuring, then points to the changed measurement as if it were pure information.

## 3. Why bounty markets complete the loop

A prediction market says: "What will happen?"
A bounty market says: "Who will make something happen?"
A memecoin market says: "Can attention make this price happen?"
A UGC market says: "Can a distributed crowd make this look organic?"

Together they form a reality-scripting stack: position, commission, performance, amplification, price movement. The most dangerous object is not any one post or clip. It is the loop that makes real-world events downstream of hidden financial incentives.

## 4. The literacy shift

This sharpens `read-the-intent-not-the-realness`. The event may be real. The participant may be real. The consequence may be real. Realness is no longer sufficient because the question is not whether the event occurred. The question is what body commissioned it, what market it served, and who absorbed the cost.

In synthetic-event conditions, the operator asks:

- What position benefits if this spreads?
- What body paid for the event or amplification?
- Who is made more programmable by this?
- Who loses membrane, trust, or dignity?
- What would boring proof look like if the stated purpose were sincere?

## 5. Firewall

Do not turn this into "everything is a psyop." That is the paranoia trap and the liar's dividend. A commissioned event is one possible reading, not the default verdict. Hold the possibility without collapsing into total suspicion.

The legal move is: read incentives, preserve uncertainty, and choose your attention accordingly. You do not need certainty to decline being routed.
