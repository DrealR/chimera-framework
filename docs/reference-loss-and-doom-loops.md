# Reference Loss and Doom Loops

> **Classification:** system-dynamics mechanism and diagnostic practice. This extends death spirals of local optimization by naming the moment a coordinated body stops using the outside world to correct itself and treats its internally generated signals as the world.

## The claim

> **A system becomes a doom loop when its outputs increasingly determine its participants' next compulsory actions while external reality loses authority to correct the loop.**

Strong coordination is not sufficient for direction. An ant spiral can remain locally coherent while collectively losing the trail's destination. A leveraged market can force selling in response to price-derived constraints even when the underlying asset changes less than the surrounding signal body.

```text
internal signal changes
→ rules or constraints compel similar responses
→ responses strengthen the signal
→ slack and alternatives shrink
→ the amplified signal becomes evidence for itself
→ outside reference arrives too late or cannot enter
```

This is **reference loss**: the map gains write-access to the territory it was meant to describe.

## Four bodies that must not be collapsed

Many doom loops become legible only after separating:

1. **Underlying body** — company, person, institution, ecosystem, or real condition.
2. **Signal body** — price, metric, ranking, narrative, score, or reported risk.
3. **Constraint body** — margin rule, mandate, algorithm, policy, incentive, or habit that converts signal into action.
4. **Narrative body** — the explanation that coordinates expectations about all three.

The signal may begin as evidence about the underlying body. Once constraints respond to it, the signal also becomes a causal force. Once the narrative treats that feedback as independent confirmation, the loop can become self-sealing.

## Conditions that raise doom-loop risk

A loop becomes more dangerous when several conditions combine:

- **high gain** — small signals produce large downstream moves;
- **compulsory coupling** — participants cannot simply wait or choose another response;
- **behavioral similarity** — many bodies share the same thresholds, models, or exit rule;
- **low slack** — little time, liquidity, trust, inventory, or emotional capacity remains;
- **reference loss** — proxy movement outranks direct evidence about the host;
- **poor metabolism** — losses, residue, debt, error, or conflict have nowhere to settle and clear.

No secret central operator is required. Locally reasonable moves can compose a globally destructive body.

## Generative loop versus doom loop

| Generative loop | Doom loop |
|---|---|
| Multiple responses remain viable | Rules synchronize bodies toward one response |
| External evidence can revise the next cycle | Internal signals validate themselves |
| Slack permits interpretation and repair | Thresholds force urgency |
| Error lands somewhere and is metabolized | Error is amplified or displaced |
| Participants retain veto and exit | Optionality collapses |
| The loop increases future capacity | The loop consumes its own future |

The distinction is not repetition. It is whether the repeated body remains answerable to something beyond its own signal.

## Distance and network bodies

In multilayer systems, distance is vector-valued. Two countries, institutions, or people may be physically distant and financially, informationally, emotionally, or operationally adjacent.

```text
D = (geographic, funding, collateral, production,
     portfolio, information, narrative, governance)
```

“Everything is connected” is therefore too weak. Ask: connected through what, in which direction does dependence run, what transmits nourishment, what transmits stress, and where are the amplifiers and circuit breakers?

## Doom-loop scan

1. Name the underlying body the system is supposed to serve or describe.
2. Name the proxy or signal being watched.
3. Write the rule that converts signal into action.
4. Identify which responses are compulsory and for whom.
5. Locate leverage, feedback gain, and shared thresholds.
6. Measure real behavioral diversity, not actor count.
7. Find evidence channels independent of the loop.
8. Locate slack, delay, reserves, and substitution paths.
9. Name where residue, loss, and failure can settle or be released.
10. Choose the smallest intervention that restores optionality and outside reference.

## Circuit breakers

Useful interventions include reserves, feedback delays, diversified strategies, leverage limits, independent audits, alternative routes, graceful degradation, appeal, restructuring, and explicit stop conditions. Their common function is not stopping all motion. It is restoring a gap in which the body can read reality before reproducing the signal.

## Firewalls

- A self-reinforcing process is not automatically false; useful adoption and learning can also compound. Test outside consequence.
- Inclusion in a causal body does not imply moral justification. Manipulation may be real and still require constraint.
- Do not infer inevitable collapse from one reinforcing link. Inspect gain, slack, substitution, and countervailing bodies.
- A market price, feed metric, or social reaction is evidence from a particular participating body, not the final state of the underlying world.
