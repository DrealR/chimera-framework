## The claim

Two scales, coupled but not identical:

| Scale | What it is |
|-------|------------|
| **Local reality** | Your body, room, relationships, day — physical life |
| **Global attention field** | High-velocity platforms (esp. X), culture, narratives installing and directing loops at scale |

> For most non-celebrity operators: the global field can shape **thought and mood** heavily, but only reshapes **local life** if you let it (act on it, fuse identity to it, or live inside high-impact nodes).

Healthy stance: **aware enough not to be blindly run; not so detached you can’t participate meaningfully.**

## Asymmetric power (low entanglement)

| Position | Global → local | Local → global |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Low-vis / anon-ish** | Field hard to wreck your day unless you internalize | You can still plant into the field |
| **High-vis / celebrity** | Global attention distorts local life hard | Local acts become global events fast |

Most people either stay offline-shielded or get pulled and let the field own mood and identity. Conscious practice uses the asymmetry: **influence without full capture**.

## “People do what they do with the information”

Same signal enters many systems. Outcomes differ by **existing structure**:

- Barely moves established loops  
- Heavily activates and shifts behavior  
- Consciously filtered / rejected  
- Used as raw material to build  

The field **amplifies tendencies** more than it invents persons from zero. Resonance still rules landing. Light + belief: [Light, Belief, and Observation](light-belief-observation.md).
