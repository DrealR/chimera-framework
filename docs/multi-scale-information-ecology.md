## The claim

Information and attention move through **multi-scale relational fields**: many local centers, each with its own subjective tick, participating in shared rhythms at different speeds.

> There is no single absolute public time. There are **layers of processing** — raw/fast, mid, deep/slow — with delays, feedback loops, and different kinds of value at each scale.

CHIMERA’s job is to help the operator **move between scales without losing coherence** — neither pure consumption at the fast layer nor forced convergence into one slow narrative.

## Three layers (practical map)

### 1. Raw / fast layer
Live attention, low friction, high velocity (e.g. real-time text graph).  
**Value:** present-moment coordinates, early signal, competitive selection.  
**Risk:** ragebait, death spirals, shallow harvest.

### 2. Mid / relational layer
Frameworks, AI mirrors, small teams, Shared Wave sandboxes, Notebook-style organization.  
**Value:** structure without full institutional freeze; operator steering.  
**Risk:** over-synthesis fog; “everything maps.”

### 3. Deep / slow layer
Longform, institutions, books, production media.  
**Value:** retention, proof work, cultural memory.  
**Risk:** lag, capture by old scoreboards, mistaking polish for origin.

AI + CHIMERA sit mostly in the **middle**: expand reach, hold structure, move signal between raw and deep without erasing either.

## Operator as multi-scale being

You yourself are multi-scale:

- body ticks (breath, heartbeat)  
- thought ticks (Pause, reflection)  
- project ticks (days–months)  
- relationship ticks (years)  

CHIMERA is partly **scale navigation of the self**: don’t let the fast layer own all attention; don’t let the slow layer freeze all motion. Allocate attention deliberately across timescales.

## Planting seeds across layers

| Goal | Prefer |
|------|--------|
| Race / early signal | Fast layer |
| Dialogue / refinement | Mid layer |
| Durable teaching / archive | Slow layer |

Track feedback both ways: deep insight should be allowed to re-enter the fast layer as a clean seed, not only as content milking.

---

## Design laws

1. **Don’t force one scale to do every job.**  
2. **Default Magnet to live layer;** use downstream for context.  
3. **Default Wave to multi-depth,** not uniform agents.  
4. **Prefer ecosystem continuity** over per-task model shopping ([Primary Relational Body](primary-relational-body-and-ecosystem-stack.md)).  
5. **Classify velocity ≠ truth.** Fast signals still need firewalls.
