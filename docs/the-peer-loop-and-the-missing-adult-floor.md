## 0. The claim, stated once

**A generation raised by its own peers through the feed — with no adult standing above the loop pouring attention from outside — faces inward, freezes at friction, and never grows the inner life that connection and problem-solving require. SPARK is the inverse: one present operator above the loop, handing the kid the pen.**

---

## 1. The core mechanism: a closed loop with no operator above it

The deepest claim: it's the first generation raised more by other kids (via the feed) than by adults — so they emulate *each other* in a closed loop, rewarded for *seeming grown* instead of being *corrected*. A field with **no operator above it**, so the feedback spiral amplifies itself. The split-body / closed-system at population scale.

**SPARK's answer:** the present coach is the missing adult floor — the operator above the peer-loop, pouring attention *from outside* the spiral.

## 2. The failure modes (each a thing SPARK answers)

- **Troubleshooting collapse** — the curated screen skipped the friction that builds problem-solving, so kids freeze at the first "I don't know" (the dead laptop, the abandoned form). → `friction-as-foundational-primitive`; "stuck is a step, not a wall."
- **Entitlement = frustration-then-rescue on repeat** — the muscle that closes the gap between wanting and getting never forms. → SPARK keeps the productive gap (v1 isn't what you pictured → figure out why → go again).
- **No inner life → nothing to connect to** (the Lewis point: friendship needs solitude first). → the journal is the protected solitude that grows the self the feed erodes.
- **Facing inward, performing** → SPARK turns the kid back out: build for a real person.

## 3. The firewalls

- **Keep the structural claim, drop the moral-panic** — "kids these days" is what every era's elders say; what's *new* is the structural removal of the adult floor. Keep that; drop the the-past-was-pure tone.
- **Pattern, not villain** — "oriented toward money/prizes" is a real pattern (money in the value function distorts development); hold any specific-person-as-villain framing loosely.
- **Cringe-tolerance is the keeper** — raising a generation unafraid to look stupid is *author-the-source-not-the-reflection*; SPARK's by-hand making (cardboard first, the ungraded journal) trains exactly that.
