## 0. The claim, stated once

**You don't just receive the algorithm — you author it, by what you search, like, watch, and comment. It is the attention-fluid with a machine in the middle: it flows by its own dynamics AND is shapeable locally by an operator casting signals. The operator surfaces the buried signal by steering deliberately; everyone else takes what's surfaced and sits in the echo chamber.**

---

## 1. Fishing in reverse

The feed is usually described as fishing *you*. The operator move is the inverse: *you fish it* — casting signals (searches, likes, watches) to pull the water toward what you want. **Write-access to your own attention, made literal** in the system built to take it: the feed wants to author you; you author it back with the signals you choose. Multi-accounts is the advanced version — partitioning the ocean, bending different currents for different purposes.

**Keeper:** authoring the feed is the conductor move (`the-attention-field`) run on the algorithm — the inverse of being harvested by it.

## 2. A dance, not control either way

You don't fully control the algorithm (it has its own flow); it doesn't fully control you (you steer with signals). It's a **partnership** — the resolution of the allocator-vs-competition fork: *flows by its own dynamics, shapeable locally.* The discipline is keeping one foot out — the operator stays separate, not the fish pulled under.

## 3. Buried, not hidden — the scarcity flipped

With infinite content (plus bots and AI slop), information isn't *hidden*, it's **buried**. The scarcity flipped from **access** (hard to find) to **navigation** (hard to surface). You pull insight out like a magnet; the skill is *surfacing*, not finding. This is why one can feel "there's nothing to watch" amid everything — the signal is buried and the feed surfaces the noise.

**Keeper:** the echo chamber is what happens when you don't author the algorithm — you take what's surfaced instead of surfacing the buried.

## 4. AI is the same move, frozen

The LLM's latent space is the internet's information **buried in weights** — frozen, delayed (trained to a cutoff). You surface from it by **prompting**, exactly as you search a feed. The only difference is **live vs frozen**: the feed surfaces live content, the model surfaces frozen content; both are *steer-and-pull*, and intention directs which buried weights activate (see `ai-as-write-head-to-your-weights`). Your prompt is to the model what your search is to the feed.

## 5. Two sides of one fluid

Authoring your feed (steering your **intake**) and people gathering around a post (attention **pooling around a node**) are the same fluid from two sides. The operator does both — steers what flows *to* them, and can become a node others flow *to*. Most people do neither.

## 6. The firewall

Authoring the feed is the operator move — but steering only toward what **confirms** you builds the echo chamber. The discipline is steering toward **truth and breadth**, not more-of-the-same; the multi-account move can wall you into curated bubbles. The tell, as always, is **direction**: steering to find what's true-and-buried, or to confirm what you already believe.

## 7. SPARK

This is **write-access-to-your-own-attention made concrete in the system kids actually live in.** The feed-raised kid drifts and takes what's surfaced (echo chamber); the operator-kid authors the feed, surfaces the buried signal, doesn't get pulled under. One of the most directly teachable pieces — every kid already has a feed and has felt it shape them, so showing them they can author it back is immediate and real.
