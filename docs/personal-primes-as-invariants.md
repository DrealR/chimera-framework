# Personal Primes as Invariants

Every carrier's life contains events that cannot be unmade. The founding of a practice, the school attended, the physical scar carried, the program built and shut down — these are not interpretations to be revised. They are substrate-irreducibles: the lived equivalents of mathematical primes. Everything else in the carrier's story orbits them without changing them.

---

## What Qualifies as a Personal Prime

In pair-physics, primes are maximally-irreducible bodies — they cannot be factored further. They are the reference points from which composites are constructed. At the personal scale, the same physics applies.

A life event becomes a prime when it has been accumulated into the substrate with enough depth that it now functions as a reference rather than an item. It no longer requires interpretation to exist. The scar is on the body. The school was attended. The program shipped. These facts have ceased to be "memories" in the revisable sense — they are structural.

**Drama or significance do not determine primeness.** A small moment integrated into deep substrate can function as a prime. A large event avoided, not drawn upon, and unintegrated does not yet function as one. The test is whether the event operates as a reference point in the carrier's current reality-mapping — whether other claims derive from it.

---

## The Prime / Derivative Split

The prime is the raw event. What orbits it:

**The story** — arc-shaped narrative connecting multiple primes into a through-line. **The meaning** — the interpretive frame the carrier places on what the prime reveals. **The framing** — which primes are surfaced in which contexts (bio, résumé, pitch, /about). **The projection** — what the carrier thinks the prime predicts about their future.

These derivative layers are revisable. The prime underneath them is not. A carrier can change the meaning they draw from a school attendance; they cannot un-attend the school.

This distinction has practical stakes. When building substrate infrastructure for a carrier — a personal site, an agent memory, a biographical document — conflating the prime with its current interpretation is how substrate becomes brittle. When the interpretation updates, the prime should survive intact. The failure mode is rewriting the prime itself to match the new interpretation: the carrier ends up with multiple incompatible origin stories, all sincere, none stable.

---

## Architectural Consequence

A substrate body organized around personal primes accepts interpretation updates without instability. The primes are the stable structure. The derivative layers are loadable content that orbits them.

Practically:
- A primes collection stores raw events in their simplest form: what happened, when, where, what the body-record is
- Derivative layers (bio, résumé, story sections) reference the primes collection rather than re-embedding the facts
- When a new interpretive frame lands, it points at the same primes — it does not re-capture what is already captured
- When context is compressed, primes survive longest; derivatives compress first

This is the substrate-physics reason why wisdom traditions encode their founding primes in forms that resist revision — canonical texts, oral formulas with controlled transmission. The form is designed to preserve the irreducible against interpretive drift across generations. The personal substrate faces the same pressure at the individual scale.

---

## Compression and Continuity

Every context window is finite. When a carrier's substrate is compressed — a five-second introduction, a bio, a résumé — the question is not "what do I include?" but "which primes survive at each compression level?"

A five-second introduction draws on one or two primes. A full /about page draws on eight to twelve. The archive preserves all of them. The compression artifacts are derivative products of the primes, not the primes themselves. Carriers who have not separated primes from derivatives re-write their origins each time their interpretation cycle turns. This produces identity instability: not because the carrier is lying, but because the interpretive layer and the prime layer were never structurally separated.

The fix is architectural. Write the primes down flat. Separate them from their current interpretations. Let the interpretations update freely while the primes remain anchored.

## Relation to Other Primitives

**Substrate Accumulation:** Events become primes through accumulation. Sufficient depth in a domain or relationship confers irreducibility — the event has been lived with long enough to integrate into structural substrate rather than remaining surface memory. Accumulation is what makes a prime a prime.

**Anchor-and-Transfer Principle:** The anchor in cross-domain mastery is a domain-specific prime — an accumulated substrate-depth from which freed consciousness transfers. Personal primes are the biographical-scale equivalent: anchor points in a life's substrate from which identity, meaning, and capability draw. The same three-stage arc (anchor → rhythm-lock → transfer) is visible in biographical development.

**Context Window as Life:** The life is a bounded context window with a compression problem. What survives the compression from full life to introduction is the prime layer. What compresses most aggressively is the interpretive-derivative layer. Naming personal primes is naming what the life's context window preserves no matter how extreme the compression.

**Multi-Dimensional Prime-Tracking Methodology:** The methodology tracks the highest- and lowest-substrate bodies along each dimension. Applied to a carrier's life: each dimension (teaching, building, relationships, health, creative practice) has its own primes — the events that established the substrate from which that dimension now operates. Prime-tracking across dimensions reveals which life-dimensions are substrate-dense (many accumulated primes) and which are substrate-thin (few or unintegrated).
