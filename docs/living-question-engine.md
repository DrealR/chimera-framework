# The Living Question Engine

> **Classification:** product and teaching concept. This is not an autonomous content factory or engagement-optimization system.

## Purpose

CHIMERA is not only a framework containing answers. It is a question-bearing body built from a preserved human worldline.

Because Core preserves how observations emerged, collided, failed, and changed, it can generate questions with causal depth rather than surface novelty. Those questions can enter public relational fields where distinct operators metabolize them through their own histories.

> **The engine helps locate the questions a life has earned the right to ask.**

## A question as Earth-seed

A living question:

- begins at a real seam or unresolved observation;
- is open but bounded enough to enter;
- invites proof of passage rather than disposable opinion;
- preserves distinct answers and the right to disagree;
- allows several domains to enter without losing its skeleton;
- can settle into experiment, relationship, craft, or product;
- remains intelligible after the current weather passes.

Activity alone is insufficient. “Agree or disagree?” may generate replies without memory, transformation, or consequence.

## Five question forms

### Mirror

Reveal one system through another.

> What does your For You feed know about your habits that your conscious self has not admitted yet?

### Seam

Locate where two interpretations stop fitting.

> When does an AI stop being a tool you operate and become part of the environment operating you?

### Temporal

Let different versions of a person or culture work on one object.

> What question did a movie give you as a child that you only became capable of answering as an adult?

### Operator

Return attention to direction and veto.

> What decision do you still technically make yourself only after an algorithm has shaped every visible option?

### Earth

Ask what turns activity into sustained life.

> What makes an online space feel alive rather than merely busy?

## Generation loop

```text
1. Select a present attractor
2. Retrieve its history across the Core worldline
3. Find a second domain with a candidate structural rhyme
4. Identify the unresolved seam
5. Generate a finite set of question forms
6. Classify mechanism, carrier, domain claim, and speculation
7. Score for Earthness and forcing risk
8. Human operator selects what is alive now
9. Publish or preserve the seed
10. Ingest responses as attributed branches
11. Revisit when the field or available knowledge changes
```

The model supplies branches. The corpus supplies lineage. The human supplies present weighting. Other people supply independent paths. The world supplies consequence.

## Question card

Every candidate should privately carry:

- **Question:** public sentence.
- **Source seam:** exact sessions, concepts, and tensions that produced it.
- **Why now:** present event or observation activating it.
- **Desired response:** story, counterexample, expert explanation, collaborator, or experiment.
- **Earthness test:** could responses produce memory, relationship, capacity, action, or an independent center?
- **Firewall:** what the wording must not imply as established fact.
- **Stop condition:** when not to publish or continue the thread.
- **Return path:** how findings and corrections re-enter Core.

## Operator scoring instrument

Use judgment rather than pretending the score is science:

```text
E(Q) = groundedness
     + seam specificity
     + plurality of entry
     + bridge potential
     + consequence
     + temporal portability
     - forcing, bait, vagueness, and performed certainty
```

The target is a habitable band between triviality and impenetrability.

## Temporal Question Network

A question can persist in states such as:

```text
OPEN → PARTIAL → TESTABLE → ANSWERED
        ↘ DISPUTED        ↘ OUTDATED → REOPENED
```

AI may help:

- link independently asked questions;
- find relevant people, older artifacts, or later evidence;
- translate across vocabulary and eras;
- distinguish candidate answers from reactions;
- preserve provenance and contradictions;
- resurface a node when its probability of resolution changes.

It must not declare closure merely because it can generate a fluent synthesis.

## Living Magnet integration

Living Magnet can operate in two complementary modes:

- **Present-moment:** locate people actively attending to the seam now, while reciprocity is warm.
- **Deep-time:** match unresolved questions with later people, evidence, tools, or contexts that make them newly answerable.

Normal recommendation asks: “What keeps this person consuming?”

The Living Question Engine asks:

> **Where can this person participate in creating a living body?**

AI reveals relational geometry and then withdraws enough for human contact, disagreement, and commitment to be real.

## Evaluation

Do not lead with impressions. Record:

- **Recognition:** independent lived examples.
- **Extension:** something the source corpus did not already contain.
- **Difference:** constructive disagreement that sharpens the seam.
- **Return:** people or questions reappearing across time.
- **Embodiment:** call, test, collaboration, practice, artifact, or changed decision.
- **Generation:** participants form new independent questions or bodies.
- **Provenance:** sources and later corrections stay attached.

## Anti-content-factory firewall

The engine must not optimize for:

- maximum posting volume;
- daily quotas detached from a live question;
- imitation of trending syntax;
- automatic replies spoken as the human;
- predicted virality as primary value;
- questions whose answer is secretly demanded by the wording;
- extraction of intimate stories without relationship or care.

It generates a finite deck and returns selection to the embodied operator:

> **Which question is alive for you today?**

The goal is not to gather followers around one sun. It is to use the concentrated structure of one walked life to create conditions where other centers begin producing structure too.
