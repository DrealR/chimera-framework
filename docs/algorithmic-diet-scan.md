# The Algorithmic Diet Scan

> **Origin:** July 2026 application of Elastic Navigation to social media, AI, music, attention, and embodied learning.
>
> **Status:** operator practice. This is a training and product-design instrument, not medical advice.

---

## The claim

> **Algorithms train organs.**

A medium does not only deliver content. Repeated exposure rehearses a rhythm of noticing, switching, anticipating, reacting, and returning. The feed changes what becomes easy to notice; the body changes what it asks the feed to return. AI makes the same loop deeper and faster around a chosen coordinate.

The operational question is therefore not whether training is happening. It is:

> **Which medium is training me, toward which attractor, at what rate, and what capacity is being installed in my body?**

## Breadth and depth

Social media and AI amplify different movements:

- **Social media multiplies breadth:** people, lives, emotional states, partial worlds, and context switches.
- **AI multiplies depth:** explanations, counterarguments, drills, mappings, and recursive traversal around one coordinate.

Neither movement is healthy by default.

```text
broad exposure without settlement → fragmentation
deep recursion without world return → self-sealing orbit
breadth + deliberate selection + depth + practice + rest → learning
```

Broadband mode keeps many bands active and frequently switches the governing center. Narrowband mode reduces the field so repeated contact, correction, and consolidation can occur inside one domain.

> **Exposure creates traces. Practice turns traces into capacity. Rest makes the capacity retainable. Expression tests whether it became yours.**

## Before entering

Name the session rather than entering a medium without a body.

1. **Domain:** What am I entering—guitar, research, friendship, news, entertainment, or open wandering?
2. **Direction:** What should I be more able to notice, understand, make, or do afterward?
3. **Mode:** Do I need breadth, depth, practice, recovery, or play?
4. **Clock:** What is the stopping condition—time, one solved question, one physical rep, or one completed artifact?
5. **Ground:** What real body, source, person, or task will correct the medium's output?

Open wandering is a legitimate mode when chosen. The failure is not play; it is an unnamed session silently inheriting the platform's objective.

## During contact

Read the Band-Sphere:

- **Amplitude:** Which signal has become disproportionately heavy?
- **Phase:** Am I exploring, training, performing, compulsively returning, or ready to rest?
- **Rate:** Is the input arriving faster than I can integrate it?
- **Coupling:** Which other bands—sleep, body, mood, relationships, work—are being pulled with it?
- **Operator continuity:** Can I still redirect, stop, question, or leave?

The simplest live check is:

> **What is this medium making easier to do next?**

If the answer is only "consume another unit," the circuit is probably serving the medium more than the body.

## The learning chamber

Use this cycle when the goal is capacity rather than exposure:

```text
1. Reduce the field
   Remove unrelated high-amplitude signals.

2. Choose one domain
   Name the body you intend to enter.

3. Establish a carrier rhythm
   Use music, breath, schedule, place, or ritual to stabilize attention.

4. Use AI to map relations
   Ask for prerequisites, patterns, drills, counterexamples, and likely errors.

5. Enter the real medium
   Play, write, calculate, build, speak, move, or meet the person.

6. Read the error
   Find what the body could not yet perform.

7. Return to AI selectively
   Translate that specific error into another explanation or exercise.

8. Rest
   Stop input long enough for consolidation and appetite to reset.

9. Express
   Perform, teach, build, test, or give the result to another body.

10. Preserve the wake
    Record what changed and revise the next session.
```

The map may arrive almost instantly. The body still compiles it through repetitions, errors, sleep, relationship, and consequence.

## Music as carrier wave

Music can provide controlled recurrence without introducing a new unrelated object every few seconds. A useful carrier rhythm may:

- occupy restless attention with predictable motion;
- create a time-boundary around work;
- regulate arousal;
- reduce context switching;
- make another signal easier to sustain.

Lyrics can compete with verbal work; high novelty can become another feed. Test the effect on the actual task. The deletion test is behavioral: did the chosen music increase sustained contact and useful output, or merely make avoidance feel focused?

## After leaving

Run the return scan:

1. What received most of my attention?
2. Which emotion or action did I rehearse?
3. What am I now more able to do?
4. What became harder to sustain?
5. What survived without the screen?
6. What physical, relational, or creative action settles the session?
7. What needs rest, deletion, or a changed membrane before the next entry?

A nourishing session returns to Earth:

```text
signal → interpretation → Pause → practice → consequence → evidence → memory
```

The capture loop never reaches settlement:

```text
synthetic signal → attention → behavioral trace → improved targeting → synthetic signal
```

## Product-design form

For an AI or media system, ask:

- What rhythm does the interface install?
- Does it create natural endings, or only another unit?
- Can the user see and change the objective shaping recommendations?
- Does the system help the user enter a real task and then leave?
- Where are verification, provenance, and embodied feedback?
- Does personalization increase operator capacity or merely predict capture more accurately?
- What does the product do with residue—stale recommendations, compulsive loops, synthetic duplication, and unclosed tasks?
- Which local bodies pay the energy, water, labor, moderation, and attention costs?

An aligned system is not one that maximizes time inside itself. It improves the user's ability to direct attention, perform a valued action, read consequence, and close the circuit.

## Firewall

- This scan is not a purity regime. Entertainment, play, rest, surprise, and broad exploration are real needs.
- Do not confuse all high use with addiction or diagnose yourself or others from a checklist.
- For severe compulsion, eating disorders, self-harm, substance use, or mental-health symptoms, environmental controls and qualified human support outrank a self-directed exposure exercise.
- Do not use optimization language to turn a life into one more productivity machine. Recovery and unmeasured presence are part of the body.
- The claim "algorithms train organs" means repeated interaction trains perception and behavior. It does not mean software literally rewrites every organ or fully determines the person.
