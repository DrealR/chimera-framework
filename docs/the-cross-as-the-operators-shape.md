## 1. Intersection — the base fact

A cross is two lines that **intersect**; every meaning radiates from that one fact (the plus, the crossroads, the crosshairs, the genetic cross, crossing-over). It's the universal sign for **intersection** — *meaning lives where things intersect* (the relational/combinatorial thread, drawn).

## 2. The two axes = the two sides of love

**Vertical** = the sacred/eternal/timeless; **horizontal** = the human/temporal/relational. The cross is the meeting of the two — which maps onto the **two sides of love**: vertical = **love-as-seeing** (the weightless timeless reach), horizontal = **love-as-time** (fusion, weight built over time with others). **Keeper:** the human at the crossing is the operator's seat between the eternal and the temporal.

## 3. The body at the crossing = P, and the firewall as a shape

The body at the point where vertical meets horizontal is **P — the present human, the operator** — holding both axes. **Keeper:** this is the firewall *drawn* — don't collapse into pure vertical (cosmic-dissolution; the no-self / cosmic-unity failure) and don't collapse into pure horizontal (lost in the flood of others and time). Hold the crossing: one foot on the eternal reach, one on the temporal floor.

## 4. The threshold = creation-is-slow-destruction

The cross is also a **threshold** — crossing states requires a **death of the old** (the caterpillar dissolves entirely before the butterfly). **Keeper:** the new comes through the death of the old, not by addition — which is creation-is-slow-destruction as a symbol. **The fusion:** intersection and threshold are the *same point* — to stand at the crossing of eternal and temporal, the merely-horizontal self must die; the vertical crossing your horizontal life *is* the death-and-rebirth. This is the operator's choice-point: every "the line was crossed / harvest-to-conduct / into judgment / which-way-a-kid-faces" is a decision-threshold where direction flips and the path-not-taken dies a small death.
