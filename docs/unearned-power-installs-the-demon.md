# Unearned Power Installs the Demon

*A framework study. The install/borrowed-legs thread made into a corruption mechanism (One Piece's Domi Reversi, ch. 1181): power that arrives faster than the capacity to hold it corrupts — because the work is what would have given you the capacity. And the immunity condition: the one who is whole (earned power, no unearned hunger) can't be bought. Graduated from a core capture. Internal register; the One Piece reading held in the story.*

## 0. The claim, stated once

**Unearned power corrupts — not because power is bad, but because power that arrives *faster than the capacity to hold it* (strength without the work that would have built the capacity) installs a demon you can't control. The work is the protection. And the corruption needs a hook: it works on the craving for unearned power and the willingness to bargain for it. So the one who is whole — earned power, no unearned hunger, wants nothing the offer sells — has no hook, and can't be bought, conveyed, or corrupted. The borrowed-legs problem, weaponized; and the operator, immune.**

---

## 1. Domi Reversi = the borrowed-legs problem weaponized

**Keeper:** the corruption mechanism is *power arriving without the effort that would have given you capacity to hold it* — strength installed faster than you walked to it. That's exactly the borrowed-legs / install-without-walking thread (`knowing-as-installation`, `not-getting-installed-by-ai`): unearned capability doesn't free you, it **installs the demon** and makes you a puppet of whatever gave it. *The kid who swallows AI raw instead of building the understanding is getting corrupted by capability that arrived without the reps.* The work was never the obstacle — **the work is what protects you from the power.**

## 2. The grim ladder: earn → sign

**Keeper:** there's a ladder of how you get power. *Earn it* (walk the brutal path — still yourself, suffering but real) is one rung; *sign for it* (trade servitude for handed power — the Covenant) is the rung above, and worse, because you stop earning and start signing. The one who earned his monstrous strength is more **tragic-but-intact** than the ones who just signed and became puppets. Power *handed* (not walked-to) is the install at its most total.

## 3. The Covenant catches the idealist too

**Keeper:** the bargain-for-power catches you whether you reach for **power** (the obvious trap) *or* for **liberation** (the subtle one). The idealist who tries to free people *by joining the system and signing its contract* gets owned by the contract — **you can't get free by signing the oppressor's pact; the means contaminate the end.** (Ties to `let-structure-emerge-dont-blueprint-it`: joining the standardizing system to do the freeing thing kills the freeing thing.)

## 4. The immunity condition: be whole, have no hook

**Keeper:** the corruption needs a **hook in you** — the craving for unearned power, the willingness to sign, the envy that wants to surpass. The immune one is **whole**: power that's **earned and un-bought** (his own, walked-to, not handed; `freedom-of-movement-is-mastery`) *and* he **wants nothing the offer sells.** You can't corrupt with the offer of power someone who doesn't crave power; can't bind with a bargain someone who won't sign; can't weaponize the envy-to-surpass in someone not trying to surpass. **The un-corruptible one has no unearned hunger for what the devil sells** — so there's nothing to hook.
