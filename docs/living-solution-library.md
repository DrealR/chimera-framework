# The Living Solution Library

> **Classification:** current provenance-preserving knowledge and curriculum design. A “living solution” is a documented path through a real problem, not universal advice, proof of causality, or authority over another person's conditions.

## The scarce unit

Generic advice removes the path that gives a solution its usable boundaries.

The more valuable unit is:

> **A problem a body encountered, what it tried, what failed, what helped, under which conditions, what changed, and who else could reproduce or challenge the result.**

AI can generalize and translate this path. A lived body supplies provenance, constraint, consequence, and reasons not to overgeneralize.

## Entry schema

```text
Problem and body:
Who lived it:
Consent and privacy boundary:
Situation and relevant conditions:
What the problem felt like from inside:
What was tried first:
What failed or caused harm:
What eventually helped:
What mechanism is observed versus inferred:
What resources and relationships were required:
What changed and how it was measured:
Who else tested it:
Where it did not transfer:
Risks and contraindications:
Current confidence:
Date / version / return condition:
```

An entry remains a path report until independent evidence supports a broader claim.

## Evidence ladder

| Level | What may be said |
|-------|------------------|
| **Witness** | This person reports experiencing and trying this. |
| **Documented change** | A before/after difference was recorded, with known limitations. |
| **Repeated locally** | The path helped more than once in similar conditions. |
| **Transferred** | Another body reproduced useful parts under stated conditions. |
| **Domain-supported** | Appropriate research or professional practice supports the mechanism. |

Do not let AI rewrite a witness report into domain-supported certainty.

## AI's bounded role

AI may:

- identify recurring conditions and contrasts;
- translate an entry for another age or language;
- generate questions and rival explanations;
- compare paths without merging their differences;
- propose a tool, checklist, lesson, or experiment;
- retrieve domain guidance for qualified review;
- flag missing evidence, consent, or safety information.

AI may not:

- invent the walked history;
- remove failed attempts to make the path look clean;
- expose a source body beyond consent;
- convert one person's health, grief, financial, legal, or safety experience into prescriptive advice;
- hide uncertainty behind fluent synthesis;
- detach a lesson from the body and conditions that warranted it.

## SPARK curriculum use

A student can interview a consenting family or community member about a bounded practical problem, preserve the path, and build a small tool that helps another real person test one transferable part.

Examples may include:

- a practice routine for a musical passage;
- a checklist learned from running a local event;
- a budgeting worksheet reviewed by a qualified adult;
- a communication aid requested by its intended user;
- a study tool derived from a learner's own tested routine;
- a small-business workflow documented with the owner.

Sensitive health, trauma, immigration, family conflict, abuse, finance, or legal stories require stronger safeguarding and may not be appropriate student collection bodies.

SPARK's public Sky treats a build given to a real person as a star. A Living Solution entry can preserve the human lineage behind such a build without claiming that publication itself proves benefit. [Spark Builders: The Builder's Brain](https://sparkbuilders.org/)

## Return and retirement

A library becomes dead advice when entries never receive later consequence. Every entry needs:

- a return date;
- reports from users or affected bodies;
- visible corrections and disagreement;
- version lineage;
- a way to withdraw consent or redact sensitive details;
- retirement when conditions change or harm emerges.

The archive should preserve that an earlier path existed without continuing to recommend a superseded path.

## Library scan

1. Is the source body and consent boundary visible?
2. Are observation, interpretation, and domain claim separated?
3. Are failure and cost preserved?
4. Are transfer conditions explicit?
5. Can another body challenge or reproduce it?
6. Has AI expanded the path without manufacturing lineage?
7. Did a real recipient become more capable?
8. What later evidence changed the entry?
9. Should the path remain active, be bounded further, or retire?

## Carry line

> **Preserve not only what helped, but who walked the problem, what failed, which conditions mattered, what another body could reproduce, and what later consequence forced the lesson to change.**
