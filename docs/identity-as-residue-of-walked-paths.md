# Identity Is the Residue of Walked Paths

Writes happen during traversal — in friction, in the gap between reaching and getting. Zero friction means zero write surface: nothing is inscribed because there was no interior passage to inscribe it during.

This inverts the usual worry about handing people solutions. A person handed every answer is not being *protected from* struggle; they are being *denied the medium their self would have been written in*. The harm isn't softness. It's that nobody gets authored.

## The division of labor

**Closed problems** have pre-existing answers. The endpoints belong to the totality — anyone who works the problem converges on the same place, and converging on it costs nothing of the self. The quadratic formula, the chords of a standard song, the config syntax: these are common property, and reaching them quickly is fine.

**Paths belong to bodies.** A path does not exist until a particular walker walks it. Two people who learn the same theorem own different walks to it, and the walks — not the theorem — are what wrote them.

So individuality is not threatened by machines answering closed questions. It is threatened by exactly one move: **laundering open problems as closed ones** — accepting a machine's median answer to a question that had no destination until a walker authored one. What this thing is for, who it serves, what it should feel like, what gets refused: these have no pre-existing answer to converge on. Taking the median there doesn't save time. It substitutes someone else's (no one's) authorship for yours, and what arrives is a median body wearing your name.

## Consequences

- The fear "AI will make everyone the same" is mis-aimed. Convergence on closed answers was always the norm and never erased anyone. Watch instead for open questions being silently treated as closed — that's where sameness actually enters.
- The opposite error is just as real: walking closed roads out of principle is not integrity, it's waste ([walk/teleport criteria](walk-teleport-criteria.md), guard two: friction is not sacred).
- For builders: the open stretches of a build are small — often an hour against the machine's minutes — but they are the identity-bearing stretches. Walk them first, by hand. That's [the seed protocol](the-seed-protocol.md).
- For teachers and parents: the kid version of this whole document is one sentence — *the friction is where the self gets written* (see [speedrunning life](speedrunning-life.md) for the teaching frame).
