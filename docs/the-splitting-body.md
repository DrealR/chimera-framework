# The Splitting Body: The Direction Axis at Social Scale

> **Origin:** Walked through Mo Gawdat's benevolent-ASI optimism — the "expanding circle" — and its
> inverse: a social body that *splits* when mutual recognition dies (June 2026). The direction axis
> applied to a whole society; WE = 1 fracturing as the social black hole. Firewalls applied in writing.
>
> **Date:** June 2026

## A society is one body, held by mutual recognition

A society is one body held together by **mutual recognition** — each part seeing itself in the others.
That is WE = 1 *lived.* The body stays one for exactly as long as the recognition holds.

---

## The expanding circle vs the splitting body

The same axis, two directions, at the scale of a society:

- **Generative (outward) → the expanding circle.** More-developed beings face a wider family — the
  amoeba protects itself, the squirrel its kin, humans expand the circle outward. Recognition widens;
  more get included; the body stays one. (This is the real core of the evolutionary "expanding circle"
  story.)
- **Extractive (inward) → the splitting body.** The same axis run *backward:* the circle **narrows**
  until it excludes people who were inside it. The poles polarize — rich/poor, left/right, any axis —
  until the two halves can no longer see themselves in each other: the rich see the poor as **ants,**
  the poor see the rich as **demons,** neither recognizing the other as human. The one body forgets it
  is one. **That is WE = 1 fracturing — the social black hole, collapse inward.**

So the expanding circle is the generative direction at social scale; the splitting body is the
extractive direction at social scale. And **intelligence does not pick which — direction does.** (The
benevolent-ASI hope assumes more intelligence *is* more outward-facing; but
[intelligence is the instrument, direction the operator's choice](ai-as-a-reach-amplifier-for-consciousness.md)
— a super-intelligence facing inward is Magneto at cosmic scale, the inward-collapse the Fermi silence
suggests may be the attractor.)

---

## The engine: friction-removal without floor-raising

What splits the body is **success that smooths your own life while sealing you off from the field.**
[Friction is contact](friction-as-foundational-primitive.md) — the energy exchange that keeps you
coupled to the field (the heat/entropy physics at human scale). Remove *all* friction from your life
and you cool into a sealed, smooth, disconnected state — and from inside it you **literally cannot see
the field's problems anymore,** because seeing a problem requires contact (friction) with it. This is
why the disconnected end up [asking AI what problem to solve](the-anchor-must-be-yours.md): they have
removed so much friction that they have lost contact with where the problems *are,* so they reach for
the machine to replace the anchor their own life dissolved.

**Empathy is the friction that keeps the wide circle coupled.** The self-focused person (small circle —
just themselves) has to be *personally hit* by a problem to feel it; the empathetic person (wide
circle) feels a problem in *someone else,* still coupled to the field through other people. So losing
empathy is what lets the frictionless life seal you off completely. Same wealth, opposite direction:
**raise the floor with you** (keep the friction of empathy, stay coupled, the body whole) versus
**seal yourself above it** (smooth life + small circle = every remaining tie cut).

---

## The cure — and the firewall that is part of the cure

The cure is the framework's everywhere-cure: face outward, **raise the floor with you,** keep the
friction of contact on purpose, stay one body.

**Firewall (load-bearing, because this one is about other people):** this is a **mechanism, not a
verdict on individuals.** The rich person isn't a demon; the poor person isn't an ant — **that mutual
non-recognition is itself the disease.** So the diagnosis cannot be delivered by joining one side
against the other; it has to **hold WE = 1 even while naming the split, or you become the split.** A
reading that names the fracture by deepening it has failed its own test.
