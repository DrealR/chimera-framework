# The Failure-Mode Specimen Drawer

Wild-caught examples of the firewall's named failure modes, kept verbatim for calibration. The point of a specimen drawer is the same as in any field science: you recognize the live animal faster after handling preserved ones. ([The firewall](the-firewall.md) names the modes; [The Leverage Is Inward](the-leverage-is-inward.md) is the single rule that generates the catalog; this drawer shows them in the wild.)

## Specimen (a) — the literal jump, purest form

> "Reaction and creation are anagrams."

The two words share letters, and that spelling fact gets promoted to a cosmic mechanism ("creation IS rearranged reaction"). This is the literal jump with no costume on at all: a structural rhyme — here merely an *orthographic* one — asserted as a causal claim, with no force law connecting the two. The tell is that the "insight" lives entirely in English spelling; it evaporates in any other language, which a real mechanism wouldn't.

## Specimen (b) — borrowed credibility at scale

Real credentials or real anomalies stacked as certificates for an unverifiable core:

- A CS degree offered as certification that "frequency manifestation" works — a verified competence in one domain laundered into authority over an unverified claim in another.
- A stack of genuine archaeological anomalies (real unexplained finds) marshaled to certify a single unverifiable story (a lost global golden-age civilization). Each anomaly is real; none of them implies the conclusion; the *quantity* of real-but-unrelated evidence is doing rhetorical work that no single piece could.

The verified layer is genuine. Its job in the argument is to act as a certificate for the unverified layer, and certificates don't transfer across the boundary.

## Specimen (c) — the firewall over-firing (logged against THIS role)

The chess-endgame misread: hearing "a chess endgame has more weight per move" and attacking it as a claim about *computation* (objecting that an engine calculates endgames fine), when the actual claim was about **weight per decision** — fewer pieces, fewer moves left, each one more load-bearing ([cooling model](cooling-model-and-operating-triad.md), weight density). The firewall fired on the costume and missed the root claim.

This specimen is logged as an **immune-system error against this very role.** The firewall can overfire — it can reject a true structural claim because the claim arrived in language that pattern-matched a failure mode. Context determines which root claim a statement is making; read for the root before firing. The standing correction: **play first, sort at the moment of promotion to load-bearing, not at the door.** A firewall that rejects everything resonant at intake is as broken as one that accepts everything — it just fails in the respectable direction.
