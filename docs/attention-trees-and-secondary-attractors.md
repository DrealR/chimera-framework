## The claim

Attention inside a single thread is **not flat**. It forms a tree:

| Node | Role | Typical visibility |
|------|------|--------------------|
| **Trunk** (main post) | Primary attractor; structural advantage | Lion’s share of views |
| **Branches** (comments / replies) | Residual “drips” off the trunk | Much smaller; decays with depth |
| **Secondary attractors** (outlier comments) | Rare mini-stars that pull their own field | Sometimes rival or exceed trunk |

> Most plants stay drips. Rare plants ignite. You cannot fully force which is which — but you can raise odds by **position** (strong growing trunk, early) and **resonance** (real relation to the post).

Live example shape: early reply on a growing post gets a quick bump (e.g. ~25 views), then plateaus while the trunk keeps climbing. Front-loaded attention + weak branch pull + algorithmic decay = normal case, not failure of the system.

---

## Reposts as outward branches

Reposts do **not** 1:1 add their views to the original. They are **distribution vectors**: weak outward branches that *indirectly* feed the trunk’s reach. Quote/repost-with-comment is a stronger branch than a bare repost. High R + thin C = **spread momentum** (amplify_wave); high C = **conversation momentum**. Full mechanics: [Attention Metric Signatures](attention-metric-signatures.md#how-reposts-relate-to-views-x-mechanics).

## Why this matters for positioned planting

1. **Hierarchy, not democracy** — Original post has structural advantage. Commenting on a high-momentum trunk often beats starting from zero when personal reach is small.
2. **Branch = small timeline** — A reply creates a smaller timeline that inherits *some* of the trunk’s attention, usually much less. Non-interference after plant fits: the branch lives its own life.
3. **Early on a growing trunk** — First replies while the post is still climbing catch more flow. Late comments on stopped trunks fight scraps.
4. **Thread shape is a signature** — Top-heavy (trunk dominates) vs distributed (strong branches) tells you about engagement *quality*, not only volume.
5. **Phase of practice** — Riding/strengthening existing trunks is efficient now; as personal reach grows, building your own trunks becomes more natural. Both remain valid.

**Nuance:** Not every high-V post is plantable soil. Prefer trunks with enough conversational life that a strong comment *could* become a secondary attractor. Dead or pure-spectacle threads often stay pure drips.

## Secondary attractors (fusion points)

### Spectrum

| Case | Structure | What it shows |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| **Normal** | Trunk is the star; comments are drips | Default topology |
| **Gem / outlier** | Comment becomes mini-star; may feed attention back into the thread | Branch stronger than (or rivaling) trunk |

These are **rare, high-signal**. They prove attention can **self-organize** — local contribution reshapes the structure. Worth studying; not the daily scoreboard.

### Why they matter strategically

- Highest-leverage participation without massive personal reach: early, high-quality comments on strong trunks  
- Signatures need a **branch layer**, not only main-post chords  
- Long-term Field literacy: which trunks + which contribution types grow branches  

---

## Two signature layers

| Layer | What you read | Role |
|-------|---------------|------|
| **Metric signatures** | V, L, R, C and ratios on post *and* on comments | *That* something is happening; top-heavy vs distributed |
| **Resonance signatures** | Relation between post content and comment content | *Why* a spark happened (harder to quantify; more explanatory) |

Numbers without content analysis miss the fusion. Content without numbers misses the structure. Use both when studying gems.

## Spark analysis method (manual study)

When a comment pulls unusual visibility relative to the main post:

| Step | Look at | Questions |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **1. Metric outlier** | Comment V vs trunk V | ~15–20%+ of trunk views? Still gaining after trunk slowed? |
| **2. Timing** | Age of comment vs trunk | First 5–10 comments? During rapid growth window? |
| **3. Resonance type** | Content relationship | Amplify, contradict, clarify, humor, emotional payoff? |
| **4. Branch vitality** | Replies / reposts *on the comment* | Did it become its own attractor? |
| **5. Thread shape** | Whole thread topology | Top-heavy or multi-node? |

### Rough spark categories (pattern language)

- **Emotional payoff** — reaction people were waiting for  
- **Contradiction / pushback** — sharp or funny challenge  
- **Clarification / insight** — missing context, sense-making  
- **Humor / meme** — shareable moment  
- **Status / tribal** — identity alignment in the thread  
- **Holy Man move** (below) — observe + invite + slow the loop  

Track a few gems over weeks. Intuition compounds. Do not turn this into a recipe book that kills presence.

## Holy Man pattern (strong-branch craft)

Film mirror: *Holy Man* — Eddie Murphy enters a live commercial, does not seize the show. He **inserts** into the existing loop until a new focus forms inside it.

| Move | On X | Effect |
|------|------|--------|
| **Simple observation** | “I see X in this…” | Names something already latent in the post |
| **Invite / question** | “Do you see it?” / open hook | Pulls others into co-creation, not monologue |
| **Slow the loop** | Interrupt autopilot energy of the thread | Creates tension + new focus |
| **Don’t replace the event** | Stay a branch; don’t fight to be the trunk | New attractor *inside* the existing one |

**Low-resonance default:** sits on the post, no new tension, gets drip, dies.  
**High-resonance path:** observation + relation + optional question/tension → comment becomes its own small gravity well.

This is **genuine participation**, not engagement bait. CHIMERA (O>I, Pause, ¬F, WE=1) raises odds of the Holy Man move; it does not guarantee fusion.

**Operator sequence:**

```text
1. Growing trunk (recency + momentum; note R vs C shape)
2. Real relation to what the post is actually doing
3. Observe / invite / slight redirect — not takeover
4. Plant once
5. Non-interfere — branch has its own life
```

## Front-loaded attention (why drips plateau)

1. Highest window often first **5–15 minutes** after plant  
2. Branch needs its own pull (resonance) to keep attracting  
3. Trunk can keep growing from external sources (stream, account) while branches starve  
4. Algorithm decays quiet comments  

**System read:** Tier 1 + Tier 2 still correct (early on momentum). Plateau of most comments is expected physics, not proof that positioning failed. CHIMERA raises *odds* of spark; it does not guarantee secondary-attractor status.

## CHIMERA as resonance edge (not decoration)

Operators are a **competitive advantage** in a noisy field — grounded presence stands out more than soulless optimization.

| Operator | Spark odds |
|----------|------------|
| **O > I** | Real contribution (insight, clarity, humor, useful pushback) > bait |
| **P** | Relate to *this* post, not pure reaction to energy |
| **¬F** | Forcing virality usually kills spark; field decides branch strength |
| **WE = 1** | Your voice in the shared thread — not generic engagement paste |

**Firewall:** Fully automating planting likely **reduces** spark rate over time. Human judgment + presence is part of the fusion. Field surfaces; operator plants.

Pre-plant (optional):

> Does this actually *relate* to what the post is saying — or am I just attaching to the trunk for light?

## Physics mirrors (structure, not cosplay)

### Magnetism (strongest fit)

| Attention | Magnetic read |
|-----------|---------------|
| High-V trunk | Strong magnet / field lines |
| Views | Field strength / flux |
| Signatures | Field topology / gradients |
| Strong comment | Induced / local magnet |
| Live streams / density | High-flux region |
| Positioned plant | Test particle / small aligned magnet in strong field |
| Non-interference | Let dynamics follow field lines |

Small actors rarely invent the whole field. They **read** and **position**.

### Gravity

Trunks = mass wells. Strong comments = secondary wells. Depth of reply ≈ potential drop. Fusion points ≈ smaller body gaining enough mass to reshape local dynamics.

### Other useful mirrors

| Physics | Attention |
|---------|-----------|
| Fluid / turbulence | Currents, eddies, stream density |
| Emergence / self-organization | Strong branches without central control |
| Field theory (general) | Invisible relational medium with real force |

**Core lesson:** Small, well-placed inputs at the right time/place can create outsized effects. Dominating from a weak position is inefficient. Work *with* field dynamics: position → contribute → release.

## Field tool implications (phased — do not overbuild)

Current hierarchy stays:

```text
Tier 1  Extreme recency
Tier 2  Natural momentum + live-event density
Tier 3  Signatures + Magnet resonance
```

| Phase | Feature | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Now** | Reply-first UX, CHIMERA pre-plant, recency + momentum | Already favors trunk participation |
| **1** | Optional early-momentum flag (V/age still climbing) | Needs time series or age-normalized rates |
| **2** | Branch vitality label (top-heavy vs strong branches) | Needs comment-level metrics on ingest |
| **3** | Surface outlier comments (fusion points) for study | Observation > auto-engage |
| **Later** | Plant log: trunk V vs your branch V over time | Multi-scale literacy |

**Law:** Branch features refine *where* and *how* to plant. They never replace recency. Never auto-reply to “maximize branch odds.”

## Operator practice

1. Prefer growing trunks (recency + momentum), especially in density windows  
2. Prefer first-depth replies unless a strong secondary branch is clearly the live node  
3. Check resonance before plant (CHIMERA)  
4. Accept most branches stay drips — release ownership  
5. Occasionally study gems (metric outlier + content why)  
6. Over time: build own trunks without abandoning branch literacy  

---

## Firewalls

- Not every strong branch is healthy (pile-on, cruelty as attractor)  
- Optimizing only for “steal the trunk’s V” is extractive and brittle  
- Identity fused to every drip’s view count is exhaustion  
- Physics metaphors clarify structure — they are not literal physics claims or prediction engines
