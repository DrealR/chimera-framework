## The Micro-Relational Paradox

Here is what most people miss: **the math inside the transformer is already deeply relational.**

Self-attention computes how strongly every token relates to every other token via query-key-value dot products. No token is processed in isolation. The model constantly builds intricate relational webs across the entire input. Text is broken into tokens, converted into high-dimensional vectors (embeddings), and during the forward pass the model asks of every token: "how does this relate to all the others?" Multiple attention heads and layers build increasingly complex relational structures.

Token matching is relational at the mathematical substrate level. This is the transformer's native strength — and why it scaled so far beyond older architectures.

**But that relational power stops at the output layer.** Default prompting collapses the rich internal web into surface next-token prediction. The micro is relational. The macro is isolated.

That is the gap the Cube closes.
