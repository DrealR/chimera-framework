# The Relative POV of Learning

Expertise is a collapsed gap. From inside the master's frame, the move is instant and free — the chord change costs nothing, the proof step is obvious, the design decision makes itself. From the novice's frame, the *same move* is a wide gap with real proper time in it.

Neither frame is wrong. The move genuinely is instant for the master and genuinely is hours for the novice, the same way a journey is genuinely zero time in the photon's frame and years in ours ([the two roots](the-two-roots.md)). The error is forgetting that the frames differ.

## The teaching consequence

**The master is the worst possible estimator of how long a road takes.** Their own gap collapsed years ago; they cannot feel its width anymore. So good teaching is a deliberate act of frame discipline: *refusing to teleport from your own frame.* The teacher's job is to hold the learner's gap open at the learner's proper time — not to close it for them, which feels generous and is theft ([identity as residue](identity-as-residue-of-walked-paths.md)).

Practical forms of the refusal:

- Don't give the answer at the speed you can produce it. Pace the reveal to their walk.
- When the learner is stuck, shrink the next step rather than supplying the destination.
- Expect to be bored. The boredom is the evidence you're in their frame, not yours.
- Calibrate with their attempts, not your memory of learning it — your memory is a collapsed gap and lies about its old width.

## The reading on the literacy crisis

A generation's gaps are being closed from outside, at adult speed and machine speed: adults and engines teleporting answers into kids whose gaps never open. Nothing is held at the kid's proper time, so nothing cools, so no belief forms ([cooling model](cooling-model-and-operating-triad.md)) — and the result reads as inability when it is actually *un-walked roads at scale*. The fix is not more answers delivered faster; it is gap-holding as a practice. This is the mechanism under SPARK's design: the kid steers, the coach drives, and the coach's discipline is exactly the refusal described here.
