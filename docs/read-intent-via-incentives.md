## 1. Read intent via incentives

Beyond *what is this doing*, ask *who profits from you believing it.* **Keeper:** the proclamation-makers selling a tool (vendor figures hyping their own product) inflate; the voices with no skin in that hype give the measured take. Weight the measured over the proclaimers — not cynically, *structurally.* This is `read-the-intent-not-the-realness` applied to the source: the framing of a claim is partly a function of what the speaker is selling, so the incentive is part of the read.

## 2. Strip the hype, keep the real bone

Vague non-actionable proclamations + manufactured urgency ("keep up or be left behind") + experts walking back their own claims weeks later are the tells of an inflated frame. **Keeper:** strip it (`strip-to-the-skeleton`) and the bone is usually small and real — e.g. an agentic "loop" is just a scheduled task that spawns sub-agents with a human approving merges; the "new paradigm" is the meat, the cron-job-orchestrating-sub-agents is the bone. **Firewall:** "it's all slop, no experts" can tip into the liar's-dividend / paranoia-trap (everything-might-be-fake → dismiss everything). Keep the real technique; firewall the keep-up-or-die framing.

## 3. The compounding-error math (why the operator stays in the loop)

**Keeper:** 95% correct per iteration over 10 iterations isn't 95% — it's **0.95^10 ≈ 60%.** Unsupervised loops *compound* errors the longer they run, so the human-in-the-loop is **mathematically necessary**, and the working forms are the **bounded for-each** and the **review loop**, not the unbounded while-loop. This is the mathematical proof under `frozen-stockpile-loses-to-the-live-loop` and the career boomerang: AI compounds errors over long autonomous runs, so the experienced operator supervising output is *load-bearing.* (Ship faster than you can detect defects and every rollback contends with conflicting changes.)

## 4. The durable value (and SPARK)

**Keeper:** the durable skill is the **judgment underneath** (the trade-offs, the supervision, knowing bounded-beats-unbounded) — what can't be shortcut and what the hype can't sell. *Play the instrument, don't swallow the hype.* SPARK teaches the same: read the intent (and the incentive), keep the operator in the loop, build the judgment the buzzword can't replace.
