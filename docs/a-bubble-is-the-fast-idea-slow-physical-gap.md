## 0. The claim, stated once

**A bubble is the fast-idea / slow-physical gap made financial. Money moves at idea-speed (everyone sees the true idea instantly); value accrues at physical-speed (the build-out crawls). The bubble is the pressure in the gap before the physical catches up — which is why the truest ideas make the biggest bubbles — and the crash is the gap closing, not the idea failing.**

---

## 1. Grantham's inversion (the keeper)

Bubbles form around the **truest** ideas, not the false ones. Railroads were real and changed the world — the railroad bubble still wiped people out. The internet was real — the dot-com bust still erased ~80% of the Nasdaq. Amazon ran 6–7× in '99, crashed ~92%, then inherited retail. **The realness of the idea is not protection against the crash — it is the cause of it:** realness attracts the over-investment that guarantees the bust. Most people reason "it's real, therefore safe." The truth is the opposite.

## 2. The gap explains the mechanism

The idea diffuses at light-speed (everyone sees AI is real, instantly, the way everyone saw the railroad). The physical build-out crawls (fabs, power, profitable products take years). So **money floods in at idea-speed while value accrues at physical-speed, and the bubble is the pressure that builds in that gap.** The clearer the idea, the faster the money outruns the slow build → the truest ideas make the biggest bubbles. Same structure as the guitar (knowing races ahead, hands catch up slow) and compute (research diffuses fast, fabs crawl).

**Keeper:** the bubble is the fast/slow gap under financial pressure — a specific instance of the framework's core unification.

## 3. The cleaner statement (past Grantham's own)

**The crash isn't the idea failing — it's the gap closing.** The price ran ahead of the slow physical reality and falls back to where the physical actually is; afterward the real idea keeps building at its true pace (Amazon inheriting retail out of the wreckage). So *"the idea survives the wreckage"* is not a happy accident — it's structural: **the idea was never the bubble; the price-ahead-of-physical was the bubble.** That's why the idea survives the wreckage every time.

## 4. The firewall

The mechanism is the keeper. **Specific calls** (a precise % decline, sell-this, timing) are one positioned perma-bear's view; a forecaster who predicts many crashes is eventually right, but that is not timing. Hold the mechanism tight and the specific/timed calls loose. **This is positioned financial opinion, not fact, and not advice** — per the money-not-in-the-decision-function discipline, the useful part is the structural understanding of how the gap creates and resolves bubbles, not the action items. The honest meta-point is the real gold: the big firms won't warn you because it's bad business, so *look at the data yourself* — which is the operator move (see `concentration-vs-distribution`).
