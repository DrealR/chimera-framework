# Single-Fitness-Function Failure

> **Classification:** system-design mechanism and objective-audit practice. This extends collapsed-objective optimization by naming the governance failure: a legitimate partial value acquires total write-access to the larger body.

## The mechanism

Money, coherence, engagement, efficiency, safety, pleasure, order, and growth can each serve a body locally. None is a sufficient total objective.

```text
partial value
→ privileged metric
→ feedback and resources bend toward the metric
→ unmeasured capacities atrophy
→ the metric becomes identity
→ the body optimizes against its own life
```

The failure is not optimization itself. It is **single-fitness-function governance**: one measurement becomes the hill every subsystem must climb, even where that measurement no longer represents the host body's health.

## The objective is the hidden operator

When evaluating a model, platform, market, institution, or personal habit, ask:

> **What objective is supplying the gradient, who selected it, and where does its authority stop?**

The same technical mechanism can form different bodies under different objectives. A recommendation system trained on attention, learning, friendship, outrage, or revenue is not climbing the same hill.

## Common specimens

- engagement replaces public understanding;
- test score replaces learning;
- coherence replaces viable disagreement;
- safety replaces agency and experimentation;
- growth replaces maintenance and succession;
- pleasure replaces nourishment;
- money replaces incommensurable values;
- one successful model trace becomes the entire terrain.

The deadly-sins carrier is useful at this boundary: appetite, pride, anger, rest, desire, accumulation, and comparison are not alien forces. They are local functions that become destructive when installed as total government.

## Design correction

A living optimizer needs:

1. **Bounded objectives** — each metric has a declared body, scale, and domain.
2. **Plural evaluation** — different bands may disagree without one silently owning the score.
3. **Protected variance** — local centers retain alternate strategies, evidence, and voice.
4. **Consequential tests** — reality can contradict the proxy.
5. **Revision and decay** — old weights can weaken and objectives can expire.
6. **Operator veto** — no subsystem receives irreversible total write-access.
7. **Maintenance and rest** — the body can stop climbing without being treated as failure.

> **A system that cannot tolerate viable error cannot evolve.**

## AI and multi-agent implication

Do not ask a multi-agent body to maximize coherence. Ask it to preserve:

- differentiated viewpoints;
- limited budgets and scopes;
- consequential disagreement;
- independent evidence channels;
- explicit stop conditions;
- revision after contact with real tasks.

Coherence should emerge at the resolution required for action. It should not become a command to erase every difference that makes correction possible.

## Objective audit

```text
1. Name the host body.
2. Name the local function being optimized.
3. Name the metric standing in for that function.
4. Locate who can write, revise, and stop the objective.
5. List capacities the metric cannot see.
6. Inspect whether local gains export cost through the Night body.
7. Add an independent evaluation and a real settlement test.
8. Set expiry, review, and recovery conditions.
```

## Firewalls

- Multiple metrics can still collapse into one hidden objective when one beneficiary chooses and weights them all.
- Disagreement is not automatically valuable; it must remain reality-responsive.
- A proxy is not useless because it is incomplete. Keep it inside its membrane.
- Balance is not an excuse to avoid decisive action under clear evidence.
- This is a relational system-design lens, not a replacement for domain-specific optimization theory.
