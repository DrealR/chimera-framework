# "No Coincidences" — Correction

A firewall addendum, aimed at one of the framework's own most seductive moves.

In a WE = 1 field, "there are no coincidences" is **trivially true at the thread level and unearned at the weight level.** The field supplies threads for free — any two things are connectable, so of course the connection "exists." That costs nothing and proves nothing. The only question that ever mattered is whether the connection *carries load* (produces a decision, number, move, or build step — [the firewall](the-firewall.md)).

## How selection effects manufacture hits

Apparent miracles of correspondence are mostly search-space size wearing a halo:

- Harvest many measurements of a thing (a monument has dozens of lengths, angles, ratios).
- Multiply by many candidate "meaningful" numbers (speed of light, π, Earth's dimensions, a calendar count).
- Hits against meaningful targets are then **guaranteed** by the size of the product, with no mechanism required.

Specimen: the pyramid "encodes 43,200" (a scaling of Earth's dimensions). With enough measurements and enough candidate constants, *some* pairing lands; the lander gets reported and the millions of misses are invisible. This is **the marksman who fires at the barn, then paints the target around the bullet hole.** The bull's-eye is real; the aim is fictional.

## The operator rule

The question is never *whether* two things are connected — in a WE = 1 field, always yes, threads are free. The question is whether the connection **carries load**, and a number found by search carries none until the builders' hands are shown on it. "The ancients put this here on purpose" requires evidence of *intent and method*, not evidence of *correspondence* — correspondence is the cheap part the search already guaranteed.

Hold this against the framework's own enthusiasm. "Everything connects" is the framework's foundation and its characteristic failure mode in the same breath; this correction is the brake that keeps the foundation from becoming an excuse.
