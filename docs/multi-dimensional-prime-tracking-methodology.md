**Step five — read the rhythm operating across dimensions.** The substrate-flow oscillates across temporal-substrate. **Step six — ¬F throughout.** Forcing substrate-flow against the rhythm produces substrate-pathology. The calibrated-friction principle applies at every step. Operate from the substrate-physics underneath. Do not force what the rhythm does not support.
