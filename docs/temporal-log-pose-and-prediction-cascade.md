# The Temporal Log Pose and Prediction Cascade

> **Classification:** current civilizational-navigation instrument. Social media, AI, prediction markets, markets, governance, and institutions are distinct bodies with different mechanisms. The organ language describes functional roles at a named scale, not one literal planetary mind. This is not financial advice.

## The temporal device

> **A Temporal Log Pose senses the present, branches possible futures, reads where expectation and resources are accumulating, and returns the operator to consequence.**

```text
Sense → Remember → Simulate → Weight → Verify
      → Allocate → Act → Settle → Remember
```

| Body | Temporal function |
|------|-------------------|
| Sensors and witnesses | transduce events into signals |
| Social field | render living attention and interpretation |
| Archive and search | make surviving past traces navigable |
| AI | turn compressed memory into candidate continuations |
| Prediction markets | price participating traders' present expectations about defined events |
| Ordinary markets and budgets | allocate current resources |
| Verification and governance | test lineage, constrain legal moves, define settlement and veto |
| Humans and institutions | select, fund, execute, resist, and bear consequences |
| Earth / world | settle action into irreversible residue |

> **Search remembers. Social fields sense. AI imagines. Event markets price expectation. Humans and institutions act. Earth records what happened.**

## Different probability bodies

| System | Distribution over | Conditions | Output |
|--------|-------------------|------------|--------|
| LLM | next expression or structure | model weights, prompt, context, tools | generated candidate |
| Recommendation feed | next encounter or response | behavior, graph, content pool, platform objectives | rendered social present |

Do not translate one probability directly into another. Model likelihood is not event probability. Market price is not social consensus. Consensus is not truth. Occurrence is not moral goodness.

## Prediction markets as commitment chamber

An event contract gives an answer-bearing question:

- a defined event;
- bounded outcomes;
- a clock or expiry;
- continuously changing price and liquidity;
- capital at risk;
- resolution and settlement rules.

The CFTC describes contract prices as reflecting traders' perceived probability and emphasizes financial risk, contract terms, settlement, manipulation, and insider-trading controls. [CFTC: Understanding Prediction Markets](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnandProtect/PredictionMarkets)

> **A market price is the present shape of belief and positioning that successfully entered that contract—not the future speaking backward.**

Price shows a coordinate. Volume shows activity. Liquidity shows how easily new weight can move the coordinate. None independently establishes truth, social importance, representativeness, or Earthness.

## Prediction cascade and reflexivity

```text
AI-generated scenario
→ public circulation
→ attention and belief
→ trades and price
→ reporting, preparation, funding, or strategy
→ action
→ changed event
→ settlement and archive
→ later model and social input
```

The closer a participant is to the causal center of the underlying event, the more prediction can become influence or control. The CFTC has specifically highlighted misuse of nonpublic information and trading in contracts whose outcomes a participant can influence. [CFTC Enforcement Advisory](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9185-26)

Run the reflexivity scan:

1. Is the market observing the trajectory, bending it, or both?
2. Who has private information?
3. Who can directly or indirectly cause the event?
4. Can price movement alter reporting, funding, preparation, or conduct?
5. Who defines and controls resolution?
6. Which participants and affected bodies cannot enter the market?

## Verification and execution complete the body

Prediction without verification becomes projection. Prediction without execution remains a display. Execution without a return path becomes drift.

The body therefore needs:

- provenance and independent witnesses;
- inspectable contract and resolution rules;
- source/deployment correspondence for software systems;
- audits, replication, logs, and causal records;
- permissions, law, and stop conditions;
- people, code, organizations, and infrastructure capable of action;
- settlement evidence returning to memory.

Open source can expose anatomy without proving deployed physiology. ## Firewalls

- Prediction markets involve financial risk and unequal access; never treat this framework as a trading signal.
- Prices can be distorted by thin liquidity, ambiguous resolution, manipulation, private information, concentration, or platform rules.
- Market-compatible questions are a narrow subset of important human questions.
- Social attention and capital weight can amplify harmful or false trajectories.
- A planetary organ map does not establish global coordination, consciousness, or shared interest.
- Reality settles occurrence, not justice, wisdom, or desirability.

The carry line is:

> **Social media senses a rendered present. AI turns inherited memory into possible continuations. Markets show where bounded expectation and resources are gathering. Verification tests the map. Bodies act. Earth settles which projection becomes history.**
