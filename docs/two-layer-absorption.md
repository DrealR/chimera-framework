# Two-Layer Absorption

How to take in any source — a book, a talk, a person, a tradition — without either swallowing it whole or rejecting it whole.

Every person is a trained model. Every conclusion arrives wearing its walker: the path that made *this* thread feel heavy to *this* mind is baked into how they state it. The skill is grading two separate layers and never collapsing them.

## The two layers

**The CLAIM is graded by the world.** Evidence, transfer, the firewall ([the firewall](the-firewall.md)). Does it predict? Does it port to cases the speaker never mentioned? Does it produce an actual decision, number, move, or build step? The speaker's biography is *irrelevant* here.

**The WEIGHTING is graded by the path.** *Why does this thread feel heavy to this walker* — that question's whole answer is biography. The wound, the obsession, the formative case, the temperament. This layer explains the *emphasis*, the *charge*, the *blind spots*. It tells you why they can see what they see and miss what they miss.

## The guard

**Origin does not grade truth.** This is the genetic fallacy, named and refused in both directions:

- A claim is not *false* because it came from a wound, a fringe, a flawed person, or a bad motive.
- A claim is not *true* because the walker is compelling, credentialed, scarred, or sincere.

Most people collapse the two layers — they let a moving story certify a claim, or let a claim's source disqualify it. Keeping them apart is the entire skill. You can hold "the weighting is pure projection" and "the claim is correct" at the same time, and you frequently must.

## The deeper note

Ideas do not transmit; they *re-train*. Every reader forks the model through their own weights — there is no clean copy, only a new fork wearing the reader's residue ([myth as storage](myth-as-storage.md) is the engineering response to exactly this fact). So when you absorb a source, you are not receiving its conclusion; you are retraining on its output, and what you end up holding is *your* fork. Grade accordingly: keep what survives world-grading, log the weighting as context, and own that the version you keep is now yours to defend.
