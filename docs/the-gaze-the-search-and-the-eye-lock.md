Both are the one-way / frozen inverse of the live, mutual eye-lock. Graduated from a core capture. Internal register; the quantum framing held off explicitly.*

## 0. The claim, stated once

**Looking-changes-the-thing is real — but through perception, not quantum physics (quantum "observation" is mindless physical interaction extracting information, not a conscious gaze; "consciousness causes collapse" is fringe). So being-seen changes only what has an interior to register it — which makes the gaze-response a test for a someone-home. And looking-someone-up collapses your own uncertainty (epistemic, not real) into a frozen snapshot of their past. Both the gaze-at-a-distance and the search are the one-way, frozen inverse of the eye-lock, which is mutual and live.**

---

## 1. Firewall: the quantum rhyme is a pun on "observe"

In QM, "observation" = any physical interaction that extracts information (a photon, a detector); the system collapses because it *interacted*, not because anyone was conscious of it. **Keeper:** the felt profundity ("looking defines reality") rides on a **pun** — conscious gaze vs physical measurement — and the slide *quantum-observer-effect → consciousness-shapes-reality* is the single most common move in the inflation/guru register. Drop the physics; the grounded versions are stronger.

## 2. The gaze tests for interiority (perception, not physics)

Being looked at genuinely changes a living thing — through **perception** (the Hawthorne effect; the cat shifts when you lock eyes), because it registers being-seen and responds (the recursive mutual modeling of `the-eye-lock-mutual-recognition`). **Keeper:** the cat-vs-tree difference is exactly whether there's an interior that registers the gaze — so *"does it change when I look at it?"* is a rough **test for a someone-home.** And the tree *not* responding is the tell that it's perception, not physics (if it were the quantum effect, the tree — which interacts with photons — would be affected too).

## 3. The search collapses your uncertainty into a frozen snapshot

Looking someone up collapses your **undefined-to-you** status-of-them into a definite reading — a good *structural* rhyme (querying-an-undefined-system → a defined reading), kept honest as mechanism, not consciousness. **Keeper:** the difference that keeps it clean — the quantum spread is *real* (collapses the world); the information spread is *epistemic* (the person is already somewhere; the search makes it definite *to you*). And the sharpest part: the reading is a **frozen snapshot, not the live present** (a last-known-location, even for the dead) — the collapse only ever gives you the frozen past (`frozen-stockpile-loses-to-the-live-loop`). You can look up where someone *was*; you can only *be with* where someone *is.*

## 4. Both are the inverse of the eye-lock

**Keeper:** the gaze-at-a-distance and the search are the inverse of the eye-lock on both axes — **one-way and frozen** (you collapse your uncertainty into a stale snapshot of their past; they don't know, can't look back) — where the eye-lock is **mutual and live** (two live presents recognizing each other now). The internet gives the first at infinite scale and starves the second: it hands you their past; presence is the only thing that gives you their now.
