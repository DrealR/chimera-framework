# Positioned Planting and Non-Interference

> **Origin:** July 2026 — seeds on the attention field; moving in silence vs whale moves; CHIMERA as seed integrity; observe propagation multi-scale; Magnet as soil sensor.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

The high-leverage move on a live attention layer is not maximum raw visibility. It is:

> **Plant a high-integrity seed in a fertile present-moment region, then step back and let the field respond.**

**Positioned planting** = quality of seed × quality of soil (signature + topic resonance) × timing.  
**Non-interference** = after plant, resist constant management that distorts natural propagation.

Visibility is still required for public existence. **Configuration of visibility** and **optionality after planting** matter more than volume alone.

---

## Chasing vs positioning

| Chasing visibility | Positioned planting |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Loud regions, competitive gravity | Fertile signatures, often moderate V early |
| Large obvious moves | Smaller well-placed seeds |
| Predictable but sticky effects | More pathways, less forced distortion |
| Optionality shrinks | Maneuverability preserved |
| Scoreboard identity | Field literacy + idea testing |

Whale-scale moves create strong pull and reduce freedom. Silence without any presence never registers. Art: **relational visibility** — enough light to participate where you care, not so much you warp the field you are trying to read.

---

## Four-step operator loop

```text
1. Recency window    — enter true present-moment layer (default 30 min; 15–60 range)
2. Live soil read    — optional momentum boost + signature/ratio + Magnet resonance
3. CHIMERA plant     — ground the seed; post/reply where soil + direction fit
4. Non-interference  — add value, move on; observe multi-scale; don’t manage old momentum
```

Extreme recency is the foundation. Signatures refine. CHIMERA quality + non-interference keep it from becoming growth hacking.

Then: refine ideas **and** field literacy from what actually propagated, twisted, or died.

---

## Why CHIMERA-quality seeds change the game

Most seeds are raw or low-coherence. A CHIMERA-grounded seed has more **structural integrity**:

- survives initial noise better  
- travels with clearer internal logic  
- returns cleaner feedback (what resonated vs collapsed)  
- tests higher-quality hypotheses about systems and humans  

This is idea-testing as science of the field, not only growth hacking.

## Non-interference (precise)

**Do:**

- Watch signatures evolve (early_momentum → discussion / amplify / hollow)  
- Note multi-scale lag (X → Reddit/YouTube/newsletter)  
- Collect observations into sessions  
- One intentional follow-up if it is *contribution*, not anxiety  

**Don’t:**

- Spam thread to force engagement  
- Buy hollow_reach  
- Over-explain every critique into a second post instantly  
- Mistake silence for failure before the field’s tick has run  

Non-interference is not abandonment. It is **¬F** after a real plant: don’t force the outcome.

## Attention trees (branch layer)

Within one thread, the main post is the **trunk** (most visibility). Comments are **branches** (usually residual drips). Rare **secondary attractors** are comments that pull their own field — sometimes rivaling the trunk. Early plants on growing trunks raise odds of a meaningful branch; most still plateau after a front-loaded drip. That is field structure, not personal failure — release ownership (¬F). Full map: [Attention Trees and Secondary Attractors](attention-trees-and-secondary-attractors.md).

## Curvature (intervention layer)

A plant is a **localized intervention** in a curvable medium. Goal is not only visibility but **meaningful local bend** — clean redirect vs noise attach vs forced turbulence. Soft medium (early, high-momentum) bends more easily. After you introduce curvature, non-interference lets the field follow its own dynamics. See [Attention as a Curvable Medium](attention-as-curvable-medium.md).

## Coherence check (gnosis-aware)

Beyond early + momentum, ask:

> Am I increasing coherence / serving real seeing, or feeding a sleep-loop (outrage, doom broadcast, status theater)?

Literacy without this check can become cold gaming. See [Field Literacy and Gnosis](field-literacy-and-gnosis.md), [Doom Broadcast and Self-Fulfilling Attention](doom-broadcast-and-self-fulfilling-attention.md).
