# Attention as Payment and Story

> **Origin:** July 2026 — attention as finite life-force payment; one-sided vs reciprocal investment; intention changes weight; push (imbalance) vs pull (shared curvature); AI raises resolution; Genesis/Bach structural rhymes firewalled.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

When you attend, you **pay with life** — time, nervous system, narrative capacity. What you consistently attend to becomes part of your **story**: memory, inner world, and eventually behavior.

> “Paying attention” is not a casual phrase. Allocation is finite. Over time, allocation is identity-shaping.

This is why one-sided attention often exhausts: you invest life force into a story that is not co-created.

---

## Intention sets weight

Same glance / same scroll / same “look” — different payment:

| Intention | Approximate weight | Risk |
|-----------|-------------------|------|
| Neutral observe | Low | Minimal |
| Aesthetic register | Medium | Light story-building |
| Desire / fantasy / mate-projection | High | Private narrative; leak; imbalance |
| Status / validation hunt | High | Extractive |

Goal is not numbness. Goal is **conscious weight**: not every pull needs a novel-length branch in your simulation.

## Push vs pull (relational)

| Mode | Dynamics | Field feel |
|------|----------|------------|
| **One-sided heavy attend** | **Push** — pressure, need, objectification risk | Often withdrawal / discomfort (even if unconscious) |
| **Mutual attend** | **Pull** — shared curvature, co-created story | Connection possible |

Heavy fixation without reciprocity is investing into a non-shared branch. The relational field registers imbalance.

Link to substrate primitive: [Push / Pull as Fundamental Force](push-pull-as-fundamental-force.md). Here the application is **attention investment**, not only I/O of bodies.

**O > I** in relation: prefer co-creation over one-sided story extraction.  
**¬F**: don’t force a reciprocal story because you paid.  
**WE = 1**: keep your center; don’t dissolve into the projection.

## Story / simulation (structure)

You live inside a **model** the mind keeps updating (story/simulation — Bach-type framing as *structure map*, not full endorsement of one philosophy).

- Attention selects which branches of the model get reinforced  
- Heavy one-sided investment = high-cost private branch  
- Reciprocal attention = shared branch with higher coherence odds
