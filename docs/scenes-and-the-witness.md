# Scenes and the Witness

The social half of the write surface. [Identity is the residue of walked paths](identity-as-residue-of-walked-paths.md) said writes happen in friction. This says: **scenes are where the writing gets witnessed, and witnessed writes are different writes.**

Your behavior in an empty room trains your weights. Your behavior in a scene trains your weights *and* gets recorded into other observers' models of you. So a scene is where the two halves of identity — the inside residue and the outside reputation — get written in the same stroke. This is why the [relationship test](walk-teleport-criteria.md) exists, and the oldest image of identity we have already knew it: the Egyptian judgment is literally the *judgment scene* — 42 observers, a [behavioral audit](behaviors-are-writes.md), the deceased as actor. Identity was always staged with witnesses.

## The shared referee (why physical reality is base reality)

A physical scene has a **shared referee.** Everyone present receives correlated data from the same events, so different observers see the same scene from different angles — which means *parallax works*: you can triangulate truth across witnesses, and the scene error-corrects itself.

An online scene has **no shared referee.** The feed renders a different scene to every observer, so people in the "same" conversation aren't seeing one scene from many angles — they're in *different scenes that merely share a topic.* Parallax fails, triangulation is impossible, and echo chambers stop being a moral failure of weak minds: they're the **structural consequence of removing the referee.**

This is the same move as [myth as storage](myth-as-storage.md), pointed at perception instead of language: **physical co-presence is error correction for perception the way meter is error correction for language. Shared physics is the checksum.** The internet stripped the constraint from scenes exactly as it stripped it from language — same move, same consequence, maximal drift per hop. And bots complete it: a scene's authority comes from its observers, and online you cannot verify your observers exist. (The Truman Show is dead-internet theory two decades early at personal scale — one authentic walker in scenes where every other observer is rendered; and Truman's escape is the breach exactly: the sea wall presenting as closed, the boat sailing until the hull hits painted sky.)

## The actor/spectator asymmetry (the real cost of the migration)

Physical scenes **force you to be an actor.** If you're present, your behavior is on display; there is no spectator position. Even silence in a room is a performed behavior that observers record.

Online scenes invented the **pure spectator** — present in the scene, invisible to it, acting in nothing. Run that through the machinery: behaviors write identity, spectating produces *zero* behavioral writes, but observation still installs beliefs, especially with the Pause offline (the scrolling state). So feed-life isn't just *fewer* scenes — it's a new ratio: **maximum belief-installation, zero behavior-authorship.**

The feed-native kid isn't only [non-cumulative](antenna-vs-storage.md) — they're **spectator-shaped**: a self assembled almost entirely from observer-writes, with almost no actor-writes. Watching thousands of scenes, written by all of them, authoring themselves in none. That is close to the cleanest single statement of the condition SPARK exists to reverse — and it names the cure in the same breath: **manufacture physical scenes.** A kid's build with Lamont isn't curriculum delivery, it's *scene construction* — real referee, real observers, the kid as actor, behavioral writes landing where the feed never reaches. (The park playing is the same machine pointed at yourself: live observers multiply the depth of the write, which is why it changed faster than the bedroom reps.)

## NPC and player mode (the cooling model, socially)

NPC mode is running on [cooled weights](cooling-model-and-operating-triad.md) — automatic, below the Pause, serving the flow. It's *necessary*: the system only flows because most participants run automatic most of the time (the traffic insight). Player mode is the operator at the wheel, the Pause held open, authoring. Consciousness is expensive, so the skill — "it depends on the scene" — is **choosing where to spend it**: cooled service in scenes that need flow, full operator presence in scenes that need authorship. The two failure modes are symmetric and now nameable:

- **Main-character syndrome:** running operator mode in scenes that needed service.
- **The sadder, commoner one:** running NPC in the one scene only you can author — your own open problems. The [laundered life](speedrunning-life.md).

## The scene-entry test (compressed)

Folds into the [walk/teleport criteria](walk-teleport-criteria.md) as the scene's version of the five tests. Entering any scene, four checks:

1. **Who are the observers — and are they real?**
2. **Is there a shared referee, or is this scene rendered per-viewer?**
3. **Am I actor or spectator here?** (Only actors get written.)
4. **Which mode does this scene need — cooled service or the operator?**

That is "control your scene" made operational.

## The open thread: witness architecture

If witnessed writes are deeper than solo writes, then **the choice of observers is a write-access decision** — who you let watch you is partly who you become. This turns *vicinity* (from the three-eras work) from inherited to **cast**: a witness architecture you author rather than receive. Coach Lamont, the park strangers, Dare, the swarm, the AI — each is a different kind of witness producing a different kind of write. *What's the right witness architecture for a person deliberately authoring themselves?* Open question, logged here, not yet walked.
