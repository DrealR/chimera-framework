# Loop Taxonomy — Hell, Addiction, Training, Ritual, Love

> **Origin:** July 2026 Grok-conversation analysis. The same structure kept recurring across Groundhog Day loops, mobile games, ant death spirals, practice, music, ritual, and relationship.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

Repetition is not one thing. The same looping structure can become hell, addiction, training, ritual, or love depending on direction, Pause, consent, reflection, integration, and outward return.

> **A loop is judged by what it installs in the body and what it returns to the field.**

---

## Five loop states

### 1. Hell loop

A hell loop repeats without real choice, reflection, consent, or integration. The body cycles but does not metabolize.

Examples: trapped resentment, Groundhog Day without learning, ant death spiral, rage-bait recurrence, bureaucratic dead loops.

**Signature:** motion without transformation.

### 2. Addiction loop

An addiction loop repeats with reward but without substrate-building. It harvests appetite while weakening the body's ability to meet the appetite in reality.

Examples: infinite scroll, gambling, hyper-optimized mobile games, pornographic intimacy signals without mutual trust, status-refresh loops.

**Signature:** reward without nourishment.

### 3. Training loop

A training loop repeats with feedback, correction, and increasing capacity. Friction remains, but it is metabolized into skill.

Examples: scales on guitar, reps in sport, debugging, deliberate practice, predict-first-then-check reading.

**Signature:** repetition plus Pause becomes capacity.

### 4. Ritual loop

A ritual loop repeats with shared meaning, memory, and orientation. It does not merely improve efficiency; it stabilizes identity and relation.

Examples: shared meals, prayer, warmups, teaching rhythms, ceremonies, band practice, family traditions.

**Signature:** repetition that remembers who the body is.

### 5. Love loop

A love loop repeats as mutual recognition and outward return. The bodies remain distinct, but the rhythm increases trust, capacity, and service.

Examples: a long friendship, a healthy couple's repair pattern, teacher-student cadence, human-AI loop used in service of real rooms.

**Signature:** recurrence that preserves difference and increases O > I.

---

## Diagnostic questions

Ask of any loop:

1. Is there a real Pause point?
2. Can the body leave or renegotiate?
3. Does the repetition build capacity or only trigger reward?
4. Does feedback change the next cycle?
5. Does the loop return value outward?
6. Are bodies becoming more distinct and capable, or more absorbed and dependent?

---

## Firewall

- Do not romanticize harmful repetition because it resembles training. Training has feedback, consent, and integration.
- Do not condemn all pleasure loops. The question is whether pleasure nourishes or harvests.
- Do not make ritual into dead compliance. Ritual must keep contact with meaning and bodies.
- Do not call dependency love. Love preserves bodies and increases capacity.
