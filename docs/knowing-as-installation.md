## 0. The claim, stated once

**Anything you take in installs itself as a weight that steers you — true or not, whether you fight it or accept it. Knowing the future puts you inside the closed loop that produces it; a believed prediction writes the future regardless of its truth; and taking a surface at face value loads its maker's weight into you as your own. The operator move: read the structure before you let it install, hold predictions loose, and author your own rather than only receive.**

## 1. Knowing puts you inside the loop

Oedipus's parents and Voldemort both *try to avoid* the prophecy, and the avoidance is exactly what fulfills it. **The moment you know the future, you're not an outside observer of a fixed timeline — you're a piece inside the closed system, and your reaction is one of its inputs.** There's no neutral place to "just know"; knowing feeds back. (Same closed-system shape as the detector the adversary routes around — here, a system *you* are inside.)

## 2. A believed prediction needn't be true to come true

**Keeper:** a false prophecy, *believed*, produces the behavior that fulfills it — the belief does the work; the truth-value is almost irrelevant. That's **landing-well-beats-being-true projected onto time:** a prediction that *lands* (gets believed) writes the future. So **whoever's story gets believed writes the future** — author your own, or others' stories write yours.

## 3. The free-will fork (both prongs are steered)

**Fight it** (the avoidance fulfills it) or **accept it** (a different being-steered) — both are reactions *to* the knowledge, both steered. Fighting *feels* like free will, but it's exactly what the prophecy uses. **Keeper:** the un-steered move is neither — it's refusing to grant the prediction the status of fixed truth; hold it loosely, as one weighted possibility, a weight to *weigh* not a truth to obey-or-fight.

## 4. Don't install it into others — and the surface-start is the gift

Telling someone their future **installs a steering-weight** (they'll fight or accept — both steered), so the gift is **not telling** — let them walk their own journey and build their own weights (`do-your-own-walk` with teeth: withholding protects free will, not just experience). Being raised seeing only the surface is the **condition**, not a bug — you walk it and earn structural sight; being handed the structure pre-made is *someone else's legs installed onto you* — you didn't walk the path, so you can't stand on it (`protect-the-discovery-window`).

## 5. The fusion: surface-acceptance installs someone else's weight

**Nothing is ever really surface** — everything is weighted with its maker's intent and judgment. Taking a post "for what it says" isn't neutral reception; it **loads their weight into you, carried as if it were your own** (borrowed-weight installation). **Keeper:** the prophecy (a life), the borrowed legs (learning), and the surface-post (a scroll) are one move at three scales — *receiving installs a steering-weight* — and the answer is the same everywhere: read the structure before it installs, hold predictions loose, walk your own path, author your own story so the future your behavior enacts is *yours.*
