# The Firewall

The framework's central discipline, and the thing that keeps "everything connects" from rotting into "everything is equally meaningful."

WE = 1 guarantees that any two things are connectable — the thread always exists ([we-equals-one formalization](we-equals-one-formalization.md)). That is precisely why naming a connection proves nothing. The firewall is the gate every cross-domain connection must pass to count.

## The gate

**A cross-domain connection earns its place only by producing an actual decision, number, move, or build step.** If the connection changes what you *do* — picks a chord, sizes a beam, sets a price, orders a lesson, writes a line of code — it carries load. If it only renames something you already saw, it's decoration, however elegant. Name the connection all you like in play; it does not become load-bearing until it pays in an action.

## The two failure modes to flag on sight

1. **The literal jump** — a structural (or merely verbal) rhyme promoted to a causal claim with no force law connecting the two. "Reaction and creation are anagrams, so creation is rearranged reaction." The resemblance is real; the mechanism is absent.
2. **Borrowed credibility** — a verifiable layer used as a certificate for an unverifiable one. A real credential or a real anomaly stacked behind an unverified claim, so the genuine thing in front launders authority onto the ungrounded thing behind.

Both are catalogued live in [the failure-mode specimen drawer](failure-mode-specimen-drawer.md).

## What the firewall does NOT do

It does not reject fiction, resonance, myth, or intuition. Those are **legitimate inputs** — often the richest ones. They are simply never **certificates.** Resonant-but-unearned material goes in a **drawer**: preserved, unsigned, not promoted, available for later. The firewall is a promotion gate, not a bouncer at the door.

And it can **overfire.** Rejecting a true claim because it arrived in language that pattern-matched a failure mode is itself a failure — an immune-system error ([specimen (c)](failure-mode-specimen-drawer.md)). The standing rule against overfire: **play first, sort at the moment of promotion to load-bearing, not at the door.** Read for the root claim under the costume before firing.

## Two corrections that live under the firewall

- ["No coincidences" correction](no-coincidences-correction.md): true of threads, unearned of weight; selection effects manufacture hits.
- [Experience vs ontology](experience-vs-ontology.md): an altered state can't certify its own realm; judge by the writes it leaves.

The firewall is also the world-facing half of [two-layer absorption](two-layer-absorption.md): the claim is graded here, by the world; the weighting is graded separately, by the walker's path. And its falsifiable, runnable form is [the deletion test](the-deletion-test.md) — strip the framework's vocabulary and check whether structural insight remains or only relabeling. For the case where question-similarity conceals answer-distance — two problems that look near as questions but are far as solutions — see [question-distance vs answer-distance](question-distance-vs-answer-distance.md): the measurement that tells you how much solution actually ports before you trust the pattern match.
