# The Anchor Must Be Yours

> **Origin:** Walked through four AI-engineering videos (agentic loops, expert-coding, the Matt Pocock
> interview, the DeepMind AGI paper), the Google-kills-the-web video, and the Monty money conversation
> (June 2026). The sharpest decision-rule of the run: AI can supply the tactical *answer* but never the
> strategic *direction* — so the anchor, the core, the problem itself must come from you. Several pieces
> were found in real contact, not metabolized from a video — which is the principle proving itself.
> Firewalls applied in writing.
>
> **Date:** June 2026

---

## AI ate the tactical; the strategic is what's left

AI ate **tactical** work — the actual writing, the closed problem, the checkable answer, the labor, the
knowledge — exactly the part the dead geniuses already solved and AI now makes trivial. What's left is
the **strategic:** what to build, which way it goes, the architecture, the why — the open problem.
That is the [Direction Axis](the-direction-axis.md) in engineering vocabulary: *tactical/strategic =
answer/direction.* "Your skills are the ceiling on what AI can do" is the **operator point** — the AI
is the instrument, you are the one playing, and a frozen structure played by a weak hand makes weak
music. The engineers and the labs independently rediscovered that the bottleneck was never the
answer-engine; it's the one steering it. *(Same water, another tap — load-bearing shape, not proof.)*

---

## The diagnostic: if you have to ask AI what problem to solve, that's the problem

Asking AI "what should I build, where do I fit, what has value" asks the answer-machine for the one
thing it structurally cannot give — **your anchor.** Problem-identification *is* the core: the act of
facing outward and noticing a real lack in a real person's life (the generative direction itself),
which can only come from a consciousness with a stake — relationships, skin in a real world it moves
through in real time. AI has no stake, no body, no field it serves, so it has no direction to lend. A
problem AI hands you is **artificial** in the load-bearing sense — not "fake," but *not from you, not
load-bearing for you:* a chord you didn't choose, that sounds fine but has no one at the root, so it
carries no value, because the value was always the human specificity — the thing only *you* would have
noticed, because of who you are and what you care about. The skill is *noticing:* open your eyes, real
problems are everywhere in the real people around you — opportunities you create by **seeing,** not
chase by **asking.**

---

## Two directions of building

- **Start human, add AI.** Begin with a real problem you care about for a real person (something only
  you would notice); bring AI in to shave off the parts a machine can do — but the anchor stays yours.
  Irreplaceable, because it's specific to you.
- **Start AI, add human.** A generic AI product solving some random problem, with "human flair"
  sprinkled on top (the 1%-touch move). Hollow, because the anchor is reproducible — anyone with the
  same prompt makes the same thing, and the human you added *last* is decoration, not foundation.

The first builds value (the foundation is the irreplaceable human core); the second builds slop (the
foundation is the thing everyone has). **Start with the core; the core must be yours.** Rule of thumb:
*wouldn't pay for it yourself? don't sell it.* And the specific **generalizes outward** — perfect it for
one real person and others like them find it — while the generic never specifies inward. (The phonics
guy: a real man in a restaurant who needed phonics — real lack, real stake — now out of a shelter
teaching others. Start-human, walked all the way through.)

## Loop the tactical, never the strategic

The agentic-loop era makes the inward-turn *executable,* and that is the sharp temptation for the
engineer. Build loops and automation for the **tactical** — the closed, the checkable, the
implementation. **Never loop the strategic** — the direction, the why, the vision — because that is the
part that is yours, and a loop running your direction while you sleep hands the
[write-head](ai-as-write-head-to-your-weights.md) to something with no stake. The loop-that-builds-loops
run unsupervised is frozen code replacing living vibration, aimed at your own work — which is exactly
why the engineers who live in it name AI-psychosis as the failure mode and put the whole craft into
*where the human checkpoint stays.* **AFK the implementation; never AFK the vision.**

## Money is artificial fuel; impact is the fuel that lasts

Money is artificial in the same sense an AI-given problem is — an abstraction, not real to you, and a
fuel that points the operator **inward** (chase it and it isolates you until you are alone). Impact is
real fuel — anchored to actual people, it points **outward** and connects, and the money flows toward
value as a byproduct (the grass grows). And the era makes this structural, not merely nicer: when the
cost of making collapses (AI democratizing labor and knowledge toward free, AI eating the capital),
money stops being the scarce thing, so anything organized purely around money-as-fuel loses its fuel.
The one fuel that doesn't run out is **human connection / impact** — precisely because AI cannot
manufacture it and it is getting scarcer; the slop-flood and the dying web make it *more* valuable, the
way the loneliness epidemic makes the safe room more precious.

*Firewall:* drawer "capitalism is ending / end times / the dying sun" — the apocalyptic costume. Keep
the load-bearing economics: when making collapses, artificial fuels lose their power, and the only
durable scarcity is the human part. Anchor to the real, because the artificial is being eaten.

---

## Play: the outward-turn when nothing forces it

When money stops organizing life and survival gets solved, the question left is not "how do I make
money" but "what do I do with a life" (why the culture turned to *Soul* and *Inside Out*). Freedom
without an anchor is the floating zombie — able to do anything and therefore doing nothing real, lost in
the [wall-less relational space](two-spaces-physical-and-relational.md). **The anchor is the cure for
the floating,** and it can only be real, specific, yours: a problem you care about for a person you can
see. That is *play* in the deepest sense — the operator facing outward **by choice when nothing forces
it** — the only freedom worth having and the only fuel that lasts.

## Firewall

- **Convergence ≠ validation.** The engineers (Pocock, the loop videos), the labs (DeepMind's
  staircase), the web-economics (Google zero-click), and this framework all land on "the
  operator/direction is the only durable value." Shared upstream root plus near-tautology — the shape
  is load-bearing, not proof.
- **Drawer the apocalyptic edge.** "Capitalism is ending," "the dying sun," "end times" — costume. Keep
  the economics: artificial fuels dry up when making collapses.
- **Strong-but-partial readings.** "Google is murdering the web on purpose" and "the loop will run your
  vision into the ground" are compelling skeletons over real facts — keep the documented numbers, hold
  the intent-framing loose (it is also just competition without intended aftermath).
