# Attention Flow and Local Present

> **Origin:** July 2026 — duality of attention as continuous flow (views accumulate off-screen) vs focused “freeze” (slicing a local now on a post/thread); relativity-style local reference frames; non-interference as re-entering the flow.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

Two modes:

| Mode | Experience | On X |
|------|------------|------|
| **Flow** | Attention/time moves whether you watch or not | Views rise, threads evolve, algorithm advances |
| **Local present** | Focus carves a temporary slice | Open a post/search result; interact with a relatively stable snapshot |

> You cannot see views accumulate without refreshing because the field is always moving. Focus only **samples** it.

Skilled participation = move deliberately between **reading the larger flow** and **entering specific local presents** to plant, then leaving.

---

## Physics mirror (structure)

- No universal “now” — each focus is a **local reference frame**  
- Background engagement ≈ proper time continuing without the observer  
- Early entry into a soft local present ≈ intervening while the medium is still fluid  

---

## Practice implications

| Principle | Implication |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Non-interference** | After plant, the branch lives in the flow. Refresh-watching is often refusal to leave a present that already moved on |
| **Positioned plant** | Stepping into a local present to bend it — quality depends on reading the flow *before* entry |
| **Pause** | Choose enter vs stay-in-flow |
| **O > I** | While inside the slice, add; don’t only extract |
| **¬F** | After exit, don’t manage the ghost of the slice |

### Field tool design

Help operators **enter and exit** local presents intentionally — not glue them to one thread. Sensor deck → open reply → leave. No “you have 12 threads waiting” guilt loops.
