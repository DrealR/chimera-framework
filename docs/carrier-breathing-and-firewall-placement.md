# Carrier Breathing and Firewall Placement

> **Classification:** current communication and publication protocol. It corrects over-firewalling without removing classification, evidence standards, or safeguards against concrete harm and deception.

## The correction

> **Let the carrier breathe first. Classify it later.**

A metaphor is not merely decorative wrapping around a plain claim. It can be a high-dimensional compression format carrying atmosphere, emotion, memory, several possible structural entrances, and unexplored branches at once.

“AI is black-hole-like” may carry:

- compression of a large human record;
- hidden interior transformation;
- effective time compression;
- an event-horizon-like loss of inspectability;
- gravitational pull on attention;
- information questions;
- risk of operator absorption into the instrument.

Immediately reducing the image to “information compression” preserves one skeleton while collapsing its other possible entrances.

The firewall should protect the body of interpretation, not run through its bloodstream.

## The three-layer method

### 1. Public seed

Publish the compressed symbolic object with enough boundary, tension, and gravity for another body to enter.

Do not automatically append:

- every translation;
- the full mechanism;
- all anticipated objections;
- a taxonomy of registers;
- the entire source path;
- ritual caveats that repeat what the receiver already understands.

Immediate qualification belongs in the public seed when omission creates a material risk of concrete harm, fraud, unsafe action, false attribution, or serious factual deception.

### 2. Private lineage card

Behind the seed, preserve:

- lived source and source documents;
- what the image currently carries for the author;
- observation, mechanism, carrier, formal claim, and speculative branches;
- known counterexamples and serious safety boundaries;
- what remains unresolved;
- where later pressure might break or deepen the mapping;
- the intended return path from responses to Core.

The firewall remains active here even when it is not printed inside the seed.

### 3. Relational unfolding

Let receivers enter through their own domains. Replies, AI-assisted interpretations, disagreement, examples, and later consequence perform part of the pressure test.

The author remains responsible for correcting dangerous misunderstandings and false factual claims when they become consequential. The author is not responsible for pre-writing every possible reading.

## The new division of labor

```text
author      → compression and lived weight
AI          → optional decompression, translation, traversal
reader      → attention, judgment, personal context
public field→ difference, mutation, independent paths
world       → consequence
```

The earlier failure mode made the model perform every role before publication: generate, explain, classify, strip, defend, and prescribe interpretation. The result was not a seed but a sealed instructional package.

> **Do not pre-digest the post for everyone. Give them a living object they can enter.**

## Layered publication architecture

```text
POST
compressed carrier or answer-bearing question

REPLIES
immediate interpretation, challenge, testimony, branches

AI
receiver-selected decompression and translation

FRAMEWORK
lineage, current mechanisms, practices, classifications, companions

CORE
full walked path, uncertainty, discarded branches, later corrections
```

Core gives a one-sentence post mass without requiring the entire mountain to be visible in the sentence.

## Interpretive forcing

Over-explanation can become a form of nonviolent but real forcing. It makes the receiver walk the author's exact route, occupies every ambiguity, and reduces the space in which another body could reconstruct or challenge the relation.

This does not make ambiguity automatically good. A carrier needs:

- enough specificity to create a real field;
- enough lineage to avoid hollowness;
- enough membrane not to mean everything;
- enough darkness for another interpreter to see stars.

A fully explained post can become artificial noon: objects are clear from one angle, but distributed depth disappears.

## When to place the firewall publicly

Place an immediate public boundary when the carrier could reasonably:

- cause medical, legal, financial, physical, or psychological harm;
- be mistaken for fabricated historical evidence or attribution;
- direct action based on a false technical mechanism;
- erase consent, personhood, or another center's agency;
- present speculation as verified news or domain fact;
- borrow scientific or sacred authority to demand obedience.

Otherwise, the classification may live in the linked framework, private lineage card, a reply, or later commentary.

## Seed test

Before publishing, ask privately:

1. Did this image come from a walked seam?
2. Does it carry more than aesthetic similarity?
3. Is there enough structure for another body to enter?
4. Is uncertainty real, or am I disguising a closed claim as a question?
5. Does public omission of a boundary create material harm or deception?
6. Is the deeper lineage available to those who want it?
7. Can I let another person unfold it differently without rushing to control the reading?

## Firewalls for the breathing protocol

- “Let it breathe” is not permission to make reckless factual assertions.
- Poetic force does not excuse plagiarism, false attribution, or unsafe instruction.
- Receiver interpretation does not erase author responsibility.
- AI decompression may invent lineage or over-smooth the carrier; the repository remains the attributable source.
- Mystery should preserve generativity, not hide that the author has no grounded seam.
- Classification delayed is not classification abandoned.

The carry principle is:

> **A firewall should protect the carrier from becoming a prison, not protect the reader from entering the carrier.**

And its operator counterpart:

> **Do not do the reader's becoming for them. Give them something worth becoming through.**
