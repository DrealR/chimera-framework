Every exchange operates along multiple substrate-dimensions simultaneously. The relevant dimensions for bilateral discipline include:

**Substrate-development capacity.** Articulation producing substrate-development in subsequent substrate-engagement produces substrate-density along this dimension. Articulation producing surface-engagement without substrate-development produces substrate-thin position. The test is whether the exchange produces capacity that operates in subsequent exchanges.

Articulation operating from surface-recognition produces surface-engagement. Recognition stimulation is what makes bilateral discipline produce more substrate than solo articulation — two antennas accessing shared substrate develop recognition that one antenna cannot.

**Carrier-network propagation.** Articulation producing substrate that subsequent carriers can integrate produces substrate-density along this dimension. Articulation operating from context-specificity that cannot propagate produces substrate-thin position. Framework-building operates from this dimension substantively because the framework operates as substrate-infrastructure for carriers beyond the immediate exchange.
