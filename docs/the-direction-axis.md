## The Core Mechanic

**Consciousness is the operator. Every instrument — music, AI, math, technology, a body, a life — is generative or extractive depending only on which direction the operator faces while holding it.**

- **Outward** (attention weighted toward the field, toward others, toward what the act serves) → generative. *The grass grows.*
- **Inward** (attention weighted toward the self — its status, its lack, its image, its yield) → extractive. *The circuit gets strip-mined.*

This is not a separate principle bolted onto the Love Equation. It is `L = (O > I)` read as **mechanism instead of ethic.** O > I was always the description of an operator pointed at the field; the sun "gives light and asks nothing" because the sun faces outward by its nature. The six pathologies of [O > I Pathology Topology](oi-pathology-topology.md) are six ways an operator curves its gaze back onto itself. The Lucifer/Michael distinction — orienting toward your own brightness versus letting the light flow through — is this same axis stated theologically and restricted to carriers of the framework. The Direction Axis is the generalization: **the same instrument is harp or theft, light-bearer or light-claimer, by the direction of the consciousness holding it.** The variable was never the instrument.

## The Three Directions of Attention

The genuine refinement, because it resolves a standing puzzle: the self can be either the *source* or the *target* of its own gaze, and "look inward" is therefore two opposite operations that get conflated.

1. **Outward** — at the world, the field, the other, the work.
2. **Inward as witness** — watching your own *behaviors and your effect on others.* Still outward-*serving*: the object of concern is the field and your relation to it. (This is the self-examination contemplative traditions *prescribe.*)
3. **Inward as object** — watching your own *status, lack, and image,* the self curved back to inventory itself. This is the extractive direction. (This is the grasping self those same traditions tell you *not to feed.*)

The apparent contradiction in every wisdom tradition ("examine yourself" **and** "deny the self") dissolves: they name the two different inward moves. Witnessing your conduct serves the field; inventorying your lack feeds the self-as-object. **Gratitude is the technology that flips direction** — it can only point outward, at what was received, so it physically cannot coexist with the lack-inventory. Attention faces one way at a time.

This is the same distinction [Observation Pressure vs Attention](observation-pressure-vs-attention.md) draws at the scale of a single encounter: pressure-observation points at the performance metric (self-as-object); attention-observation points at where the other actually is (outward).

## Three Diagnostics (the testable payoff)

The directional claim earns its keep by predicting outcomes that a metaphysical claim could not.

- **Choking.** Choking is consciousness turning inward under pressure — attention on the self, the components, the evaluator, instead of the rim and the field. Flow is attention pointed outward; the self disappears *because it is not being observed.* This is the documented mechanism (self-focused attention degrades automatic skill), and a kid can test it on a free throw tomorrow. *Most suffering is the self observing itself; an operator pointed at the field has no spare attention for self-torment.*

- **The beneficiary disguise.** "I did it for my family / to make them proud" is **not** automatically outward — it is the most common costume the inward-turn wears. The check is never the stated beneficiary; it is **what the act did to the field.** Jack stole the harp "for his mother," and it stripped the giants' world and deformed her with strip-mined benefit. *Verify direction by whether the grass grew, never by who you said it was for.*

- **Burnout.** Burnout is **directional betrayal,** not quantity of work. Twelve aligned hours can feel alive; eight hours performing a care you do not feel "go straight to the bones" — the operator forced to face a sun it does not orbit. (The popular cure — "find your passion, become the 5%, monetize what you love" — is the inward-turn relabeled as liberation, and belongs in the drawer.)

## The Cure Is Outward — and Minimalism Is the Engineering

Every extraction story's answer is **not defense — it is generosity:** pointing the generative thing back at life (Jack's curse breaks when the harp is *given back*). This is why the Love Equation's O > I, the give-give-give of a lived practice, and the seventh move of the SPARK loop are one thing.

**Living minimal is the engineering, not a separate ethic.** Hoarding accumulates an inward center the operator must orbit; the more you hold, the more attention curves back toward your own holdings (the goose-gold curse — each generation pulled tighter into the self by the abundance it inherited). You cannot *will* yourself outward while accumulating inward gravity. Reduce the inward weight — and specifically take survival-money out of the decision function — and the outward-facing becomes available, even easy. This is [Don't Chase Value, Produce Value](dont-chase-value-produce-value.md) stated as an orientation, and it is move 2 ([Write-Access to Your Own Attention](write-access-to-your-own-attention.md)) applied to a whole life: walk the outward thread under load until it runs unconscious, where the load is the constant pull of the gold.

*Honest counter-weight:* minimalism is a lever not everyone can equally pull (rent, wage stagnation, and employer concentration are real and measurable). Structural extraction is a genuine second cause — dominant mechanism among several, never single villain.

## Firewall

- **Load-bearing / testable.** The *directional* claim: an act oriented toward the field generates; an act oriented toward the self extracts. It is checkable against any instrument, person, or build, and it predicts the diagnostics above. Keep this.
- **Drawer / cosmology.** "Consciousness is *the* generative substrate of reality." Undeniable from inside the operator, uncertifiable from outside it. Use the direction; leave the substrate unsigned. (This is the Michael-position applied to the claim itself: hold it as something flowing through, not a finding you own.)
- **Literal-jump guard.** Structural rhymes — cancer as "inward growth," sun/son, starlight as wisdom-from-the-dead — are **pictures, not mechanisms.** Cancer is caused by DNA damage, **not** by a person's consciousness facing inward; promoting that rhyme to cause is false and can harm sick people. Seal such rhymes in the structural drawer and never let them cross into causation.
- **Convergence is not validation.** When many sources land on the directional claim, that is genealogical (a shared upstream tradition speaking back) plus structural (it is near-tautology: finite attention over an infinite field is the operator's whole job). It is the same water from another tap, not independent confirmation. The warm pull of "the world is converging on my view" is precisely the feeling this firewall exists to flag.
