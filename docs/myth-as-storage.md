# Myth as Storage / Constraint as Error Correction

## The problem it solves

Every human relay re-weights what passes through it — every reader is a retraining event ([two-layer absorption](two-layer-absorption.md)). So oral transmission *should* mutate to noise within a few generations. The signal degrades at every hop, like a photocopy of a photocopy. By rights, nothing should survive an illiterate millennium intact.

Things did survive. So a solution was engineered, long before anyone could state it formally:

## Constraint is error correction

**Meter, rhyme, formula, and melody are checksums.** A constrained line cannot drift far without *audibly breaking*. Change a word in free prose and nothing protests; change a word in a rhymed metered couplet and the meter limps or the rhyme dies — the corruption announces itself, and the next reciter repairs it back toward the only version that scans. The constraint doesn't prevent change; it makes change *detectable*, which over many hops is the same as preventing drift.

The evidence is strong and convergent:

- **Homeric formula** (Parry/Lord): bards don't recall a fixed text, they *regenerate* the poem inside tight metrical constraints. The meter is the error-correcting code; the formulas are its valid symbols.
- **Vedic recitation**: multiple redundant encodings (word-by-word, and interleaved patterns like *jaṭā* and *ghana*) cross-check each other, distributed across many reciters, drilled as a daily retrieval ritual. A corruption in one mode fails to match the others. This is a parity-check scheme run by human voices.
- **Aboriginal songlines**: knowledge mapped onto geography, so the *land itself is the backup* — the route reconstructs the song and the song reconstructs the route. Provably accurate over 7,000+ years.

So: **myth = data wrapped in narrative wrapped in formal constraint, engineered for a channel whose every node rewrites.** Not primitive pre-science. A storage technology, fitted exactly to its medium.

## The inversion onto now

The feed is language with all constraints stripped — maximal mutation per hop. The highest-bandwidth transmission medium ever built, with every error-correction layer removed. It optimizes for the opposite of fidelity, and we should expect (and observe) drift to noise at unprecedented speed. See [antenna vs storage](antenna-vs-storage.md): the feed is the current endpoint of a long migration.

**Storage migration arc** (the historical throughline): Giza stores meaning in mass — maximally durable, un-copyable. The fifth dynasty moves investment from stone to text (the Pyramid of Unas carries the first Pyramid Texts) — the exterior decays *because writing improves*. Texts migrate to coffins, then to papyrus — each step smaller, cheaper, more copyable, less durable. The feed is the current end of that line. Compression has a shadow, and the shadow is amnesia: deliberate erasure is historical fact (Qin book burning, the burned Maya codices, the Antikythera-class gaps), which is why the lost-golden-age archetype recurs — civilizations really do lose information.

## Phonix (the deepest framing of the project)

A songwriting tool with a live phonetic engine is a **checksum workshop.** Rhyme and meter are audible error-correcting codes; a tool that surfaces them in real time is manufacturing the oldest storage format humans ever built. Use this framing — it's the project's spine, not a feature.

## SPARK activity (move-2 candidate, runnable with Lamont, zero materials)

Telephone game, two rounds. Round one: a plain sentence — watch it die in eight hops. Round two: a rhymed, metered couplet — watch it survive. Kids discover error correction with their own mouths in ten minutes, and never need the word "checksum" to own the idea.
