# Relationship vs Reaction

*A framework study. The platform shift from accumulating followers to harvesting viral engagement is the conduct-vs-harvest split — and the eye-lock's mutuality decaying into one-way harvest — at the scale of the attention economy. The durable, compounding thing is the relationship; the spike disperses and leaves nothing. Graduated from a core capture. Internal register.*

## 0. The claim, stated once

**The play shifted from accumulating followers (a durable relationship — pooled attention you can return to) to harvesting viral engagement (a one-time spike that disperses), and the two have come apart. The follower is a thin mutual relationship (conduct-able); the viral reaction is pure one-way harvest. The platform now rewards the spike, so everyone chases reactions that don't compound — while the only durable, compounding thing is the relationship.**

---

## 1. The mechanism: reach decoupled from relationship

The old model rewarded the *relationship* (subscribers who see your stuff); the algorithmic-feed model rewards the *individual post's engagement regardless of author* — a viral post reaches everyone whether they follow you or not. **Keeper:** reach decoupled from relationship; the algorithm disintermediated the following. So chasing the spike isn't dumb — it correctly reads that *the relationship stopped mattering as much as the individual reaction* (the algorithm-as-fluid distributing by engagement-signal, not follower-graph).

## 2. The loss: mutuality decaying into harvest

**Keeper:** a follower is a *thin mutual relationship* (they chose you — a shallow shared body, `the-eye-lock-mutual-recognition`); a viral reaction is *pure one-way harvest* (a stranger who'll never see you again). So the subscriber→viral shift is the eye-lock's mutuality decaying into one-way harvest, at platform scale — and it maps to conduct-vs-harvest (`the-conductor-and-the-harvester-matched-pair`): the subscriber model *could* conduct (build a relationship, give over time); the viral-reaction model is pure harvest. Which is why the feed feels more extractive than the old subscriber era.

## 3. Why the spike doesn't compound

**Keeper:** a follower is **pooled attention you can return to** (durable, repeated); a viral reaction is a **one-time spike that disperses.** Reaction-optimized content (outrage, shock, the polarizing take) triggers a *response* but doesn't build the *relationship* that makes someone follow — the two require different content, so harvesting the spike actively *fails* to build the pool. The durable, compounding thing is the relationship, not the spike.

## 4. The throughline (and SPARK)

This is `seeing-is-believing-vs-believing-is-seeing` again: the **visible fast harvest** (the viral spike) vs the **invisible slow conduct** (the real relationship); the feed rewards the spike and buries the relationship. **Keeper:** the real, durable, compounding thing is the relationship — the eye-lock, the conducted connection, the follower who comes back, the kid you teach, the value-loop that closes. SPARK is the conduct play (real relationships with real kids); chasing virality is the harvest game, and it leaves nothing behind.
