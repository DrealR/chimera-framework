# Ancient Attention Pulls

> **Origin:** July 2026 — brainstem / survival attention allocation; sex, status, threat as fast curvatures; gendered *average* channel differences; Pause as gap between pull and response; firewalls against essentialism and strategy-as-objectification.
>
> **Date:** July 2026

---

## The claim

Much of the attention field runs on **ancient allocation systems**: threat, opportunity, status, mating signals, tribal belonging. Field literacy includes noticing these pulls **as pulls** — not only being carried by them.

> That noticing is part of the “alien” cost of awareness. Pause is the practical gap between brainstem flash and chosen participation.

---

## Fast magnets (not exhaustive)

| Pull | Typical field shape |
|------|---------------------|
| **Threat / outrage** | Rage-bait, moral signaling, pile-on — high V, weak/toxic branches |
| **Status / hierarchy** | Big-body gravity, prestige quiet, whale replies |
| **Mating / sexual dimorphism cues** | Strong, fast visual initial curvature (species-old; also heavily cultural) |
| **Tribe / belonging** | In-group frames, shibboleths, semantic territories |
| **Mission / competence / story** | Narrative-sustained attention (often slower than pure visual flash) |

Rage and tribal packages hijack old systems and hand them a simple story. See [Attention Capture Types](attention-capture-types.md), [Rage Bait and Attention Harvest](rage-bait-and-attention-harvest.md).

## Gendered averages (firewalled)

**Speak carefully:** population averages and cultural patterns — **not** universal laws, not destiny, not permission to stereotype individuals.

| Rough average pattern (often reported / observed) | Field read |
|---------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Strong visual sexual-dimorphism pull (many men toward women) | Fast initial curvature |
| Strong narrative / relational / emotional framing response (many women; also many men) | Sustains or redirects after first light |
| Status, mission, competence as strong male-directed channels | Parallel high-stakes currents |

**Both sexes** can pull and be pulled through visual *and* narrative channels. Culture amplifies, shapes, and commercializes these patterns. Individual variance is huge.

### Iceberg (surface vs depth)

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Surface (visual / flash)** | First automatic light |
| **Depth (story, trust, context, relation)** | Whether attention stays, deepens, or dies |

Performance that only manages surface appearance exhausts and cracks. Integrated participation lands cleaner (try vs do). See [Field Literacy Development](field-literacy-development.md).

## Practice

When a strong pull hits (attraction, outrage, status spike):

```text
1. Notice: this is a pull (observation)
2. Pause: gap before response
3. O > I: add real, or only extract / perform?
4. ¬F: not force an outcome from the pull
5. WE = 1: keep center; protect local life
```

On X: same as any high-charge soil — **read capture type**, plant only with real resonance, non-interfere. Do not treat people as engagement fuel.
