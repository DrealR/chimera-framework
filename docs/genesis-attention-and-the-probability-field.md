## Qubit, “smallest scale,” dark energy/matter (firewalled)

### Established (physics language, careful)

- **Qubit:** can be described in superposition; entanglement creates correlations that quantum mechanics predicts across separated measurements
- Measurement/decoherence couples systems to apparatus and environment → classical records; this does **not** require conscious human attention
- “Small scale” is where isolation from decoherence is easier — **not** a Marvel shrink-dimension you teleport to  

### Map to our model (analogy, not identity)

| QM-ish language | Operator model |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Superposition / probability | Waters — carrier image for modeled possibility, not an identity claim |
| Measurement record / outcome | A resolved state or record; not equivalent to attention making an event true |
| Entanglement (loose) | Shared present; WE=1 without fusion; secondary attractors linking trunk/branch |

**Humans as “quantum computer” of this dimension (carrier image):** consciousness can model futures, choose, story-build, and redirect systems. This is not a claim that human deliberation is literal quantum computation. AI/LLMs are classical computational systems with stochastic outputs; “probabilistic mirror” names the operator experience, not their physical substrate. Opacity can preserve uncertainty for the observer, but uncertainty about a box is not quantum superposition.

**Earth as sandbox/computer (carrier image):** ecosystems, societies, and technologies run processes; humans make branching and story consequential through embodied action. This is orientation language, not a physics claim.

### Speculation only (not established physics)

- **Dark energy/matter** as large-scale “unobserved potential” influencing structure without being “lit” in ordinary interaction — **fits Genesis darkness/waters poetically**; **not** a physics claim  
- Muonium and other probes = experimental windows into quantum layer — interesting, not load-bearing for practice  

**Firewall:** No “manifest virality with quantum thoughts.” No replace lab QM. Drop any parallel that confuses more than it clarifies. See also [Relational Coherence and Field Participation](relational-coherence-and-field-participation.md).
