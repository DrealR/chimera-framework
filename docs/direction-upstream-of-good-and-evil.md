## Good-vs-evil is the biggest costume

Good-vs-evil is the most common human narrative — the biggest costume, the one that spans every story.
Underneath it is the deeper root: **inward vs outward,** extraction vs generation — the
[Direction Axis](the-direction-axis.md). And you can tell direction is the *deeper* root precisely
**because it cuts clean exactly where good/evil stutters.** The Joker, the antihero, the sympathetic
villain, the brotherhood with a good cause doing bad actions — there, the surface good/evil label
breaks down, but the question *"which way is he facing, and what's the cost of that direction to
himself and the field?"* still works. Magneto has a good cause (protect mutants) and does extractive
actions (dominate, control), so good/evil stutters but direction doesn't. **A reading that holds
exactly where another reading fails is the one upstream.** Direction is upstream of good and evil.

---

## Why direction is *moral* (WE = 1 is the charge)

Direction is the mechanism, but it isn't morally neutral — and the reason is **WE = 1.** Extraction is
"bad" not by decree but because *if WE = 1, pulling inward against the field is damaging yourself.*
That's Magneto's tragedy: it isn't that he hurts mutants' enemies — it's that **the inward-pull
consumes him** (extraction destroys the extractor; the black hole; the rat-race toward the cold,
concentrated, dead point). Palpatine's ruined face is the visible cost of the same direction walked
far enough.

So the three layers stack:

```
Good / Evil   = the costume (the human-readable label)
Direction     = the mechanism (inward vs outward — holds where the label fails)
WE = 1        = why the mechanism has moral charge (inward against the field is self-harm)
```

## The two poles

- **Xavier — outward.** His whole power is reaching *into* other minds — empathy, understanding,
  connection-by-understanding. Generative.
- **Magneto — inward.** His power pulls things *toward* himself, bends the world to his will —
  connection-by-control. Extractive, even when aimed at others.

Magnetism itself is the **binding-and-separating** force (dipoles are how coherent bodies form): the
same relational fundamental, faced two opposite ways. Which is why Magneto is the perfect shadow of
Xavier — not a different power, the same one inverted.

## Causality is the thread

A person's **direction walked through time** is what makes them Yoda or Palpatine — the face is the
**cooled residue of the direction walked.** (And *root beats elaboration:* Yoda's power is the simplest
fundamental — push and pull, the bare Force — and is immense *through* simplicity, while the dark
side's lightning and choking are the ornate, downstream elaboration. The simple fundamental is upstream
of its derivatives. **Firewall:** keep this as a parallel illustration of "root beats elaboration" —
do *not* force it onto the music/math/code layering; that instinct to call the mapping "probably
random" was the discipline working.)
