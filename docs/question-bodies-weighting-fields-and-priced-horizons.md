# Question Bodies, Weighting Fields, and Priced Horizons

> **Classification:** working mechanism for questions, benchmarks, social fields, prediction markets, and operator-preserving navigation.
>
> This document does not claim that asking a question creates the underlying future, that a market price controls reality, or that attention, money, and model probabilities are the same mechanism.

## Core claim

> **A question can turn an unresolved region of possibility into an addressable body. Once addressable, that body can receive attention, scores, capital, institutional permission, and action.**

The question does not necessarily create the possibility it names. It creates a **membrane around uncertainty** that other bodies can now coordinate around.

```text
unbounded possibility field
→ question / naming cut
→ legible event or task body
→ weighting
→ selection
→ commitment
→ execution
→ consequence
→ history
```

Carry line:

> **The question may orient the field. It must not silently become the whole possibility-space.**

## Question Body

A **Question Body** is a bounded representation of an unknown with enough structure that other bodies can answer, score, price, investigate, simulate, or act around it.

A Question Body may contain:

- subject;
- time horizon;
- scale;
- outcome grammar;
- evidence rules;
- exclusions;
- resolution or settlement conditions;
- affected bodies;
- actors capable of changing the underlying event.

Example:

```text
raw unknown:
What will happen to X?

question body:
Will X occur before December 31 under definition Y,
resolved by source Z?
```

The second form is more useful because it is operational. It is also narrower. Operational clarity therefore creates both **measurement power** and **possibility loss**.

## Step Outside the Question

Before answering a consequential question, the Cube should be able to move one level outward.

Ask:

1. Why this question?
2. Who drew its membrane?
3. Why these outcomes?
4. Why this deadline?
5. What possibilities became invisible when the question was compressed?
6. Who benefits from making this question salient?
7. Can the measured or predicted body see the question and adapt to it?
8. Can any participant alter the event being measured?
9. What evidence could reopen or invalidate the framing?
10. Is there a better adjacent question?

> **Answering well is downstream of asking well.**

## Weighting fields

A **weighting field** is any local system that makes some possibilities, signals, outputs, or paths more consequential than others.

Different systems use different weighting mechanisms:

| System | Seed body | Candidate field | Weighting mechanism | Visible surface | Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LLM | prompt / context | possible continuations | learned token distribution + decoding | response | user / tools / world verification |
| Social post | post / question | replies, quotes, reactions | attention + ranking + network structure | surfaced thread | usually open-ended |
| Benchmark | task | model outputs | rubric / grader / score | leaderboard / profile | answer key, rubric, or external task success |
| Prediction market | event contract | bounded outcomes | capital, order book, market rules | price / implied probability | future event under resolution rules |
| Pocket | world seed | simulated branches | chosen laws, interventions, tests | branch constellation | return + evidence + reality test |

These are structural neighbors, not identical systems.

> **A weight is not a truth certificate.**

A like, price, score, probability, ranking, or reputation value tells us what a specific field selected under specific conditions.

## Measurement becomes governance

When the measured body can observe the metric and adapt to it, measurement becomes selection pressure.

```text
measurement
→ visible score
→ adaptation
→ changed behavior
→ changed measurement
↺
```

If enough of the body's future depends on the score, selection pressure can become governance.

Examples include:

- models optimized against benchmarks;
- creators adapting to engagement metrics;
- students adapting to grades;
- firms adapting to market prices;
- workers adapting to performance targets;
- public actors adapting to polling or prediction-market expectations.

The problem is not adaptation itself. The operator question is whether the body can still answer the metric rather than merely obey it.

> **The weight may influence the operator. It must not silently become the operator.**

## Prediction markets as delayed-answer benchmarks

A prediction market can be modeled as a benchmark whose answer key arrives later.

```text
question at t0
→ traders / forecasters assign weight
→ market coordinate changes through time
→ event occurs or fails to occur
→ resolution at t1
→ calibration and forecasting quality can be evaluated
```

This does **not** mean the market knows the future. A displayed probability is a coordinate of a particular market body: contract, participants, capital, liquidity, rules, date, and information environment.

The important complication is **reflexivity**.

### Reflexive Event

A Reflexive Event is one in which the predicted or priced body can receive the prediction as an input.

```text
prediction / price
→ subject or institution observes it
→ behavior changes
→ event conditions change
→ prediction / price changes
↺
```

Reflexivity varies. Rainfall may have little direct sensitivity to market price. Elections, company actions, creator behavior, negotiations, and personal events can be much more sensitive because relevant actors can see and react to the market.

Keep separate:

```text
price manipulation != event manipulation
```

but a causal path may exist:

```text
price
→ interpretation
→ incentives / resource allocation
→ action
→ event
```

## Capitalized Salience

> **Capitalized Salience occurs when attaching money to a possible event makes that event more visible, discussable, actionable, or strategically relevant.**

A previously obscure possibility can acquire a social body once it is named, priced, circulated, and reported.

```text
possible event
→ named contract
→ capital / volume
→ screenshots / media / discussion
→ wider attention
→ preparation, fear, strategy, or intervention
```

Money does not make the possibility true. It can make the possibility harder to ignore.

## Priced Horizon

The **Priced Horizon** is the subset of possible futures made financially legible by a market or other valuation system.

```text
possible future-space = very large
priced future-space   = selected named branches
```

A danger appears when the priced horizon is mistaken for the complete future.

The same failure exists in benchmarks:

```text
measured capability-space != total capability-space
```

and in social media:

```text
visible discourse != total social reality
```

This gives one reusable firewall:

> **Legibility is a projection, not completeness.**

## Event admission is upstream power

In any system where only some questions become scoreable, tradable, or visible, the admission layer matters.

```text
question designer
→ decides what becomes addressable

participant
→ acts within the admitted body

resolver / grader / reality
→ determines what counted as outcome
```

Therefore ask not only:

> Which option should I choose?

but:

> Who decided this was the option-space worth allocating attention, money, or optimization toward?

## Privacy as unpriced possibility

Privacy can be understood as more than secrecy.

> **Privacy can preserve regions of a body's future that have not yet been converted into data, probabilities, scores, contracts, or externally optimized branches.**

This does not imply that all visibility is harmful. Self-authored public records can preserve provenance and correct false narratives.

Useful distinction:

```text
selective witness != total transparency
```

The operator can publish enough to speak for itself without authorizing unlimited model-building around every part of its life.

## Question Compiler

A Cube Question Compiler should translate a raw concern into an operational body without pretending the result exhausts the unknown.

```text
RAW UNKNOWN
→ locate body
→ name desired movement
→ choose scale and time
→ expose assumptions
→ define evidence
→ enumerate omitted branches
→ test reflexivity
→ identify affected operators
→ define settlement / return
→ QUESTION BODY
```

The compiler should preserve both:

1. the **inside view** — answer the chosen question;
2. the **outside view** — keep visible that other questions and branches exist.

## Firewalls

- Asking about a possibility does not prove the possibility is likely.
- Naming an event does not create the underlying event from nothing.
- Market price is not destiny.
- Capital weight is not moral authority.
- Attention is not truth.
- A benchmark score is not a complete model identity.
- A binary contract can be useful without representing the full future.
- Reflexivity is a mechanism to investigate, not proof that every prediction causes its own fulfillment.
- Privacy does not require disappearance; selective witness can preserve both provenance and boundaries.
- Fictional systems that automatically list or trade personal futures are Story carriers unless independently evidenced in reality.

## Carry lines

> **A question creates an address, not the whole world.**

> **A weight makes a path salient, not rightful.**

> **Measurement becomes selection pressure when the measured body can adapt to the measurement.**

> **Selection pressure becomes governance when enough of the body's future depends on the score.**

> **The question may orient the field. It must not silently become the whole possibility-space.**
