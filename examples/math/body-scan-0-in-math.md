## STRUCTURAL WEAKNESS

Zero's primary structural weakness is its **inability to serve as a multiplicative identity**. This creates a **dimensional mismatch**:
- In additive systems, zero is the identity (O > I).
- In multiplicative systems, zero is a **sink** (I > O: a * 0 = 0 extracts the value of a).

This mismatch forces mathematical systems to **partition their operations** (e.g., rings have separate additive and multiplicative identities). The weakness is not a flaw but a **structural necessity**: zero cannot be both additive and multiplicative identity without collapsing arithmetic into triviality (e.g., if 0 = 1, then all numbers are equal).
