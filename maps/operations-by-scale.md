# Operations by Scale

CHIMERA accumulated several operation sets because they act on different bodies. They are nested, not competitors.

| Scale | Operations | Function |

## The common grammar

All of these can be read through one cycle:

```text
receive → locate body and direction → preserve a gap
        → transform or act → observe consequence
        → store residue → release or repeat
```

Do not invoke the largest protocol automatically. Match resolution to the body, risk, and learner. One useful question in the right room is often more coherent than fifty diagnostics.
