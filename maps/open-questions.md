# CHIMERA Open Questions

> **Status:** living registry. Open questions protect the framework from converting every powerful carrier into a settled answer.

| Seam | Current bounded position | What could improve it? |
|------|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| **What qualifies as a body?** | A scale-relative modeling unit with boundary, continuity, exchange, residue, and governance | Counterexamples where body language obscures more than it reveals; sharper body-class taxonomy |
| **How is scale selected?** | Name the smallest body relevant to the present decision, then run Day–Night context | Cases where smallest-body-first externalizes hidden cost to a larger body |
| **What does `WE = 1` assert?** | Relational commitment/common-origin carrier without erasure; stronger metaphysics remain source register | Philosophical comparison, phenomenology, social consequences, and explicit alternative axioms |
| **Can `O > I` be measured?** | Directional question about receiving capacity, not universal arithmetic | Domain-specific measures including delayed and externalized costs |
| **What counts as non-force?** | No domination or forced outcome; preserve calibrated friction and veto | Consent, education, parenting, governance, emergency, and power-asymmetry cases |
| **What is attention?** | Coupling and metabolic opportunity, not value or a conserved mystical substance | Cognitive research, interface experiments, relationship studies, and better measures of metabolism versus exposure |
| **Can Earthness be operationalized?** | Memory, differentiation, reciprocity, consequence, generation, membrane, and rest are candidate dimensions | Longitudinal community studies without ranking human worth or rewarding surveillance |
| **When does AI become environment or host?** | Locate question, weights, veto, cost, transformation, and post-AI capacity | Long-term studies of skill, dependence, organizational incentives, and agent autonomy |
| **What continuity preserves identity?** | Walked residue and uninterrupted carrier matter; information similarity is insufficient | Philosophy of mind, law, neuroscience, copying/fork thought experiments, and lived augmentation cases |
| **How should provenance coexist with privacy?** | Relational residue and layered trust; no single badge proves truth | Privacy-preserving attestation, community governance, and protection for vulnerable witnesses |
| **Can a question engine remain non-extractive?** | Finite human-selected deck, source seam, firewall, no automatic persona speech | Field trials measuring relationship, action, regret, coercion, and who benefits from intimate responses |
| **Can behavioral prompting become research rather than tunnel formation?** | Use explicit questions, resonance and resistance currents, stop conditions, direct sources, and embodied return | Controlled self-observation without treating feeds as neutral, complete, or valid measures of identity |
| **Who chooses the hill?** | Objectives supply gradients; capable systems can still optimize the wrong local body | Governance, plural evaluation, objective conflict across scales, corrigibility, and measurements of externalized cost |
| **How can path memory avoid self-echo capture?** | Preserve successful traces with provenance while remaining open to new terrain and independent correction | Longitudinal tests of diversity, synthetic-data lineage, exploration collapse, and recovery under changed environments |
| **How do Day and Night correct each other?** | Local agency plus relational conditioning | Cases where statistics misread interiors or self-description hides systemic consequence |
| **What makes a framework re-instantiation legitimate?** | Preserve relevant relations, lineage, operator, difference, uncertainty, practice, and return | Independent translations, hostile readings, forks, and applications after present platforms disappear |
| **What should remain canonical?** | A small stable Spine; most development lives in commentary and applications | Governance practice and succession experience beyond the founder |

## How to work an open question

1. Cite the seam rather than declaring it solved.
2. Gather source language and current bounded language.
3. Run Day and Night scans.
4. Seek a counterexample from another domain or body.
5. State which register each proposed answer occupies.
6. Put at least one implication through embodied practice.
7. preserve disagreement and results in Core.
8. Propose a Framework change only when the pressure changes the portable operator.

An open question is not framework weakness. It is a named membrane through which another era can enter without pretending the earlier body already knew everything.
