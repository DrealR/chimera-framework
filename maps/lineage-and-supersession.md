## Document status vocabulary

- **Current:** recommended public formulation.
- **Companion:** another register or audience route for the same structure.
- **Developmental:** records an earlier state of the model.
- **Carrier:** preserves a story or metaphysical picture.
- **Formal candidate:** invites domain validation; not yet established.
- **Superseded for mechanism:** retained for lineage but bounded by a later document.
- **Archive:** preserved source body, not the default entry point.
