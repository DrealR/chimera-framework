# Domain Atlas

Domains are translation and pressure-testing bodies. None is a universal validation authority for all the others.

| Domain body | Primary contribution | Best teaching use | Main caution |
|-------------|----------------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Body Theory** | Boundary, interior, medium, cycle, metabolism, residue, scale | Locate what is being analyzed | “Everything is a body” is a modeling move, not proof of identical mechanics |
| **Language** | Receiver, channel, distinction, weighting, translation | Reach different perceptual bodies | Words influence action; they do not magically replace causal work |
| **Mathematics** | Explicit definitions, invariants, compositional structure | Formal clarity and counterexample | Pattern and adjacency are not proof of metaphysical claims |
| **Stories** | Memory, perspective, moral tension, portable imagery | Carrier portals and teach-back | Resonance and recurrence are not validation |
| **AI / software** | Operator location, state, interfaces, failure modes, scaling | Make governance and host inversion inspectable | Fluency is not experience; simulation is not passage |
| **Chess** | Legal moves, tempo, position, sacrifice, shared rulebook | Compressed strategic language | Game boundaries are clearer than life boundaries |
| **Basketball** | Spacing, rhythm, role, team coordination, read-and-react | Embodied group dynamics | Competitive success is not identical to relational health |
| **Music / guitar** | Timing, friction, practice, listening, shared key, entrainment, field-shaping, distributed prediction, recovery | Identity-bearing walk, physical transduction, group scanning, minimum playable floors, and visible return through another body's movement | Musical harmony is a carrier, not a universal equation, psychometric instrument, or proof of identical experience |
| **Cooking** | Medium, transformation, pacing, nourishment, care | Everyday metabolism and O>I | Caretaking without boundaries can become extraction from self |
| **Relationships** | Reciprocity, otherness, consent, consequence | Test whether WE=1 preserves local centers | Never use the framework to play or diagnose another person |
| **Crypto / economics** | Incentives, transaction versus flow, governance | Inspect who benefits and bears risk | Treat financial claims as high-risk domain claims |
| **Biology / medicine** | Membranes, adaptation, metabolism, pathology | Rich body examples | Metaphor never substitutes for clinical evidence or care |

## Applied proof bodies

- **BeyondChess:** strongest example of one vocabulary bridging child-friendly, chess, computer science, and life language.
- **SPARK / Youth Builder:** observation → research → build → ship; children direct the instrument toward a real person's need.
- **Field:** attention sensing and reflective participation rather than engagement automation.
- **Guitar Cave:** machine reach returning to embodied practice and consequence.
- **Word Weight Lab:** testable edge for how wording changes interpretation and choice.
- **Honeydew / Grove:** economic and relational gateways where generosity, trust, and local accountability face reality.
- **Living Magnet / Living Question Engine:** searches for reciprocal, generative regions and time-portable questions rather than maximizing content consumption; AI reveals geometry, humans walk the relation.

An applied project earns framework weight by producing feedback, counterexamples, demonstrated capacity, or changed relationships—not merely by using CHIMERA vocabulary.
