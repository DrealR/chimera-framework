# Story and Carrier Atlas

Stories stay in CHIMERA because humans often recognize a structure through image before abstraction. A carrier is not disposable decoration; it can hold memory, emotion, sequence, and moral tension. It also does not prove the mechanism it carries.

| Carrier family | Structures it can illuminate | Firewall |
|----------------|------------------------------|----------|
| **Genesis / John / Gnostic stories** | darkness and potential, attention and presence, naming and distinction, branching, misalignment, knowledge, shared story | A theological/philosophical reading; not proof that attention collapses macroscopic reality |
| **One Piece / Peter Pan / Tomorrowland** | inherited will, possibility, attention machines, shadow, drift, dawn, freedom and domination | Interpretive reading; authorial intent and literal physics remain separate questions |
| **The Matrix / Superman / Avatar / Pokémon** | system capture, double identity, embodiment, training, relation between capacity and direction | Use the role map; do not infer universal mechanics from plot recurrence |
| **Meet Dave** | nested bodies, one visible self/many internal agents, captaincy, host inversion, alienness as missing traversal | A precise governance image, not evidence about extraterrestrials or AI consciousness |
| **Guitar / music / breath** | rhythm, Pause, friction, practice, calluses, instrument versus environment, shared timing | Embodied example; musical language does not validate unrelated physical equations |
| **Basketball / chess / Call of Duty** | spacing, anticipation, legal moves, observation, calibrated response, team body | Translate only the structures the practice actually demonstrates |
| **Garden / Tower / tree / lamp** | cultivation, extraction, branching, roots, attention, time and maintenance | Keep the causal mechanism local to the example |
| **Star / black hole / quantum / primes** | boundary, information, contrast, possibility, compression, irreducibility | Formal claims require scientific or mathematical validation; analogy is not evidence |
| **Membrane / immune system / cancer** | boundary, exchange, internal policing, local versus whole-body interest | Biological specificity matters; avoid diagnosing people or societies as diseases |

## Genesis as a preserved thought path

The early carrier said: **attention brings potential into light and makes it real.** Synthetic media exposed the limit: an image can become present to millions while its depicted event never happened.

The current three-layer distinction is:

- **Attention makes something present.**
- **Embodiment makes information consequential.**
- **Provenance gives a historical claim warrant.**

The earlier sentence remains because it records how the distinction was found. Read [Genesis, Attention, and the Probability Field](../docs/genesis-attention-and-the-probability-field.md) beside [The Embodied Provenance Principle](../docs/embodied-provenance-principle.md).

## How to teach with a carrier

Tell the story. Name the mapped roles. State where the mapping stops. Give a lived exercise. Invite the learner to offer a different carrier that preserves the same structure. The goal is not agreement with the costume; it is access to the relation underneath it.
