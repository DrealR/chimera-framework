## Genesis as a preserved thought path

The early carrier said: **attention brings potential into light and makes it real.** Synthetic media exposed the limit: an image can become present to millions while its depicted event never happened.

The current three-layer distinction is:

- **Attention makes something present.**
- **Embodiment makes information consequential.**
- **Provenance gives a historical claim warrant.**

The earlier sentence remains because it records how the distinction was found. Read [Genesis, Attention, and the Probability Field](../docs/genesis-attention-and-the-probability-field.md) beside [The Embodied Provenance Principle](../docs/embodied-provenance-principle.md).

## How to teach with a carrier

Tell the story. Name the mapped roles. State where the mapping stops. Give a lived exercise. Invite the learner to offer a different carrier that preserves the same structure. The goal is not agreement with the costume; it is access to the relation underneath it.
