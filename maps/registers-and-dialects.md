# Registers and Dialects

CHIMERA reaches across domains by changing language. To do that responsibly, separate **epistemic register** from **audience dialect**.

## Epistemic registers

| Register | What it contains | What it may claim |
|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Operator / mechanism** | Bounded causal claims, protocols, governance tests | What to inspect or try in a named body and scale |
| **Carrier / story** | Myth, scripture, fiction, sport, music, everyday image | A memorable mapping or question; not mechanism proof |
| **Formal / domain** | Math, physics, biology, engineering, history, social science | Only what the host discipline's definitions and evidence support |
| **Source / cosmology** | Metaphysics, high-reach synthesis, developmental intuition | The author's thought path, possibilities, and worldview—not settled fact |

One paragraph can contain multiple registers, but the seam must be visible.

## Audience dialects

- **Child / coach:** concrete action, one concept, immediate feedback.
- **Plain:** ordinary language, familiar situation, minimal vocabulary.
- **Artist / story:** image, rhythm, character, felt tension.
- **Builder / AI:** boundary, inputs, state, operator, failure mode, stop condition.
- **Teacher:** learning objective, sequence, exercise, teach-back.
- **Expert / skeptic:** operational definition, evidence class, alternatives, counterexample.
- **Framework-native:** compressed CHIMERA vocabulary and backlinks.

## Translation test

A translation is structurally faithful when these survive the wording change:

1. the bodies and boundaries;
2. the direction of flow;
3. the sequence of transformation;
4. the operator and veto;
5. the cost, risk, or residue;
6. the uncertainty and limits.

If only the emotional shape survives, call it a carrier. If a mechanism survives, state the causal path in plain language. If a technical claim appears, validate it inside that discipline.

## A practical sequence

1. **Ground:** learn the receiver's domain, channel, and current question.
2. **Name:** state the smallest checkable skeleton in plain language.
3. **Reflect:** offer two or three candidate mirrors.
4. **Translate:** choose the mirror with lived traction.
5. **Classify:** mark mechanism, practice, carrier, formal claim, or speculation.
6. **Delete:** remove CHIMERA words and test what remains.
7. **Restore:** return the story and dignity once the skeleton is clear.
8. **Practice:** let a real body receive feedback and consequence.
9. **Teach back:** have the learner translate it into their own language.
10. **Entangle:** preserve the artifact, source path, and later corrections.

> **Register changes wording. Classification controls what wording is allowed to claim.**
